Custom Inverted Wipe Transitions in Kdenlive

Creating customized wipe transitions in Kdenlive allows editors to add unique visual styling beyond the default presets. By designing custom grayscale matte images and applying them through Kdenlive's Wipe composition tools, you can define the exact geometric or organic pattern of a scene change. Utilizing the built-in inversion settings then allows you to flip the progression of the transition, causing the wipe to reveal or conceal clips in the reverse direction.

Step 1: Create a Custom Grayscale Matte

Wipe transitions in Kdenlive rely on luma mattes, which are grayscale images where the transition progresses from black (0% luminance) to white (100% luminance).

  1. Open an image editor like GIMP or Photoshop.
  2. Create a new document matching your project's resolution (e.g., 1920x1080).
  3. Design a pattern using pure black, pure white, and smooth gradient shades of gray. The black areas will transition first, followed by the mid-tones, ending with the white areas.
  4. Export the file in PNG or PGM format.

Step 2: Add the Custom Matte to Kdenlive

To make your custom matte available in the transition menu, save the file to your system's Kdenlive user folder:

  • Linux: ~/.local/share/kdenlive/lumas/HD/
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\kdenlive\lumas\HD\

Alternatively, you can select the file directly from within the project interface.

Step 3: Apply the Wipe Transition to the Timeline

  1. Place the outgoing video clip on track V1.
  2. Place the incoming video clip on track V2, overlapping the outgoing clip by the desired duration of the transition.
  3. Click the bottom-left corner of the upper clip on V2 to add a transition, or right-click the overlap and select Add Composition > Wipe.

Step 4: Configure the Custom Wipe and Invert Direction

  1. Select the Wipe composition block on the timeline to open the Properties panel.
  2. Under the Wipe Method or Luma File dropdown, locate and select your custom grayscale image. If you did not place it in the system directory, use the file browser icon to load the custom file directly.
  3. Locate the Invert checkbox in the properties window. Checking Invert flips the luminance order, making the white areas transition first and the black areas transition last.
  4. Adjust the Softness slider to control how sharp or blurred the edge between the two clips appears during the transition.
