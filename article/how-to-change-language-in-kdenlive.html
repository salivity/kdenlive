How to Change Language in Kdenlive

This guide provides a quick, step-by-step walkthrough on how to change the user interface language in the Kdenlive video editor. Whether you are using Windows, macOS, or Linux, you can easily switch the application's workspace to your preferred language using the built-in configuration settings.

Step-by-Step Guide to Changing Kdenlive's Language

  1. Open Kdenlive: Launch the Kdenlive application on your computer.
  2. Access the Settings Menu: In the top menu bar, click on Settings (on macOS, this may be under the application menu).
  3. Select Language Options: From the dropdown menu, click on Switch Application Language....
  4. Choose Your Language: A small configuration window will appear. Click on the Primary language dropdown menu and select your desired language from the list.
  5. Apply and Close: Click the OK button to save your selection.
  6. Restart Kdenlive: Close the Kdenlive application and reopen it. The user interface will now be displayed in your newly selected language.

Note: If your preferred language does not appear or apply correctly, ensure that you have the appropriate language packs installed on your operating system, particularly if you are using a Linux distribution.
