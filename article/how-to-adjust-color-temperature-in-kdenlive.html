How to Adjust Color Temperature in Kdenlive

Adjusting the color temperature of your video footage is an essential color grading technique used to correct unnatural lighting or evoke specific moods by shifting tones between warm and cool. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to locate, apply, and adjust the Color Temperature effect in Kdenlive to achieve balanced and professional visuals.

Step 1: Add Your Clip to the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and open your project.
  2. Drag your video clip from the Project Bin onto a video track in the Timeline.
  3. Click on the clip in the timeline to ensure it is selected (it will be highlighted).

Step 2: Locate the Color Temperature Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (if it is not visible, go to the top menu and select View > Effects).
  2. In the Effects search bar, type Color Temperature or navigate to the Color and Image Correction category.
  3. Locate the Color Temperature effect in the list.

Step 3: Apply the Effect to the Clip

  • Drag and drop the Color Temperature effect directly onto your selected clip in the timeline.
  • Alternatively, double-click the effect while your clip is selected to apply it immediately.

Step 4: Adjust the Temperature Settings

  1. Open the Effect/Composition Stack panel (found under View > Effect/Composition Stack if hidden).
  2. Locate the Color Temperature controls.
  3. Adjust the Temperature parameter to shift the tone:
    • Cool Tones (Blue): Lower the numerical value or move the slider to the left to correct overly warm lighting or create a cold, sterile, or nighttime atmosphere.
    • Warm Tones (Orange/Amber): Increase the numerical value or move the slider to the right to correct cool lighting or create a cozy, golden-hour, or energetic look.
  4. (Optional) Adjust any associated parameters, such as Neutral Color or Tint, if your version of Kdenlive includes them, to fine-tune the overall white balance.

Step 5: Preview and Keyframe (Optional)

  • Monitor the changes in real-time in the Project Monitor.
  • If the lighting in your footage changes over time (for example, moving from indoors to outdoors), click the clock/keyframe icon next to the Temperature slider to add keyframes and transition smoothly between different color temperatures throughout the clip.
