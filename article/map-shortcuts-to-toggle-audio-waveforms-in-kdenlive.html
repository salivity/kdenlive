Map Shortcuts to Toggle Audio Waveforms in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to map a custom keyboard shortcut to quickly toggle audio waveform displays (audio thumbnails) across your timeline tracks in Kdenlive. Configuring this shortcut allows you to instantly clean up your visual workspace or reduce timeline rendering lag during playback without manually navigating track header menus.

Step 1: Open the Shortcut Configuration Menu

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open your project.
  2. Click on Settings in the top menu bar.
  3. Select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... from the dropdown menu (or press the default shortcut Ctrl + Alt + ,).

Step 2: Locate the Audio Waveform Action

  1. In the search bar at the top of the Configure Shortcuts window, type Audio Thumbnails or Waveform.
  2. Locate the action named Show Audio Thumbnails (or Toggle Audio Thumbnails, depending on your Kdenlive version).

Step 3: Assign a Custom Keybinding

  1. Select the action from the list to highlight it.
  2. Choose the Custom radio button in the shortcut assignment section below the list.
  3. Click the input button (which may display None or a previous shortcut).
  4. Press the key combination you wish to use (for example, Alt + W or Shift + A).
  5. Ensure the chosen shortcut does not conflict with an existing essential command. If a conflict occurs, Kdenlive will prompt you to reassign or choose a different combination.

Step 4: Apply and Test the Shortcut

  1. Click OK to save your new configuration.
  2. Return to the timeline and press your assigned shortcut.
  3. The audio waveforms across all active timeline tracks will toggle between visible and hidden immediately.
