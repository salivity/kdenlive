Adjust Opacity Transitions in Kdenlive

This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough on how to adjust opacity transitions between overlapping video tracks using the Composite composition tool in Kdenlive. By placing clips on separate tracks and keyframing the opacity values within the Composite settings, you can achieve smooth crossfades, fade-ins, and fade-outs between multiple video layers.

1. Arrange Your Video Tracks

  1. Place the base video clip on a lower video track (e.g., Track V1).
  2. Place the secondary video clip on an upper video track (e.g., Track V2).
  3. Align the upper clip so that it overlaps the lower clip in the timeline where you want the transition to occur.

2. Apply the Composite Composition

  1. Hover your cursor near the bottom corner of the upper clip (Track V2) where the overlap begins until a circular indicator appears, then click to automatically add a composition. Alternatively, right-click the upper clip, select Add Composition, and choose Composite.
  2. Resize the Composite block in the timeline by dragging its edges so that its duration matches the exact length of the overlapping area.

3. Open Composition Settings

  1. Click directly on the yellow Composite block on your timeline to select it.
  2. Navigate to the Properties panel (usually located on the right or top dock of the workspace).

4. Keyframe the Opacity Transition

  1. In the Properties panel, locate the Opacity slider and the keyframe timeline.
  2. Set the start point: Move the playhead to the beginning of the transition block. Click the Add Keyframe button (plus icon) and set the Opacity slider to 0% (transparent) for a fade-in, or 100% (opaque) for a fade-out.
  3. Set the end point: Move the playhead to the end of the transition block. Add another keyframe and adjust the Opacity slider to the target value (e.g., 100% for a fade-in, or 0% for a fade-out).

5. Fine-Tune the Transition

  1. Adjust the position of the keyframes along the mini-timeline to control the speed and pacing of the blend.
  2. Change the interpolation mode between keyframes (e.g., Linear, Smooth) by right-clicking a keyframe marker if you prefer a non-linear acceleration during the fade.
  3. Play back the timeline in the Project Monitor to preview the result and adjust the timeline duration of the Composite block as needed.
