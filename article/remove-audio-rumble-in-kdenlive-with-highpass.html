Remove Audio Rumble in Kdenlive with Highpass

Low-frequency rumble caused by wind, handling noise, HVAC systems, or traffic can significantly degrade the audio quality of a video project. This guide provides a straightforward method for using Kdenlive’s built-in Highpass audio filter to isolate and eliminate these unwanted low-end frequencies, ensuring your dialogue remains crisp, clear, and balanced.

Step 1: Add the Audio to the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your media into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag and drop the video or audio clip containing the background rumble onto the timeline.

Step 2: Apply the Highpass Filter

  1. Navigate to the Effects panel (usually located in the bottom-left or tabbed with the Project Bin).
  2. Type Highpass or High Pass into the search bar, or browse to the Audio Correction category.
  3. Drag the Highpass effect directly onto the target audio clip on your timeline. Alternatively, drag it to the track header to apply the filter to every clip on that track.

Step 3: Configure the Cutoff Frequency

  1. Select the clip on the timeline and open the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Locate the Frequency parameter within the Highpass filter settings. The Highpass filter allows frequencies above this set value to pass through while cutting frequencies below it.
  3. Set the initial frequency between 80 Hz and 100 Hz. This range typically removes deep mechanical rumble without affecting the fundamental tones of human speech.
  4. If the rumble is still present, gradually increase the frequency toward 120 Hz to 150 Hz.

Step 4: Listen and Fine-Tune

  1. Play back the timeline with headphones to monitor the changes.
  2. Ensure that speech does not sound overly thin or tinny. If the voice loses too much body, lower the cutoff frequency slightly.
  3. Toggle the effect on and off using the eye icon in the Effect Stack to compare the filtered audio against the original track.
