Align Text Horizontally and Vertically in Kdenlive

Adjusting text positioning in Kdenlive ensures your titles, lower thirds, and subtitles look professional and well-balanced. This guide explains how to quickly align text layers horizontally and vertically using Kdenlive’s built-in Title Editor alignment buttons, coordinate controls, and the Transform effect.

Using the Title Editor Alignment Tools

The most direct way to position text is inside the Title Clip editor:

  1. Open the Title Clip: In the Project Bin, right-click and select Add Title Clip, or double-click an existing text clip to open the editor window.
  2. Select Your Text: Click the Selection Tool (arrow icon) and click on the text box you want to align. A bounding box will appear around the text.
  3. Locate the Alignment Tools: Look at the toolbar at the top of the preview canvas. You will find icons for both horizontal and vertical alignment:
    • Horizontal Alignment: Click Align Left, Align Center, or Align Right to snap the text box relative to the project frame.
    • Vertical Alignment: Click Align Top, Align Center, or Align Bottom to position the text vertically.
  4. Fine-Tune with Coordinates: To nudge the text precisely after aligning, adjust the X (horizontal) and Y (vertical) coordinate values located in the top toolbar.
  5. Save Changes: Click Create Clip (or Update Clip) to apply the alignment settings.

Adjusting Text Position on the Timeline Using the Transform Effect

If you need to animate the text position or align the entire title layer dynamically across the timeline:

  1. Add the Transform Effect: Select the title clip on your timeline, go to the Effects tab, search for Transform, and drag it onto the clip.
  2. Use the Effect Controls: In the Effect/Composition Stack panel, you will see options for positioning:
    • Use the Horizontal Alignment and Vertical Alignment icons within the Transform panel to snap the layer to the center or edges.
    • Adjust the X and Y sliders to manually offset the position by exact pixel values.
  3. Use the Monitor Overlay: Drag the text box directly inside the Project Monitor using the red on-screen positioning crosshair.
