Export Lossless FLAC Audio in Kdenlive

Exporting an audio-only mastering project in Kdenlive using the FLAC (Free Lossless Audio Codec) format allows you to preserve full audio fidelity without wasting storage on unnecessary video streams. This guide outlines the exact step-by-step process to configure project settings, select the correct render preset, and export a pristine, mastered FLAC audio file.

Step 1: Prepare Your Audio Project

Ensure your audio tracks are properly arranged, mixed, and mastered on the timeline. Double-check that all volume levels, equalizers, compressors, and limiter effects are enabled and applied to your desired tracks or the master track. Make sure any mute or solo buttons are set correctly so that unintended audio is not exported or omitted.

Step 2: Open the Render Menu

Open the rendering dialog using one of the following methods:

  • Click the Render button in the main top toolbar.
  • Navigate to the top menu bar and select Project > Render.
  • Use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Enter (Linux/Windows) or Cmd + Enter (macOS).

Step 3: Select the Audio-Only FLAC Preset

  1. In the Render window, locate the Presets panel on the left side.
  2. Expand the Audio only category from the list.
  3. Select the FLAC preset.

Step 4: Configure Render and Output Settings

  1. Output File: Click the folder icon next to the "Output file" field, choose your target save directory, and enter a name for your track, ensuring the extension is .flac.
  2. Render Range: Under the settings section, choose whether to render the Full Project or only a selected Zone if you defined in/out points on the timeline.
  3. Audio Quality / Bitrate: Kdenlive’s default FLAC preset applies lossless compression automatically. If custom adjustments are needed, click the Edit Profile button to modify the audio sample rate (e.g., 44100 Hz or 48000 Hz) and channel layout (stereo or mono) to match your master session.

Step 5: Render the File

Click the Render to File button at the bottom of the dialog. The Job Queue will display the export progress. Once finished, your lossless FLAC master file is ready for playback, archiving, or distribution.
