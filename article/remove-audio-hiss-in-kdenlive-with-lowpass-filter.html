Remove Audio Hiss in Kdenlive with Lowpass Filter

High-pitched hiss in your video's audio can ruin an otherwise great production, but Kdenlive provides built-in tools to resolve it effectively. This guide covers how to apply and configure the Lowpass audio filter in Kdenlive to cut off unwanted high-frequency background noise while keeping dialogue clear and natural.

Step 1: Select Your Audio Clip

Open your project in Kdenlive and place the affected clip onto the timeline. If the audio is part of a video clip, you can apply the effect directly to the video track, or right-click the clip and select Split Audio to separate the audio track for independent editing.

Step 2: Add the Lowpass Filter

  1. Go to the Effects panel (if not visible, enable it via View > Effects).
  2. Type Lowpass in the search bar.
  3. Drag and drop the Lowpass (or Low Pass Filter) effect onto the audio clip in the timeline. Alternatively, double-click the effect while the clip is selected.

Step 3: Configure the Cutoff Frequency

  1. Select the clip on the timeline and open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the filter settings.
  2. Locate the Cutoff frequency (Hz) parameter. The Lowpass filter works by allowing frequencies below this threshold to pass through while attenuating (cutting) frequencies above it.
  3. High-pitched hiss generally resides in the range of 5,000 Hz to 20,000 Hz. Human voice fundamentals and main harmonics usually stay below 4,000 Hz to 6,000 Hz.
  4. Set the Cutoff frequency to around 8000 Hz as a starting point.

Step 4: Preview and Fine-Tune

  1. Play back the audio in real-time.
  2. Gradually lower the Cutoff frequency (towards 4,000 Hz – 5,000 Hz) until the high-pitched hiss is removed.
  3. Ensure you do not lower the frequency too much; setting it below 3,000 Hz will cause voices to sound muffled, muddy, or like they are speaking underwater.
  4. Once the hiss is attenuated without compromising voice intelligibility, leave the settings in place and continue editing your project.
