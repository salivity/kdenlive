How to Map Custom Split Keys in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to map custom keyboard shortcuts in Kdenlive to split individual media clips directly on the timeline without needing to select them with your mouse first. By configuring these specific shortcut commands, you can instantly cut footage at the playhead position on active tracks, significantly speeding up your video editing workflow.

Step 1: Open the Shortcut Configuration Menu

Launch Kdenlive and navigate to the top menu bar. Click on Settings and select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... from the dropdown menu (or use the default shortcut Ctrl + Alt + ,).

Step 2: Search for the Cut Command

In the search bar at the top of the Keyboard Shortcuts window, type Cut Clip.

Kdenlive provides different cutting behaviors:

  • Cut Clip: Slices the clip located under the playhead on the currently active track without requiring the clip itself to be selected.
  • Cut All Clips: Slices every clip across all unlocked tracks at the current playhead position.

Step 3: Assign Your Custom Shortcut

  1. Select the Cut Clip action from the list.
  2. In the bottom section of the window, select the Custom radio button.
  3. Click the shortcut button (which will display "None" or the current key combination).
  4. Press the key or key combination you wish to use (such as C, S, or V).
  5. If the chosen key is already mapped to another function, a conflict warning will appear. Confirm the reassignment or choose a different key.

Step 4: Apply and Test on the Timeline

Click OK to save the new configuration.

To use the shortcut:

  1. Ensure the track containing your clip is designated as an active track (indicated by the highlighted track header on the left).
  2. Move the playhead over the section of the clip you want to divide.
  3. Press your assigned custom key. The individual clip on the active track will split immediately at the playhead without requiring a mouse click or clip selection.
