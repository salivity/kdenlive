How to Map Zoom to Fit Shortcut in Kdenlive

Navigating a long video editing timeline is significantly faster when you can quickly zoom out to view your entire sequence with a single keystroke. In Kdenlive, the feature responsible for displaying your full timeline within the visible window is called "Fit Zoom to Project." This guide walks you through the quick process of configuring a custom keyboard shortcut for this function to streamline your editing workflow.

Steps to Assign the Shortcut

  1. Open the Shortcut Settings: Launch Kdenlive and navigate to the top menu bar. Click on Settings, then select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... (or press Ctrl + Alt + K on most Linux and Windows systems).

  2. Search for the Action: In the search bar at the top of the shortcut configuration window, type Fit Zoom or Zoom to Project. Locate the action labeled Fit Zoom to Project in the results list.

  3. Select the Custom Shortcut Option: Click on the Fit Zoom to Project entry to highlight it. In the configuration area below, select the Custom radio button.

  4. Assign Your Shortcut: Click the button next to the Custom option (which typically displays "None" or the existing shortcut). When the button indicates it is waiting for input, press the key combination you wish to use (for example, Shift + Z or \ ).

  5. Resolve Conflicts (if any): If the key combination is already assigned to another command, Kdenlive will alert you. Choose whether to reassign it or pick an alternate combination.

  6. Save Changes: Click the OK button at the bottom right of the window to apply your new settings.

Testing the Shortcut

To verify the shortcut is working properly, zoom into a specific clip on your timeline using the timeline zoom slider or Ctrl + Mouse Wheel. Once zoomed in, press your newly mapped keyboard shortcut. The timeline view should immediately adjust so that all clips from the beginning to the end of the project are visible on the screen.
