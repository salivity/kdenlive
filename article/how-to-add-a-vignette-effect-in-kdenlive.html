How to Add a Vignette Effect in Kdenlive

Adding a vignette effect is a simple way to draw focus to the center of your frame and achieve a polished, cinematic widescreen look in your video projects. This guide covers how to locate the Vignette effect in Kdenlive, apply it to individual clips or master tracks, fine-tune its parameters for a soft and natural falloff, and ensure it integrates properly with cinematic aspect ratios and export settings.

1. Add the Vignette Effect to Your Footage

You can apply the vignette effect directly to an individual clip or to an entire track to affect the whole timeline.

  1. Select your target clip on the timeline, or select the track header if you want to apply the effect across all clips on that track.
  2. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  3. In the search bar at the top of the Effects panel, type Vignette.
  4. Double-click the Vignette effect or drag and drop it onto your selected clip or track.

2. Configure the Settings for a Subtle Look

To avoid an overpowering, amateur look, configure the parameters to create a soft, imperceptible darkening around the edges.

  1. Open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the controls for the Vignette effect.
  2. Radius: Increase the radius to push the vignette further toward the outer edges of the frame. A higher radius ensures the center subject remains unaffected.
  3. Smoothness / Softness: Increase the softness value to blend the dark edges gradually into the lit areas. Harsh transitions will look like a cutout rather than natural lens falloff.
  4. Opacity / Intensity: Lower the intensity or opacity so the corners are only subtly shaded. If the effect does not have an explicit opacity slider, adjust the Radius and Smoothness together to soften the overall contrast.
  5. Center X and Center Y: Keep these centered at 50% (or 0.5) unless you want to offset the focus toward an off-center subject.

3. Positioning the Vignette with Letterboxing (Optional)

If you are using top and bottom black bars (letterboxing) to create a cinematic widescreen look within a standard 16:9 canvas:

  • Apply the Vignette beneath the bars: Place your letterbox matte on a video track above your footage, and apply the Vignette effect directly to the footage track. This prevents the vignette from bleeding unnaturally over the solid black bars.
  • True 21:9 Projects: If your project profile is already set to a native ultrawide resolution (such as 2560x1080 or 3840x1600), simply apply the Vignette normally; it will calculate its boundary based on the active canvas dimensions.

4. Export the Project

  1. Click the Render button in the main toolbar or press Ctrl + Enter.
  2. Select your desired export preset (such as Generic > MP4-H264/AAC).
  3. Confirm that your resolution matches your intended cinematic output dimensions.
  4. Click Render to File to produce your final video.
