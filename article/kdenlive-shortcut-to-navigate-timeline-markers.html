Kdenlive Shortcut to Navigate Timeline Markers

Navigating sequence markers efficiently in Kdenlive can significantly speed up your editing workflow. By default, jumping between timeline markers may not have dedicated shortcuts mapped, but Kdenlive provides a fully customizable keyboard shortcut configuration tool that lets you assign custom keybindings to jump directly to the next or previous sequence marker.

Step 1: Open the Shortcut Configuration Menu

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open your project.
  2. In the top menu bar, click on Settings.
  3. Select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... from the dropdown menu (or press Ctrl + Alt + , on default configurations).

Step 2: Locate the Marker Navigation Commands

  1. In the search bar at the top of the Configure Shortcuts window, type Marker.
  2. Scroll through the filtered list of actions to find the sequence marker navigation options:
    • Go to Previous Marker
    • Go to Next Marker
    • (Optional) Go to Guide/Marker... commands if working with sequence guides.

Step 3: Assign Your Custom Hotkeys

  1. Click on the action you want to assign, such as Go to Next Marker.
  2. Select the Custom radio button located near the bottom of the window.
  3. Click the shortcut button (which may currently say None).
  4. Press the key or key combination you wish to use on your keyboard (for example, Alt + Right or Ctrl + Shift + ]).
  5. Repeat this process for Go to Previous Marker using a complementary keybinding (such as Alt + Left or Ctrl + Shift + [).

Step 4: Resolve Any Conflicts and Save

  • If your chosen key combination is already used by another command, Kdenlive will prompt you with a conflict warning. Choose whether to reassign the shortcut to the marker navigation command or pick a different combination.
  • Click OK at the bottom right of the window to apply and save your new shortcuts.

Your custom hotkeys are now active, allowing you to instantly jump the playhead between sequence markers across the Kdenlive timeline.
