How to Apply Unsharp Mask in Kdenlive

Applying an unsharp mask effect in Kdenlive allows video editors to enhance edge contrast and restore perceived clarity in soft or slightly out-of-focus footage. This guide provides a step-by-step method to locate the unsharp effect, apply it to a video clip on your timeline, and configure its parameters to achieve crisp visuals without introducing unwanted digital artifacts.

Step 1: Add Footage to the Timeline

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. Import your video clip into the Project Bin.
  3. Drag the video clip from the Project Bin onto an active video track in the Timeline.

Step 2: Locate the Unsharp Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel. If it is not visible, go to the top menu and select View > Effects.
  2. Click on the Main effects tab or navigate through the Blur and Sharpen category.
  3. In the search bar at the top of the Effects panel, type Unsharp to locate the Unsharp Mask (or Unsharp) filter.

Step 3: Apply the Effect to Your Clip

  1. Ensure your target clip is selected in the timeline.
  2. Double-click the Unsharp effect in the list, or drag and drop the effect directly onto the clip in the timeline.
  3. The effect will now appear in the Effect/Composition Stack panel for that specific clip.

Step 4: Adjust Effect Parameters

Open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to fine-tune the sharpening values:

  • Matrix Size / Radius (Luma & Chroma): Controls the width of the edges being sharpened. Smaller values target finer details, while larger values affect broader edges.
  • Amount / Scale: Determines the strength of the sharpening effect. Increase this value to make edges more pronounced, but keep it balanced to avoid creating bright "halos" around high-contrast areas.
  • Threshold: Sets the minimum brightness difference required before an edge is sharpened. Increasing the threshold helps prevent the filter from sharpening flat surfaces, background noise, or film grain.

Step 5: Preview and Render

  1. Place the playhead over the clip and preview the playback in the Clip Monitor or Project Monitor.
  2. Toggle the effect on and off using the eye icon in the Effect Stack to compare the original footage with the sharpened version.
  3. Once satisfied with the results, proceed with your project edit or render the final video.
