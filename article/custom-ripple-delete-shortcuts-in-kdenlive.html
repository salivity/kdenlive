Custom Ripple Delete Shortcuts in Kdenlive

This guide demonstrates how to configure custom keyboard shortcuts in Kdenlive to split and ripple delete clips simultaneously with a single keystroke. By setting up single-key pruning and extract commands—commonly referred to as top-and-tail editing—you can instantly cut unwanted footage at the playhead position and close the resulting timeline gap without performing manual multiple-step operations.

Step 1: Open the Keyboard Shortcut Configuration

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open any project.
  2. In the top application menu, navigate to Settings > Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... (or press the default shortcut Ctrl + Alt + ,).

Step 2: Locate the Prune and Ripple Actions

Kdenlive includes built-in commands that combine splitting and ripple deleting into one action based on the playhead position:

  • Prune to Left / Prune to In: Cuts the clip at the timeline playhead and automatically ripple deletes everything from the start of the clip up to the playhead.
  • Prune to Right / Prune to Out: Cuts the clip at the timeline playhead and automatically ripple deletes everything from the playhead to the end of the clip.
  • Extract: Removes the selected clip or region between the In/Out points and immediately closes the gap on the timeline.

To find these actions:

  1. Click into the search bar at the top of the Configure Keyboard Shortcuts window.
  2. Type Prune to display the directional split-and-ripple-delete commands, or type Extract to locate the clip/zone extraction command.

Step 3: Assign Custom Keybindings

  1. Select the action you want to customize (for example, Prune to Left).
  2. Select the Custom radio button underneath the shortcut details.
  3. Click the shortcut assignment button (it will display None or the existing key).
  4. Press the single key you wish to assign on your keyboard (editors commonly use Q for pruning left and W for pruning right).
  5. If the key is already assigned to another command, Kdenlive will prompt a conflict resolution dialog. Select Reassign to apply the key to your ripple operation.
  6. Repeat the process for the opposite direction (e.g., assigning W to Prune to Right) or for Extract.
  7. Click OK to save the changes and return to the timeline.

Step 4: Using the Shortcuts on the Timeline

  • Position the timeline playhead within any active clip.
  • Press your newly assigned key (Q or W). The clip will instantly be sliced at the playhead, the unwanted segment will be deleted, and all subsequent clips on the timeline will shift left to fill the empty space.
