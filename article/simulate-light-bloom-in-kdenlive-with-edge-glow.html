Simulate Light Bloom in Kdenlive with Edge Glow

Simulating a soft, cinematic light bloom effect in Kdenlive can be easily achieved using the built-in "Edge Glow" effect combined with track compositing. This guide provides a step-by-step workflow for isolating the brightest parts of your footage, applying the glow, and blending it over the original video to create a natural, diffused lighting aesthetic.

Step 1: Duplicate Your Footage onto Two Tracks

  1. Place your base video clip onto the timeline on track V1.
  2. Duplicate the same clip and place it directly above the original on track V2, ensuring both clips are perfectly aligned in time.

Step 2: Apply the Edge Glow Effect

  1. Select the top clip on track V2.
  2. Open the Effects tab.
  3. Search for Edge Glow (or Glow) in the effects library.
  4. Drag and drop the Edge Glow effect onto the clip on V2.

Step 3: Configure the Edge Glow Settings

In the Effect/Composition Stack panel for the top clip, adjust the following parameters to isolate and diffuse the highlights:

  • Threshold: Increase the threshold so the effect only captures the brightest areas of the image (light sources, reflections, or sky) rather than the entire frame.
  • Radius / Blur: Increase the radius to soften the edges of the glow, creating a gentle spread of light.
  • Intensity / Brightness: Adjust the strength to control how intensely the light bleeds into surrounding areas.

Step 4: Blend the Top Track with the Base Footage

To merge the glow naturally with the original clip on V1:

  1. Right-click the clip on V2 and select Insert an Composition > Composite and Transform (or use a Blend Mode composition).
  2. Set the Compositing / Blend Mode to Screen or Addition (Linear Dodge). This ensures that only the bright, glowing pixels are layered over the original video while dark areas remain transparent.

Step 5: Fine-Tune the Final Look

  • Adjust Opacity: Lower the opacity of the Composite and Transform track or the top clip if the bloom appears too harsh.
  • Optional Blur: If the glow edges are too defined, add a Gaussian Blur effect right after the Edge Glow effect on the V2 clip to smooth out the diffusion.
