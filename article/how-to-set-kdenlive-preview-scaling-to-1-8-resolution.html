How to Set Kdenlive Preview Scaling to 1/8 Resolution

Editing high-resolution 4K or 8K footage often causes timeline playback lag and heavy CPU or GPU usage. Configuring Kdenlive's preview resolution scaling to one-eighth (1/8) reduces playback load, improves scrubbing responsiveness, and speeds up the editing workflow without degrading your final exported video.

Step 1: Open Your Project

Launch Kdenlive and open the project containing your high-resolution video files.

Step 2: Locate the Project Monitor Controls

Find the Project Monitor panel where your video previews play. Directly below the video playback area, locate the monitor's toolbar.

Step 3: Access Preview Resolution

  1. Look for the Preview Resolution button (represented by a monitor icon or a fraction like 1:1 or 720p) on the bottom toolbar of the Project Monitor.
  2. Alternatively, navigate to the top menu bar and select Settings > Preview Resolution (or right-click inside the Project Monitor and select Preview Resolution).

Step 4: Select 1/8 Resolution Scaling

  1. Click the dropdown menu to view the available preview scaling options.
  2. Select 1:8 (or the lowest resolution option available, such as 270p/360p depending on source resolution).
  3. The preview window will immediately downscale the playback frames, freeing up system resources.

Important Considerations

  • Render Quality Unaffected: Changing the preview resolution only alters what is displayed in the Project Monitor during editing. Your final render will still export at full project resolution.
  • Combine with Proxy Clips: If you still experience stuttering after setting the preview to 1/8, go to Project > Project Settings > Proxy Clip and check Enable Proxy Clips for even smoother playback.
