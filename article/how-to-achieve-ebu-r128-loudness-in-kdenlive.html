How to Achieve EBU R128 Loudness in Kdenlive

Achieving EBU R128 compliance in Kdenlive requires measuring your project’s integrated loudness and adjusting the Master audio output to hit the standard target of -23 LUFS (±0.5 LU) with a maximum True Peak of -1 dBTP. This guide explains how to read Kdenlive's loudness analysis tools and properly adjust the Master track volume fader or apply audio effects to meet broadcast delivery specifications.

Understanding the EBU R128 Standard

The EBU R128 standard normalizes audio based on perceived loudness rather than peak levels. The primary parameters to monitor are:

  • Integrated Loudness: The target must be -23 LUFS (with an acceptable tolerance of ±0.5 LU for non-dynamic content).
  • Maximum True Peak Level: Audio must not exceed -1 dBTP to prevent distortion during digital conversion.
  • Loudness Range (LRA): Measures dynamic variation throughout the timeline.

Step 1: Open the Loudness Meter and Audio Mixer

  1. Go to the top menu and select View > Audio Mixer to display the Master and individual track faders.
  2. Select View > Loudness Meter (or Audio Scopes) to display real-time LUFS and True Peak measurements.

Step 2: Measure Integrated Loudness

To determine the required adjustment, measure the total integrated loudness across the entire sequence:

  1. Reset the Loudness Meter readings.
  2. Play through your entire timeline from start to finish without interruption.
  3. Check the final Integrated (I) value displayed in the meter.

Step 3: Calculate and Adjust the Master Track Volume Fader

Because the LUFS scale corresponds directly to decibels (1 LU = 1 dB), adjusting the Master fader requires basic arithmetic:

\[\text{Gain Adjustment (dB)} = -23\text{ LUFS} - \text{Measured Integrated LUFS}\]

  • If the project is too loud: If your measured Integrated loudness is -19 LUFS, subtract 4 dB from your Master track fader.
  • If the project is too quiet: If your measured Integrated loudness is -27 LUFS, add 4 dB to your Master track fader.

Click and drag the Master fader in the Audio Mixer, or double-click the decibel value to type the exact gain adjustment.

Step 4: Apply the EBU R128 Audio Effect (Automated Method)

Kdenlive also provides built-in filters to apply accurate normalization directly to the Master audio bus:

  1. In the Effects tab, search for Normalize (two pass) or Loudness EBU R128.
  2. Drag the effect onto the Master track (found at the left or top of the timeline track headers).
  3. Set the target loudness to -23 LUFS.
  4. Click Analyze to run the two-pass calculation. The filter will automatically adjust the gain to match the specification.

Step 5: Verify Peak Levels

After adjusting the Master fader or applying the filter, play the loudest sections of your timeline:

  • Check that the True Peak (Max TP) indicator in the Loudness Meter stays at or below -1 dBTP.
  • If the true peak exceeds -1 dBTP after increasing the gain, add a Limiter effect to the Master track with a ceiling of -1.0 dB to catch transient peaks without altering the integrated loudness.
