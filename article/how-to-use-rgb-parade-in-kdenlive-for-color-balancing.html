How to Use RGB Parade in Kdenlive for Color Balancing

This guide provides a direct walkthrough on setting up and utilizing the RGB Parade scope layout in Kdenlive for precise color grading and balancing. By monitoring isolated Red, Green, and Blue color channels side-by-side, you can accurately remove unwanted color casts, adjust tonal ranges, and ensure consistent exposure across your project.

Step 1: Open the RGB Parade Scope

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open your video project.
  2. Go to the top menu and select View > Layouts > Color (or switch workspaces using the layout switcher in the top-right toolbar).
  3. If the scope is not visible, go to View > Scopes and check RGB Parade.
  4. Dock the RGB Parade window adjacent to the Clip Monitor or Project Monitor for easy viewing while making adjustments.

Step 2: Add Color Correction Effects to Your Clip

  1. Select the clip on the timeline that requires color balancing.
  2. Open the Effects tab.
  3. Search for and apply either the Lift/Gamma/Gain effect or the Color Balance effect to the clip.

Step 3: Interpret the RGB Parade Display

The RGB Parade displays three separate waveforms representing the Red, Green, and Blue channels from left to right:

  • Vertical Scale (0–255 or 0–100%): The bottom represents pure black (shadows), and the top represents pure white (highlights).
  • Horizontal Alignment: Corresponds to the left-to-right axis of your video frame.
  • Neutral Balance: A neutral white, grey, or black object should show equal heights across all three color channels.

Step 4: Balance the Shadows (Lift / Offset)

  1. Identify a dark or black area in your footage.
  2. In your color correction effect, adjust the Lift (or Blacks/Offset) color wheel.
  3. Align the bottom baselines of the Red, Green, and Blue channels in the RGB Parade so they rest evenly at the 0 level without crushing into pure black.

Step 5: Balance the Highlights (Gain)

  1. Identify a bright or white area in your footage.
  2. Adjust the Gain (or Whites/Highlights) color wheel.
  3. Match the peaks of all three channels so they align horizontally at your target peak level (typically near the top limit without clipping past 255/100%).

Step 6: Adjust Midtones (Gamma)

  1. Look at the middle sections of the RGB Parade representing midtone areas like skin tones or neutral backgrounds.
  2. Adjust the Gamma (Midtones) wheel to eliminate any remaining color casts, ensuring the middle values of neutral elements remain level across all three channels.

Step 7: Verify and Fine-Tune

  1. Toggle the effect's visibility icon in the Effect/Composition Stack to compare the corrected image with the original.
  2. Check the Project Monitor to confirm that the visual result looks natural while the RGB Parade confirms technical accuracy.
