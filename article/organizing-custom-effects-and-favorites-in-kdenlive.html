Organizing Custom Effects and Favorites in Kdenlive

Organizing custom effect favorites into dedicated user categories in Kdenlive streamlines your video editing workflow by keeping your most-used tools accessible. This guide explains how to add standard effects to your favorites list, save tailored effect configurations with specific parameters, and group them into custom categories within the Effects panel.

Adding Effects to the Favorites Tab

  1. Open the Effects tab in Kdenlive. If it is not visible, enable it via the top menu by selecting View > Effects.
  2. Browse through the default categories (such as Video, Audio, or Transform) or use the search bar to locate the effect you want to favorite.
  3. Hover over the effect name and click the Star icon next to it, or right-click the effect and choose Add to Favorites.
  4. Click the Favorites tab (indicated by a star icon at the top of the Effects panel) to quickly access your curated list of frequently used tools.

Saving Custom Effect Stacks into Dedicated Categories

When you customize an effect's settings or combine multiple filters into a stack, you can save the entire setup as a reusable asset.

  1. Select a clip in your timeline and apply the desired effect or combination of effects.
  2. Adjust all parameters, keyframes, and settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel to your preference.
  3. Click the Save Effect icon (floppy disk icon) located in the header of the specific effect, or click the Save Effect Stack button at the top of the stack panel to save the entire group.
  4. In the dialog window that appears:
    • Name: Enter a clear, descriptive name for your custom preset.
    • Comment: (Optional) Add notes regarding when to use the effect.
    • Category / Group: Type a new category name (for example, Color Grading, Social Media Presets, or My Audio Fixes) or select an existing custom category from the dropdown menu.
  5. Click OK to save.

Managing and Accessing Your Custom Categories

  1. In the Effects panel, select the Custom category tab.
  2. Your newly defined categories will appear as collapsible folders containing your saved presets.
  3. To mark a custom preset as a favorite, click the Star icon next to it within the Custom tab; it will then appear directly in your primary Favorites list.
  4. To remove or update a custom preset, right-click its entry in the Custom tab to edit its properties or delete it from your library.
