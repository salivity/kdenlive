Copy and Paste Effects in Kdenlive

This article provides a quick, step-by-step guide on how to copy video or audio effects from one clip and apply them to another in Kdenlive. You will learn the exact keyboard shortcuts and menu options needed to streamline your video editing workflow by duplicating effect settings across multiple timeline clips.

Method 1: Using the Copy and Paste Effects Commands

The quickest way to duplicate effects across clips is by using the clipboard.

  1. Select the source clip: On your timeline, click on the clip that contains the effect(s) you want to copy.
  2. Copy the clip: Right-click the clip and select Copy, or press the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + C (Cmd + C on Mac).
  3. Select the target clip(s): Click on the destination clip where you want to apply the effects. To select multiple clips, hold down the Ctrl key while clicking them.
  4. Paste the effects: Right-click the selected destination clip(s) and choose Paste Effects from the context menu, or press the shortcut Ctrl + Alt + V.

All effects, along with their specific parameter settings and keyframes, will be immediately applied to the destination clip(s).

Method 2: Dragging from the Effect Stack

If you only want to copy one specific effect from a clip that has multiple effects applied, you can drag it directly.

  1. Select the source clip: Click the clip on the timeline to open its properties in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Drag the effect: Click and hold the icon next to the specific effect name inside the Effect Stack panel.
  3. Drop onto the target clip: Drag the effect over to the destination clip on your timeline and release the mouse button.
