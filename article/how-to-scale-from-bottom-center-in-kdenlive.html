How to Scale from Bottom Center in Kdenlive

Scaling a video clip or image from its bottom-center point in Kdenlive allows graphics, lower thirds, and animations to grow upward from a fixed base rather than expanding from the default center. By applying Kdenlive's built-in Transform effect and adjusting the origin coordinates, you can lock the clip's anchor point to the bottom-middle and animate the scale precisely.

Step 1: Add the Transform Effect

  1. Place your video clip or image onto the timeline.
  2. Open the Effects tab (or press Alt + 6).
  3. Search for Transform in the search bar.
  4. Drag and drop the Transform effect directly onto your timeline clip, or select the clip and double-click the effect.

Step 2: Open the Effect Options

  1. Select the clip in the timeline to open its Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Locate the Transform effect settings.

Step 3: Set the Anchor Point to Bottom-Center

To change the reference point around which the clip expands:

  1. Locate the Origin or Anchor Point parameters within the Transform effect.
  2. Set Origin X to 50% (or half the width of your source clip resolution). This centers the horizontal axis.
  3. Set Origin Y to 100% (or the total height of your source clip resolution). This sets the vertical anchor to the very bottom edge.

Note: Depending on your Kdenlive version, you may see an interactive 3x3 anchor grid in the effect parameters. If available, click the bottom-middle square to instantly assign the bottom-center coordinates.

Step 4: Position and Scale the Clip

  1. Adjust the X and Y position parameters to place the base of your graphic where you want it on the screen.
  2. Modify the Size or Scale X / Scale Y sliders. The clip will now shrink downward toward the bottom edge or grow upward from the base.
  3. To animate the scaling effect, enable Keyframes inside the Transform panel, set an initial keyframe with the scale at 0%, and create a second keyframe with the scale at 100%.
