How to Use Kdenlive Notes Panel for Project Feedback

Managing video editing projects requires careful tracking of revisions, ideas, and collaborator feedback. This article provides a straightforward guide on how to locate, enable, and utilize the built-in Notes plugin panel in Kdenlive. You will learn how to write structured logs, format your notes, and use interactive timeline timestamps to streamline your editing workflow and keep your projects organized.

How to Enable the Notes Panel

By default, the Notes panel may not be visible in your Kdenlive workspace. To enable it:

  1. Open Kdenlive and load your project.
  2. Go to the top menu and click on View.
  3. Look for Notes in the drop-down list and check the box next to it.

The Notes panel will appear in your user interface. You can drag and dock this tab anywhere that suits your workflow, such as next to the Project Bin or the Effects tab.

Keeping Track of Project Changes

The Notes panel acts as an internal text editor saved directly within your .kdenlive project file. This ensures your project history, creative decisions, and edit logs never get separated from your project file.

  • Version Control: Document what changes you made in each edit session (e.g., "V1: Rough cut completed," "V2: Applied color grading and adjusted background music levels").
  • To-Do Lists: Keep a running list of tasks you still need to complete, such as adding transitions, mixing audio, or inserting lower thirds.
  • Asset Tracking: Note down external asset links, font names, or specific color hex codes used in the project for quick reference.

Integrating Feedback with Timestamps

One of the most powerful features of the Kdenlive Notes panel is its automatic integration with the timeline through timestamps.

  • Create Clickable Links: Type a timecode in the standard format (such as 00:02:15:10 or simply 2:15) inside the Notes panel.
  • Navigate Instantly: Kdenlive automatically recognizes these timecodes as active hyperlinks. Clicking on a timestamp in your notes will instantly move the timeline playhead to that exact frame.

This feature is incredibly useful for implementing client feedback. For example, if a client requests an edit at a specific mark, you can write: "00:01:45:00 - Cut the awkward pause here." Clicking the timestamp jumps you right to the frame that needs editing.

Exporting and Collaborating

Because the notes are embedded inside the project file, any other editor who opens your Kdenlive project will instantly see your notes. If you need to share these notes with external stakeholders, you can easily highlight the text, copy it, and paste it into an email, document, or project management tool.
