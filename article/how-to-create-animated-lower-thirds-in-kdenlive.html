How to Create Animated Lower Thirds in Kdenlive

This guide demonstrates how to design and animate dynamic lower thirds in Kdenlive using built-in shape tools, text layers, and the Transform effect. By utilizing keyframes to manipulate scale, position, and opacity, you can build custom, professional motion graphics that smoothly enter, hold, and exit your video compositions without relying on third-party software.

Step 1: Create the Lower Third Elements

  1. Go to the Project Bin, click the Add Clip dropdown arrow, and select Add Title Clip.
  2. In the Title Clip editor, use the Rectangle tool to draw the background shape for your lower third. Customize the fill color, border, and opacity in the right-hand properties panel.
  3. Click the Text tool, type the name or message, and position it directly over the shape.
  4. If you want a two-tier lower third (e.g., Name and Subtitle), create a smaller secondary rectangle and a secondary text field with a contrasting font size or weight.
  5. Click Create Clip to add it to your Project Bin.
  6. Drag the Title Clip onto a video track above your main footage on the timeline.

Tip: For more complex animations where the text and shape move independently, create the shape and the text as two separate Title Clips on different timeline tracks.

Step 2: Apply the Transform Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab and search for Transform.
  2. Drag and drop the Transform effect directly onto your lower third clip on the timeline.
  3. Select the clip to display the effect controls in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.

Step 3: Animate the Entry with Keyframes

  1. Move the timeline playhead to the very first frame of the title clip.
  2. In the Transform panel, click the Add Keyframe button (if a keyframe is not automatically created).
  3. Set your initial off-screen or collapsed state:
    • Slide-in Animation: Adjust the X position to move the graphic entirely off the left or right edge of the screen.
    • Wipe/Expand Animation: Uncheck Uniform Scale, then change the Size X (width) or Size Y (height) to 0% so the shape starts collapsed.
  4. Move the playhead forward by roughly 15 to 30 frames (0.5 to 1 second).
  5. Click Add Keyframe and reset the position or scale values back to their default visible settings (e.g., Size X/Y to 100% or X/Y position to the intended onscreen placement).

Step 4: Smooth the Motion

  1. In the Transform effect timeline, right-click on the keyframes.
  2. Change the keyframe interpolation type from Linear to Smooth or Ease In / Ease Out.
  3. This removes robotic movement and gives the graphic a dynamic, polished acceleration curve.

Step 5: Add the Hold and Exit Animation

  1. Move the playhead to the point where the lower third should begin disappearing (typically 1 to 2 seconds before the clip ends).
  2. Click Add Keyframe to lock in the final static position during the hold phase.
  3. Move the playhead to the very last frame of the clip.
  4. Click Add Keyframe and change the position (sliding off-screen), scale (collapsing back to zero), or reduce the Opacity to 0% to complete the exit animation.

Step 6: Save as an Effect Template (Optional)

  1. Once the movement looks correct, click the Save Effect icon at the top of the Transform effect panel.
  2. Name the preset (e.g., "Lower Third Slide In/Out").
  3. You can now apply this pre-timed animation curve to any future text or shape clip instantly.
