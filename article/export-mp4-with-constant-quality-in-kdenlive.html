Export MP4 with Constant Quality in Kdenlive

Exporting an MP4 video using constant quality rate control in Kdenlive ensures optimal visual fidelity while keeping file sizes efficient. This guide explains how to access the Kdenlive rendering settings, select the appropriate MP4 profile, configure the Constant Rate Factor (CRF) slider for consistent video quality, and render the final project.

Step 1: Open the Render Dialog

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. Click the Render button in the main toolbar, or press the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Enter (or Cmd + Enter on macOS).

Step 2: Select the MP4 Preset

  1. In the Presets panel on the left side of the Render window, expand the Generic category.
  2. Select MP4-H264/AAC (or MP4-H265/HEVC if you prefer the H.265 codec for better compression).

Step 3: Configure Constant Quality (CRF)

  1. Locate the Quality section on the right side of the Render window.
  2. Ensure the video quality mode is set to use the CRF slider (Constant Rate Factor).
  3. Adjust the Quality slider to your preferred setting:
    • For H.264: A CRF value between 18 and 23 is recommended. Lower numbers yield higher visual quality and larger file sizes, while higher numbers increase compression and reduce quality. A value of 23 is the standard default for good quality.
    • For H.265: A CRF value between 24 and 28 provides visual equivalence to H.264 at a lower bitrate.
  4. Set the Audio quality to your preferred bitrate (e.g., 192 kbps or 320 kbps for high fidelity).

Step 4: Specify the Output Destination

  1. At the top of the dialog, click the folder icon next to Output file.
  2. Choose the destination directory on your computer, enter a filename with the .mp4 extension, and click Save.

Step 5: Render the Video

  1. Verify that the Full project option is selected under the Render details section (or choose Selected zone if only exporting a specific timeline section).
  2. Click the Render to File button at the bottom of the window to begin encoding.
