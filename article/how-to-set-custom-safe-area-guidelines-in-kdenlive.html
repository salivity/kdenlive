How to Set Custom Safe Area Guidelines in Kdenlive

This article provides a step-by-step guide on how to configure and display custom safe area guidelines and aspect ratio overlays on the Kdenlive Project Monitor. You will learn how to enable monitor overlays, adjust custom margin percentages for action and title safe zones, and configure aspect ratio guides to ensure your composition is properly framed for different display platforms.

Step 1: Enable Safe Zones on the Project Monitor

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. Locate the Project Monitor panel.
  3. Click the Switch Monitor Overlay button (the grid or guide icon located below the monitor display) or right-click directly inside the Project Monitor display area.
  4. Select Safe Zones from the context menu to make the overlay visible on your monitor screen.

Step 2: Configure Custom Safe Area Margins

  1. Go to the top menu bar and select Settings > Configure Kdenlive...
  2. In the configuration window, select the Monitors tab from the left sidebar.
  3. Switch to the Safe Areas section.
  4. Adjust the percentage values for your safe margins:
    • Action Safe Zone: Enter custom percentages for the outer boundary (standard broadcast is 90% or 93%).
    • Title Safe Zone: Enter custom percentages for the inner boundary to ensure text elements are not cut off (standard broadcast is 80%).
  5. Customize the line color or style if needed to contrast with your video footage.
  6. Click Apply and then OK to save your changes.

Step 3: Add Custom Aspect Ratio Guidelines

To frame your footage for specific aspect ratios (such as 9:16 for vertical video, 1:1 for social media, or 2.35:1 for cinematic widescreen) on a standard 16:9 canvas:

  1. Open Settings > Configure Kdenlive...
  2. Go to the Monitors tab and select the Guides / Grids section.
  3. Choose the guide layout style (such as Rule of Thirds, Grid, or Custom Aspect Ratio).
  4. Specify your custom framing dimensions or aspect overlay constraints.
  5. Click Apply and OK.

Toggling Guidelines During Playback

Once configured, your custom safe zones and aspect guidelines can be toggled on or off at any time directly from the Project Monitor control bar without affecting your final exported video render.
