How to Organize Kdenlive Effect Presets

Managing a growing library of custom transition and filter settings in Kdenlive can quickly become cluttered. This article provides a step-by-step guide on how to organize your custom effect presets into new, custom folders within the Kdenlive effects panel, helping you streamline your video editing workflow.

Step 1: Locate the Kdenlive Effects Directory

Kdenlive stores custom effects as XML files in a specific system folder depending on your operating system. To organize them, you must first navigate to this directory.

  • Windows: Press Win + R, type %appdata%, and press Enter. Navigate to kdenlive and then the effects folder (usually AppData\Roaming\kdenlive\effects).
  • Linux: Open your file manager and enable hidden files. Navigate to ~/.local/share/kdenlive/effects/.
  • macOS: Open Finder, press Cmd + Shift + G, and paste ~/Library/Application Support/kdenlive/effects/.

(Note: If the effects folder does not exist, you may need to save at least one custom effect in Kdenlive first to generate it automatically).

Step 2: Create New Folders

Within the effects directory, you can create new subfolders to act as custom categories in your Kdenlive Effects panel.

  1. Right-click inside the effects folder and select New Folder.
  2. Name the folder according to how you want it to appear in Kdenlive (e.g., "Color Grading," "Audio Enhancements," or "Custom Transitions").
  3. You can create as many subfolders as you need to categorize your presets.

Step 3: Move Your Custom Presets

Your saved custom effects are stored in the main effects folder as individual .xml files.

  1. Identify the .xml files corresponding to the custom effects you want to organize.
  2. Drag and drop (or cut and paste) these files into the newly created subfolders.

Step 4: Restart Kdenlive

For Kdenlive to register the new folder structure, you must restart the application.

  1. Save your current project and close Kdenlive.
  2. Reopen Kdenlive.
  3. Open the Effects tab. You will now see your custom folders listed as distinct categories containing your organized presets.
