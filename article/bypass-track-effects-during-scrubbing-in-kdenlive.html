Bypass Track Effects During Scrubbing in Kdenlive

High-speed timeline scrubbing in Kdenlive can become sluggish when tracks have heavy effects applied. By toggling global timeline effects, muting individual track effect stacks, and adjusting playback performance settings, you can completely bypass track-level processing to ensure fast, responsive timeline navigation.

Method 1: Toggle the Global Effects Bypass

The fastest way to bypass all track and clip effects during playback and scrubbing is using the global timeline switch:

  1. Locate the timeline toolbar located directly above or below your timeline tracks.
  2. Find the Disable Timeline Effects icon (represented by an effect wand with a slash or an eye icon).
  3. Click the icon to temporarily disable all track-level and clip-level effects.
  4. Scrub across the timeline at high speeds without the overhead of rendering filters.
  5. Click the icon again to re-enable effects once navigation is complete.

Method 2: Disable Individual Track Effect Stacks

If you want to keep clip-level effects active while only bypassing heavy track-level filters:

  1. Go to the track header on the left side of the timeline for the target video track.
  2. Click the Effects (FX) icon on the track header to open the track's effect stack.
  3. Uncheck or toggle the visibility/active state of the effects applied to the track.
  4. Alternatively, mute the track's processing by toggling the track effect button to inactive directly on the header.

Method 3: Optimize Playback and Dropped Frame Settings

To make high-speed scrubbing smooth without manually disabling effects every time:

  1. Open the top menu and select Settings > Configure Kdenlive.
  2. Navigate to the Playback tab.
  3. Check the option for Drop frames during playback to allow Kdenlive to skip effect processing on intermediate frames during fast scrubbing and high-speed forward/rewind.
  4. Set the Preview Resolution (located in the bottom-right of the Project Monitor or in settings) to 1/2, 1/4, or 1/8 to drastically reduce the rendering load of active track effects while scrubbing.
