How to Use Kdenlive Edge Detect for Outlines

This guide details the exact steps required to transform architectural video footage into clean, stylized outlines using the Edge Detect filter in Kdenlive. By isolating structural boundaries, adjusting algorithm parameters, and applying color modifications, you can easily produce blueprint-style visuals, technical wireframes, or animated pencil sketches directly on your timeline.

1. Import and Add Footage to the Timeline

Open Kdenlive and import your architectural footage into the Project Bin. Drag and drop the clip onto a video track (e.g., V1) in the timeline. Footage with high contrast and well-defined geometry works best for architectural line art.

2. Apply the Edge Detect Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Type Edge Detect or Sobel in the search bar.
  3. Drag the Edge Detect filter directly onto your architectural clip on the timeline, or double-click the effect while the clip is selected.

3. Configure the Filter Settings

With the clip selected, open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to fine-tune the parameters:

  • Algorithm / Function: Choose the edge detection algorithm that best defines the building's geometry (Sobel or Prewitt are standard choices for crisp structural lines).
  • Threshold / Sensitivity: Adjust the threshold slider to eliminate noise (such as subtle textures on walls or sky noise) while keeping strong lines (like window frames, rooflines, and pillars).
  • Invert: Enable the invert option if you want dark lines on a white background (resembling a sketch or schematic) instead of bright neon lines on a black background.

4. Enhance Contrast and Color

To make the architectural lines sharper and achieve a blueprint or technical drawing aesthetic:

  1. Add a Curves or Levels effect after the Edge Detect effect in the stack to crush midtone noise and boost edge contrast.
  2. To create a blueprint style, add the Colorize or Tint effect and set the primary color to an architectural cyan or deep blue.

5. Composite with Original Footage (Optional)

If you want to overlay the outlines over the original video:

  1. Place the original, unmodified video on track V1.
  2. Duplicate the clip directly above it on track V2.
  3. Apply the Edge Detect effect to the clip on V2.
  4. In the effect stack or via a composition transition, set the blend mode of V2 to Screen (for light outlines) or Multiply (for dark, inverted outlines) to reveal the original architectural textures beneath the structural lines.
