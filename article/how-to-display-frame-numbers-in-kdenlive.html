How to Display Frame Numbers in Kdenlive

Displaying real-time frame numbers across an entire Kdenlive project is essential for video analysis, client reviews, and precise editing workflows. This guide explains how to generate a continuous sequence-level frame counter by combining an adjustment layer with Kdenlive's Dynamic Text effect, allowing you to accurately track every frame rendered along your timeline.

Step 1: Create an Adjustment Layer

To ensure the frame count spans your entire sequence rather than resetting on individual clips, apply the effect to a dedicated top track:

  1. Right-click in the Project Bin and select Add Color Clip.
  2. Set the color to transparent or create an Adjustment Clip depending on your Kdenlive version.
  3. Drag the clip onto the topmost video track in your timeline.
  4. Stretch the clip across the entire length of the sequence you want to measure.

Step 2: Apply the Dynamic Text Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab in the sidebar.
  2. Search for Dynamic Text in the search bar.
  3. Drag and drop the Dynamic Text effect directly onto the adjustment clip on your top track.

Step 3: Configure the Frame Number Keyword

  1. Select the adjustment clip on the timeline to open its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. In the text input field, enter the dynamic frame keyword: 
    #frame
    (Note: Depending on your specific Kdenlive/MLT build, %frame% or %{frame} may also be used.)
  3. The preview monitor will instantly update to show the current sequence frame number.

Step 4: Adjust Formatting and Placement

  1. Font & Size: Adjust the font family, font size, and color to ensure readability against your footage.
  2. Background: Enable an outline or a solid background box behind the text to maintain visibility across both light and dark scenes.
  3. Alignment: Use the horizontal and vertical alignment controls or the coordinates fields (X/Y) to position the counter (e.g., lower-right corner).

Step 5: Verify Playback

Scrub through your timeline to verify that the frame counter updates incrementally in real time as the playhead moves from the start of the sequence to the end. When rendering the final export, ensure the adjustment track is visible so the frame numbers burn into the output video.
