Remove Tungsten Color Cast in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to neutralize heavy tungsten (orange and yellow) color casts in video footage using Kdenlive's built-in Color Balance filter. By adjusting shadows, midtones, and highlights with complementary blue and cyan tones, you can correct inaccurate indoor lighting and restore natural skin tones and white points.

1. Add the Footage to the Timeline

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and import your video clip.
  2. Drag the clip onto an active video track in the timeline.
  3. Click the clip to select it.

2. Add the Color Balance Effect

  1. Go to the Effects panel (usually located in the bottom-left or center panel; if not visible, enable it via View > Effects).
  2. Type Color Balance into the search bar.
  3. Drag the Color Balance (or Color Balance (RGB)) effect onto your timeline clip, or double-click the effect while the clip is selected.

3. Neutralize the Tungsten Cast

Tungsten lighting produces strong red and yellow tones (low Kelvin temperature). To neutralize this, you must introduce the complementary colors: cyan (reducing red) and blue (reducing yellow/green).

In the Effect/Composition Stack panel, adjust the following sliders:

  • Shadows (Lift):

    • Increase the Blue level slightly if dark areas appear muddy or brown.
    • Keep adjustments minimal here to avoid turning true blacks blue.

  • Midtones (Gamma):

    • Midtones control the bulk of your image, including skin tones and walls.
    • Reduce the Red value to remove deep orange tones.
    • Increase the Blue value to cool down the overall image.
    • Fine-tune the Green value slightly down if the footage leans green-yellow, or up if the image shifts toward magenta after adding blue.

  • Highlights (Gain):

    • Increase the Blue slider to make bright light sources and reflective surfaces appear neutral white rather than yellow.
    • Lower the Red slider to eliminate hot orange highlights.

4. Verify Using the RGB Parade Scope

To ensure accurate color balance rather than relying solely on your monitor:

  1. Open the scope monitor via View > Scopes > RGB Parade.
  2. Observe the Red, Green, and Blue channels. In tungsten-lit footage, the Red channel will be significantly higher than the Blue channel.
  3. Adjust the Color Balance sliders until the Red, Green, and Blue levels line up evenly across neutral parts of the frame (such as white walls, gray surfaces, or shirts).
