How to Apply a Rec.709 LUT in Kdenlive

Applying a Rec.709 Look-Up Table (LUT) in Kdenlive allows you to quickly transform flat, log-encoded video footage from cameras into a standardized color space with natural contrast and saturation. This guide walks you through the step-by-step process of importing your log footage, applying Kdenlive’s built-in LUT effect, loading a 3D Rec.709 conversion LUT, and fine-tuning your exposure for accurate color grading.

1. Prepare Your Log-to-Rec.709 LUT

Before opening Kdenlive, ensure you have the appropriate 3D LUT file (typically in .cube or .3dl format) designed specifically for your camera's color profile. Most camera manufacturers (such as Sony for S-Log, Canon for C-Log, and Panasonic for V-Log) offer official technical conversion LUTs on their support websites to map their specific log profiles directly to the Rec.709 color space.

2. Import and Add Footage to the Timeline

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open your project.
  2. In the Project Bin, click Add Clip or Folder and select your log video files.
  3. Drag the imported clip onto a video track in the timeline. Alternatively, you can apply the effect directly to the clip in the Project Bin if you want every instance in the timeline to inherit the conversion.

3. Add the "Apply LUT" Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Use the search bar to search for Apply LUT or navigate to the Color / Image Correction category.
  3. Drag and drop the Apply LUT effect directly onto the clip in the timeline (or double-click it with the clip selected).

4. Load the Rec.709 LUT File

  1. Select the clip in the timeline to open the Properties / Effect Stack panel.
  2. In the Apply LUT effect controls, click the folder icon next to the LUT file field.
  3. Browse your local storage to locate your .cube or .3dl Rec.709 LUT file and click Open.
  4. Set the Interpolation mode to Trilinear or Tetrahedral for smoother color transitions and minimal banding artifacts.

The clip in the preview monitor will immediately update from the flat log profile to a standardized Rec.709 image.

5. Adjust Pre-LUT Exposure and White Balance

Technical Rec.709 conversion LUTs assume that footage was exposed and balanced at base parameters during recording. If your shot is slightly over- or underexposed:

  1. Add color adjustment effects, such as Color Curves, Lift/Gamma/Gain, or White Balance.
  2. In the Effect Stack, drag these corrective effects above the Apply LUT effect.
  3. Adjust exposure and white balance before the signal enters the LUT to prevent clipping and preserve the full dynamic range of the original log data.
