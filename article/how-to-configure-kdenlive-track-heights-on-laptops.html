How to Configure Kdenlive Track Heights on Laptops

Managing limited vertical screen space on laptops is crucial for an efficient video editing workflow in Kdenlive. This guide explains how to minimize individual and global timeline track heights, remove non-essential visual elements like waveforms and thumbnails, and adjust interface settings to maximize usable vertical screen real estate on compact displays.

Manually Resize Individual Tracks

You can quickly adjust the height of individual video or audio tracks directly on the timeline:

  1. Hover your cursor over the bottom boundary of any track header on the left side of the timeline until the cursor changes to a vertical resize arrow.
  2. Click and drag upward to reduce the track height to its minimum allowed size.
  3. To toggle between a compact view and the previous height, double-click the separator line between track headers.

Set Global Track Height

Instead of resizing each track manually, set a uniform compact height across the entire timeline:

  1. Right-click on any empty space within a track header.
  2. Navigate to the Track Height submenu.
  3. Select Small to immediately reduce all tracks to their most compact preset.

Disable Audio Waveforms and Video Thumbnails

Thumbnails and waveforms require extra vertical padding to render properly. Disabling them allows tracks to shrink further:

  1. Right-click the track header of the target track.
  2. Uncheck Show Video Thumbnails for video tracks.
  3. Uncheck Show Audio Waveforms for audio tracks.
  4. Alternatively, disable them globally via Settings > Configure Kdenlive > Timeline to keep all newly created tracks minimal by default.

Set Default Track Heights in Preferences

To ensure new projects open with a compact timeline layout optimized for your laptop screen:

  1. Open the top menu and go to Settings > Configure Kdenlive.
  2. Select the Timeline tab from the left sidebar.
  3. Locate the Track height option and set the default pixel value to a smaller number (e.g., 30–40 pixels).
  4. Click Apply and OK.

Maximize Vertical Space with Layout Adjustments

Combine smaller track heights with interface tweaks to further increase timeline visibility:

  • Toggle Fullscreen: Press F11 to hide the operating system taskbar and window borders.
  • Collapse Timeline Tools: Right-click the timeline toolbar and choose to hide or shrink icon sizes.
  • Rearrange Dockable Panels: Drag preview and project bin panels to side-by-side arrangements rather than stacking them above the timeline.
