Audio Level Visualization in Kdenlive Clip Monitor

The Audio Level Visualization overlay in the Kdenlive Clip Monitor provides a real-time visual representation of audio amplitude directly on top of the source video preview. This feature allows video editors to evaluate sound levels, balance, and quality during the footage selection process before adding media to the timeline. By monitoring decibel ranges directly in the playback window, editors can quickly spot potential audio issues and streamline their editing workflow.

Primary Functions and Capabilities

The Audio Level Visualization overlay performs several critical tasks during the media preview stage:

  • Real-Time Loudness Monitoring: It displays an active decibel (dB) meter that fluctuates according to the audio playback of the source file. This allows you to verify that dialog, ambient noise, or music tracks sit at appropriate volume levels.
  • Clipping and Distortion Detection: The visual meter highlights peak levels. If an audio signal exceeds 0 dBFS (decibels relative to full scale), the meter indicates peaking, warning you of digital clipping or audio distortion present in the raw footage.
  • Channel Balance Assessment: The overlay displays individual meters for multi-channel audio (such as Left and Right stereo channels). This makes it easy to detect whether an audio track is unbalanced, mono-recorded on a single channel, or missing entirely on one side.
  • Efficient Clip Selection: By integrating audio meters into the preview screen, you do not need to insert a clip onto the timeline or open separate audio analysis panels just to check its volume. You can set In and Out points while simultaneously verifying both visual framing and sound integrity.

How to Enable the Overlay

To use the Audio Level Visualization in Kdenlive:

  1. Open a media clip in the Clip Monitor.
  2. Click the Options (gear or three dots) menu in the monitor toolbar or right-click the video display.
  3. Navigate to the Current Monitor Overlay settings.
  4. Select Audio Level Visualization (or Audio Levels) to toggle the meter on or off.
