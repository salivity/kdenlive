How to Use Extract Tool in Kdenlive

Editing videos often requires removing unwanted footage without leaving empty spaces in your timeline. This article provides a quick, step-by-step guide on how to use the "Extract" tool in Kdenlive to cut out a specific section of a clip and automatically close the resulting gap, saving you from manually shifting your media.

Step 1: Define the Timeline Zone

To remove a specific section, you must first define the "In" and "Out" points on the timeline ruler:

  1. Position the playhead at the start of the section you want to remove and press the I key on your keyboard to set the In point.
  2. Move the playhead to the end of the unwanted section and press the O key to set the Out point.
  3. You will see a highlighted zone appear on the timeline ruler representing the area to be deleted.

Step 2: Target the Correct Tracks

Ensure that the track containing your clip is active. If you want to extract the zone across all tracks, keep them unlocked. If you only want to affect a specific track, lock the other tracks by clicking the lock icon on their track headers to prevent them from being altered.

Step 3: Extract the Zone and Close the Gap

Once your zone is set, you can remove the footage and close the gap using one of the following methods:

  • Keyboard Shortcut: Press Shift + X on your keyboard.
  • Timeline Toolbar: Click the Extract button located in the timeline toolbar (represented by an icon showing a block being removed with arrows pointing inward).
  • Menu Bar: Go to Timeline in the top menu, navigate to Current Clip / Zone, and click Extract.

Upon performing this action, Kdenlive will delete the footage within the defined zone and instantly ripple-shift all remaining clips on the right to close the empty space.
