Generate Color Bars and Test Tone in Kdenlive

This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to generate standard calibration tools—specifically video color bars and an audio test tone—using the built-in Generators menu in Kdenlive. These assets are essential for testing video levels, aligning audio tracks, and ensuring your editing workspace is properly calibrated.

Step 1: Access the Generators Menu

To create these utility clips, you must use the Project Bin interface in Kdenlive:

  1. Open your Kdenlive project.
  2. Locate the Project Bin tab (usually in the top-left corner).
  3. Click the Add Clip or Folder dropdown arrow (the small arrow next to the plus icon).
  4. Hover your mouse over Generators to reveal the submenu.

Step 2: Generate Video Color Bars

Standard SMPTE color bars are used to calibrate video monitors and verify color accuracy.

  1. In the Generators submenu, click on Color Bars.
  2. A dialog box will appear. Here, you can customize the clip's properties:
    • Duration: Set how long you want the color bars to last on your timeline.
    • Name: Give the clip a recognizable name (e.g., "SMPTE Color Bars").
  3. Click OK.
  4. The generated color bars clip will now appear in your Project Bin.

Step 3: Generate an Audio Test Tone

A standard 1 kHz audio sine wave (test tone) is commonly used to calibrate audio levels and ensure there is no clipping.

  1. Click the Add Clip or Folder dropdown arrow in the Project Bin again.
  2. Navigate to Generators and select Audio Signal.
  3. In the settings window, configure your audio tone:
    • Frequency (Hz): Type 1000 (this represents the standard 1 kHz test tone).
    • Duration: Set this to match the duration of your color bars.
    • Gain/Volume: Keep this at a standard safe level (usually -12 dB or -20 dB depending on your broadcasting standards).
  4. Click OK.
  5. The audio signal clip will appear in your Project Bin.

Step 4: Add the Clips to Your Timeline

Once both clips are generated, you can place them into your editing timeline:

  1. Drag the Color Bars clip from the Project Bin onto a video track (e.g., V1).
  2. Drag the Audio Signal clip onto an audio track directly beneath the color bars (e.g., A1).
  3. Align the start and end points of both clips so they play simultaneously at the very beginning of your video project.
