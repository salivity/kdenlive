How to Auto-Split Clips by Scene in Kdenlive

Kdenlive features a built-in Scene Cut Detection tool that analyzes video files for visual transitions and automatically splits them into distinct sub-clips or markers. This guide covers how to access the scene detection feature, configure sensitivity settings for accurate detection, and populate your project bin or timeline with cut segments efficiently.

Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure that you have your video file imported into your Kdenlive project and that the Project Bin panel is visible.

Step 1: Access the Scene Detection Tool

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your source video into the Project Bin.
  2. Right-click on the imported video clip in the Project Bin.
  3. Hover over Clip Jobs in the context menu.
  4. Select Scene Detection (or Scene Split depending on your Kdenlive version).

Step 2: Configure Scene Detection Settings

A configuration window will appear with parameters to control how Kdenlive identifies cuts:

  • Threshold / Sensitivity: Determines how drastically the frame content must change to trigger a cut.
    • A lower threshold makes detection more sensitive, which is ideal for subtle cuts or low-contrast footage.
    • A higher threshold reduces false positives from rapid motion, panning, or lighting changes. A value between 15% and 25% is recommended as a baseline.
  • Minimum Scene Duration: Sets the minimum length (in frames or seconds) that a detected scene must have. Use this to prevent single-frame glitches or quick flashes from creating unwanted micro-clips.

Step 3: Choose Output Actions

Decide how you want Kdenlive to handle the detected scenes:

  • Cut markers: Places markers at each detected transition point without separating the physical clip in the bin.
  • Create sub-clips: Automatically generates individual sub-clips in your Project Bin for each detected scene, keeping your original clip intact.
  • Add to timeline / Cut on timeline: Automatically places the cut clips sequentially onto your timeline.

Step 4: Run the Detection

  1. Click Start or Process to begin the automated analysis.
  2. Monitor the progress bar. Processing time depends on the length of the video and your system's hardware.
  3. Once complete, inspect the generated sub-clips or markers in your Project Bin and drag the separated scenes directly onto your editing timeline.
