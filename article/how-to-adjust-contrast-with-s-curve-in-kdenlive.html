How to Adjust Contrast with S-Curve in Kdenlive

Adjusting contrast using an S-curve is one of the most effective ways to add depth, richness, and dynamic range to your footage in Kdenlive. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on applying the Curves effect, manipulating the tone graph to build an S-curve, and fine-tuning your highlights and shadows for a balanced, cinematic grade.

Step 1: Add the Curves Effect to Your Clip

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and select the video clip on your timeline that requires contrast adjustment.
  2. Go to the Effects tab (or select Menu > View > Effects if it is not visible).
  3. Search for Curves or RGB Curves under the Color and Image Correction category.
  4. Drag and drop the effect directly onto your timeline clip or double-click to apply it.

Step 2: Open the Effect Parameters

  1. Switch to the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the settings for the applied Curves effect.
  2. You will see a square grid with a diagonal line running from the bottom-left corner (representing pure black/shadows) to the top-right corner (representing pure white/highlights).
  3. By default, the tool is set to adjust all color channels simultaneously (Luma/Master), which is ideal for contrast adjustments.

Step 3: Create the S-Curve

The classic S-curve enhances contrast by darkening the shadows and brightening the highlights while keeping the midtones relatively stable.

  1. Lower the Shadows: Click on the diagonal line in the lower-left quadrant (around the 25% mark) to create a control point. Drag this point slightly downward. This darkens the shadow areas of the image without crushing pure blacks.
  2. Raise the Highlights: Click on the diagonal line in the upper-right quadrant (around the 75% mark) to create a second control point. Drag this point slightly upward. This brightens the highlight areas without blowing out pure whites.
  3. Check the Curve Shape: The line should now resemble a smooth, gentle "S" shape.

Step 4: Fine-Tune and Balance

  • Midtone Control: If the overall exposure becomes too bright or dark, click the center of the curve (50% mark) to anchor or adjust the overall exposure of the midtones.
  • Avoid Clipping: Keep the curve adjustments subtle. Dragging points too far will result in clipped highlights or crushed, noisy shadows.
  • Use Scopes: Open the RGB Parade or Histogram via Menu > View > Scopes to ensure your video signals stay within standard broadcast-safe ranges (0–255 or 0–100 IRE).
