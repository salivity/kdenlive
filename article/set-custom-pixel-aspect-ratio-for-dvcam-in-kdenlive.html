Set Custom Pixel Aspect Ratio for DVCAM in Kdenlive

Legacy DVCAM footage recorded in standard-definition PAL or NTSC uses non-square pixels, which often display distorted or stretched in modern video editors designed for square pixels. In Kdenlive, you can correct these distortion issues by configuring the project profile and adjusting individual clip properties to match the exact Pixel Aspect Ratio (PAR) of your source media.

Understanding DVCAM Aspect Ratios

Standard DVCAM video uses standard-definition resolutions that rely on specific non-square pixel aspect ratios:

  • PAL Standard (4:3): 720×576 resolution with a PAR of 16:15 (1.067) or 12:11 (1.09).
  • PAL Widescreen (16:9 Anamorphic): 720×576 resolution with a PAR of 64:45 (1.422) or 16:11 (1.46).
  • NTSC Standard (4:3): 720×480 resolution with a PAR of 8:9 (0.889) or 10:11 (0.91).
  • NTSC Widescreen (16:9 Anamorphic): 720×480 resolution with a PAR of 32:27 (1.185) or 40:33 (1.21).

Step 1: Configure Project Profiles

To prevent stretching across the timeline, configure your project profile before importing media:

  1. Go to Project > Project Settings.
  2. Select the Settings tab.
  3. Choose a preset under DV/DVD that matches your footage standard (e.g., DV/DVD PAL (4:3) or DV/DVD Widescreen PAL (16:9)).
  4. If a custom aspect ratio is required:
    • Click the Manage Project Profiles button (icon next to the profile dropdown).
    • Click Add New Profile.
    • Enter the frame rate and frame size (e.g., 720 x 576).
    • In the Pixel Aspect Ratio field, enter the custom ratio (numerator and denominator).
    • Click Save and select your new profile for the project.

Step 2: Override Pixel Aspect Ratio at the Clip Level

If imported DVCAM footage defaults to square pixels (1:1) in the Project Bin, force the correct PAR directly on the clip:

  1. Right-click the DVCAM video file in the Project Bin.
  2. Select Clip Properties.
  3. Navigate to the Advanced tab.
  4. Locate the Aspect Ratio or Pixel Aspect Ratio setting.
  5. Check the option to override or force the aspect ratio.
  6. Select your target ratio from the dropdown list, or type your custom fraction (e.g., 16/11 for PAL 16:9 DVCAM).
  7. Click Apply or OK.

Step 3: Render and Export Options

When exporting your finished edit, choose between maintaining native non-square pixels or converting to square pixels for web playback:

  • For Web/Modern Displays (Square Pixels): Render using a standard square pixel profile by scaling the canvas (e.g., upscale PAL 4:3 to 768×576 or PAL 16:9 to 1024×576 / 1920×1080).
  • For Archival/DVD (Native Non-Square): Select a render profile under the DVD or Generic (MPEG-2/DV) category that retains the native DVCAM resolution and flag parameters.
