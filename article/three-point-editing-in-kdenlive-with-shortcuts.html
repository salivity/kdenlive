Three-Point Editing in Kdenlive with Shortcuts

Three-point editing is a fundamental, highly efficient video editing technique used to precisely insert footage from your source monitor onto your timeline. This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough on how to perform three-point editing in Kdenlive using the Clip Monitor (source monitor) and keyboard shortcuts to significantly speed up your post-production workflow.

What is Three-Point Editing?

Three-point editing relies on defining three distinct points—two in your source clip and one on your timeline (or vice versa)—to dictate exactly where a clip starts, ends, and lands. Kdenlive uses these coordinates to calculate the fourth point automatically, ensuring precise frame placement without manual dragging and dropping.

Step-by-Step Guide to Three-Point Editing in Kdenlive

Step 1: Load and Prepare Your Source Clip

First, you need to select your source footage and define the portion you want to use.

  1. Double-click a video clip in your Project Bin to load it into the Clip Monitor (Source Monitor).
  2. Press Spacebar to play/pause, or use J, K, and L to rewind, pause, and fast-forward through your clip.
  3. Find the beginning of the desired segment and press I on your keyboard to set the In Point.
  4. Navigate to the end of the desired segment and press O to set the Out Point.

Step 2: Set the Destination Point on the Timeline

Next, tell Kdenlive where this footage should go on your timeline.

  1. Click on your timeline to make it the active window (or use Page Down / Page Up to navigate).
  2. Move your timeline playhead to the exact frame where you want the clip to begin. This playhead position serves as the third point (the Timeline In Point).
  3. Ensure the target track (e.g., V1 for video, A1 for audio) is selected and active.

Step 3: Perform the Edit Using Shortcuts

With your three points established (Clip In, Clip Out, and Timeline Playhead), you can perform the edit instantly using one of two keyboard shortcuts:

  • Insert Edit (Shortcut: V): Pressing V places the source clip at the timeline playhead position, cutting any existing clips at that point and shifting them to the right to make room. This is ideal when adding new scenes into the middle of an existing sequence.
  • Overwrite Edit (Shortcut: B): Pressing B places the source clip at the timeline playhead position, overwriting any footage already on the timeline for the duration of the new clip. Use this when replacing a placeholder or B-roll shot.

By mastering these steps and memorizing the I, O, V, and B keys, you can assemble complex sequences in Kdenlive entirely from your keyboard, keeping your editing workflow fast and fluid.
