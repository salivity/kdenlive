How to Add Copyright Metadata in Kdenlive

Adding metadata to your video files is crucial for protecting your intellectual property and establishing ownership. This article provides a step-by-step guide on how to embed author and copyright metadata directly into your Kdenlive project settings before rendering, ensuring your credentials are baked into the final video file.

To configure metadata in Kdenlive, follow these steps:

Step 1: Open Project Settings

Launch Kdenlive and open your project. In the top menu bar, click on Project and select Project Settings from the dropdown menu.

Step 2: Access the Metadata Tab

In the Project Settings dialog window, click on the Metadata tab. This tab displays a list of standard metadata keys that can be embedded into your video file.

Step 3: Enter Author and Copyright Information

Within the Metadata tab, you can input your creator details:

  • Locate the author key, click in the Value column next to it, and type your name or your organization's name.
  • Locate the copyright key, click in the value field, and enter your copyright statement (for example: Copyright © 2024 Your Name. All rights reserved).
  • If you want to add custom metadata fields that are not listed by default, click the Add (+) button at the bottom of the list to create a new key and value pair.

Step 4: Save the Settings

Once you have entered all the necessary information, click the Apply button at the bottom of the window to save the changes to your project, then click OK to close the dialog box.

Step 5: Render Your Video

With your metadata saved, you can proceed to render your video. Click on Project in the top menu and select Render (or press Ctrl + Enter). Choose your preferred output format, such as MP4 or WebM, and click Render to File.

Kdenlive utilizes FFmpeg during the rendering process to encode the metadata directly into the container format of the exported video. Once the render is complete, the copyright and author information will be readable by media players, file explorers, and digital asset management systems.
