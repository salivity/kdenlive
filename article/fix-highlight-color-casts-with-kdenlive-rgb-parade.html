Fix Highlight Color Casts with Kdenlive RGB Parade

The RGB Parade in Kdenlive is an essential video scope that separates visual data into discrete Red, Green, and Blue channels to display luminance intensity across a video frame. In color grading, its primary function regarding highlight regions is to provide an objective, visual measurement for identifying and correcting unwanted color casts. By observing the alignment of signal peaks at the top of each channel's waveform, editors can determine whether highlights contain a neutral white balance or suffer from an unnatural color tint.

How the RGB Parade Works

The RGB Parade plots image brightness on a vertical scale ranging from pure black (0% or 0 IRE at the bottom) to pure white (100% or 100 IRE at the top). The horizontal axis corresponds directly to the horizontal position of pixels across your video frame. Unlike a standard RGB waveform that overlays all three channels into a single composite graph, the parade displays the Red, Green, and Blue channels side-by-side.

Identifying Highlight Color Casts

In a correctly balanced video frame, neutral highlights—such as clouds, white walls, or direct reflections—should contain equal amounts of red, green, and blue light.

When evaluating highlights using the RGB Parade:

  • Balanced Highlights: If a scene contains neutral white elements, the highest peaks of the Red, Green, and Blue graphs will reach the exact same horizontal level near the top of the scope (typically around the 90–100% mark).
  • Warm/Red Cast: If the Red channel's peaks sit significantly higher than the Green and Blue channels in areas corresponding to bright objects, the highlights have a warm or reddish color cast.
  • Cool/Blue Cast: If the Blue waveform reaches the top boundary while the Red and Green waveforms remain lower, the highlights are overly cool or blue.
  • Tint Issues (Green/Magenta): Discrepancies where the Green channel is disproportionately elevated or suppressed compared to Red and Blue indicate green or magenta casts in the highlight regions.

Practical Application in Kdenlive

Detecting highlight casts visually on a computer monitor can be unreliable due to uncalibrated screens, environmental lighting, and eye fatigue. The RGB Parade serves as an objective reference tool.

To correct a cast identified in the highlights:

  1. Open the RGB Parade scope via Kdenlive's View menu.
  2. Apply a color correction effect to the clip, such as Color Curves, Lift/Gamma/Gain, or White Balance.
  3. Adjust the Gain (highlights) or the top-right points of individual color curves.
  4. Watch the RGB Parade until the peaks of the Red, Green, and Blue channels align evenly across the top threshold.

By leveling the peaks of all three channels on the RGB Parade, you ensure that the brightest areas of your footage remain clean, neutral, and free of unintended color contamination.
