How to Adjust Kdenlive Timeline Thumbnail Settings

Managing how video and audio clips display on your timeline is crucial for maintaining an efficient editing workflow and optimizing system performance. This article provides a direct, step-by-step guide on how to adjust, enable, or disable thumbnail and waveform displays on the Kdenlive timeline using both global settings and quick-access toolbar options.

Method 1: Adjusting Global Thumbnail Settings

To change the default behavior of clip thumbnails for all projects, use the main configuration menu:

  1. Open Kdenlive.
  2. Navigate to Settings in the top menu bar and select Configure Kdenlive... (On macOS, go to Kdenlive > Preferences).
  3. In the configuration window, click on the Timeline tab in the left-hand sidebar.
  4. Locate the Thumbnails category.
  5. Adjust the following settings according to your preference:
    • Video Thumbnails: Choose between No thumbnails, Only at start, Start and end, or All frames (In between). Note that selecting All frames provides the most visual detail but requires more processing power.
    • Audio Thumbnails: Check or uncheck the box to enable or disable audio waveforms on audio tracks.
  6. Click Apply and then OK to save your changes.

Method 2: Quick Toggle via the Timeline Toolbar

If you need to quickly toggle thumbnails on or off to free up system resources while editing, you can do so directly from the timeline interface:

  1. Look at the toolbar located directly above or below your timeline tracks.
  2. Click on the Timeline Options icon (represented by a gear icon or a hamburger menu, depending on your Kdenlive version).
  3. In the dropdown menu, check or uncheck Show Video Thumbnails to instantly toggle them on the active timeline.
  4. You can also toggle Show Audio Waveforms from this same menu to quickly hide or show audio details.

Method 3: Adjusting Individual Track Heights

Sometimes thumbnails do not appear because the timeline tracks are too small. You can adjust track heights to make thumbnails visible:

  1. Hover your mouse cursor over the boundary line between two track headers on the far left of the timeline.
  2. Click and drag the boundary up or down to adjust the height of the track.
  3. Increasing the track height will automatically make the video thumbnails and audio waveforms larger and easier to see.
