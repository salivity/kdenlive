How to Stabilize Shaky Video in Kdenlive

Shaky camera footage can distract viewers and lower the overall quality of your production, but Kdenlive offers a powerful, built-in tool to smooth out these unwanted camera movements. This article provides a quick, step-by-step guide on how to automatically analyze and stabilize shaky video footage in Kdenlive using its integrated stabilization tool, ensuring your final edit looks professional and steady.

Step 1: Import Your Footage

Open Kdenlive and import your shaky video clip into the Project Bin by dragging and dropping it, or by clicking the Add Clip or Folder button. Do not add the clip to the timeline just yet; the stabilization process is best performed directly on the source clip in the Project Bin.

Step 2: Open the Stabilize Tool

  1. Right-click the shaky video clip inside the Project Bin.
  2. Hover over Clip Jobs in the context menu.
  3. Select Stabilize from the submenu.

Step 3: Configure the Stabilization Settings

A configuration window will appear with several parameters. For most videos, the default settings work well, but you can adjust them for better results:

  • Shakiness: Set this on a scale from 1 (mildly shaky) to 10 (extremely shaky) to help the software understand the severity of the camera movement.
  • Accuracy: Higher values yield more precise stabilization but increase the processing time. A value between 5 and 15 is recommended.
  • Step Size: Determines how many pixels the algorithm skips during analysis. Lower values increase accuracy but slow down render times.
  • Zoom / Crop: Because stabilizing requires rotating and shifting the frame, the edges of the video will move. Checking the zoom option allows Kdenlive to slightly upscale the video to hide these shifting black borders.

Step 4: Run the Analysis

Once you have adjusted your settings, click OK. Kdenlive will begin analyzing the clip in the background. You can monitor the progress via the small loading bar on the clip's thumbnail in the Project Bin.

Step 5: Add the Stabilized Clip to Your Timeline

Once the analysis is complete, Kdenlive will generate a new, stabilized version of the file in your Project Bin (usually appended with .stabilized or indicated by a green checkmark icon). Drag this newly stabilized clip onto your timeline. Play back the video in the Project Monitor to review the smooth, shake-free results.
