How to Use Mask Apply with Rotoscoping in Kdenlive

This article provides a step-by-step guide on using Kdenlive’s "Mask Apply" effect in combination with the Rotoscoping tool. You will learn the correct effect stack order to isolate specific visual effects—such as blurs, color grading, or pixelation—strictly within a custom, animated boundary on your video clips.

The Three-Layer Effect Sandwich

In Kdenlive, masking works sequentially down the Effects stack. To constrain an effect to a rotoscoped shape, you must arrange three components in the following exact order:

  1. Rotoscoping (Mask): Defines the boundary and creates an internal alpha matte.
  2. Target Effect(s): The visual modification you want to apply (e.g., Gaussian Blur, Color Adjustment, Old Film).
  3. Mask Apply: Closes the operation and blends the modified masked area back onto the original clip.

Step-by-Step Implementation

1. Add the Video Clip to the Timeline

Drag your video footage onto a video track in the timeline and ensure the clip is selected.

2. Apply the Rotoscoping Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab.
  2. Search for Rotoscoping and add it to your clip.
  3. In the Project Monitor, click to create anchor points around the subject or region you want to isolate.
  4. Close the shape by clicking on the first node or right-clicking.
  5. In the Rotoscoping settings:
    • Set Mode to Alpha (or ensure it outputs an alpha mask).
    • Adjust Feather width to soften the edges if needed.
    • Use keyframes along the timeline to animate the mask if your subject moves.

3. Add Your Desired Effect

  1. Search for the effect you wish to constrain (for example, Gaussian Blur or Pixelize).
  2. Drag and drop it onto the clip.
  3. In the effect stack, ensure this effect is placed directly below the Rotoscoping effect.
  4. Adjust the effect's parameters (e.g., increase blur radius). At this stage, the effect may cover the entire frame or look incorrect; the next step will fix this.

4. Add the "Mask Apply" Effect

  1. Search for Mask Apply in the Effects panel.
  2. Drag it onto your clip.
  3. In the effect stack, ensure Mask Apply sits directly below your target effect.

Once placed, Kdenlive instantly limits the target effect to the exact area defined by your rotoscoped path.

Managing Multiple Effects Within the Mask

You can stack multiple filters between Rotoscoping and Mask Apply. Any filter placed between these two boundary effects will only affect the masked region. Filters placed above Rotoscoping or below Mask Apply will apply to the entire image normally.
