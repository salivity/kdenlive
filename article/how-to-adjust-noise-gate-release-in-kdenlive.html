How to Adjust Noise Gate Release in Kdenlive

This article provides a quick and clear guide on how to configure the release setting of the Noise Gate audio effect in Kdenlive. You will learn where to find the effect, how to apply it to your timeline audio, and how to fine-tune the release parameter to ensure smooth audio transitions without abrupt cutoffs.

Applying the Noise Gate Effect

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and select the audio or video clip on the timeline that requires noise reduction.
  2. Open the Effects tab (typically located in the workspace panels or via View > Effects).
  3. Search for Noise Gate in the search bar or navigate through the Audio categories.
  4. Drag and drop the Noise Gate effect directly onto your target clip in the timeline.

Locating and Adjusting the Release Parameter

  1. Select the clip on your timeline and open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the active filters.
  2. Locate the Noise Gate filter in the list.
  3. Find the Release (or Release time) slider among the effect parameters.
  4. Adjust the value by dragging the slider or manually typing a millisecond (ms) value:
    • Increase the release time if the audio cuts off too abruptly at the end of spoken words or sounds, creating an unnatural stutter.
    • Decrease the release time if unwanted background noise lingers too long after the main audio signal drops below the threshold.
  5. Play back the timeline to monitor the changes in real-time, fine-tuning the release duration until the gate closes smoothly after each phrase.
