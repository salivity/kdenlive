Custom Hotkeys for Group and Unlink in Kdenlive

Managing audio and video tracks efficiently in Kdenlive often requires frequent grouping and unlinking of clips on the timeline. This guide provides a straightforward method to access the shortcut settings in Kdenlive and assign custom keyboard shortcuts to group, ungroup, and unlink your selected media assets to speed up your editing workflow.

Step 1: Open the Shortcut Configuration Menu

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open any project.
  2. In the top application menu bar, click on Settings.
  3. Select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... from the dropdown menu (or press Ctrl + Alt + , if using default shortcuts).

Step 2: Search for Grouping and Unlinking Commands

Use the search bar at the top of the shortcut configuration dialog to locate the relevant commands:

  • Group Clips: Search for Group Clips. This command binds multiple selected timeline items together so they move and behave as a single unit.
  • Ungroup Clips: Search for Ungroup Clips. This command separates grouped clips, allowing you to move or edit the audio and video tracks independently.

Step 3: Assign Your Custom Hotkeys

  1. Select the action you want to modify (e.g., Group Clips) from the list.
  2. In the lower section of the window, select the Custom radio button next to the "Shortcut:" field.
  3. Click on the button labeled None (or the existing key combination).
  4. Press the key combination on your keyboard that you want to assign (for example, G for group and U for ungroup/unlink).
  5. If the shortcut is already assigned to another action, Kdenlive will display a conflict warning, allowing you to reassign it or choose a different combination.

Step 4: Apply and Save Changes

  1. Repeat the assignment process for the Ungroup Clips command.
  2. Click the OK button at the bottom right of the configuration window to save your settings.

Step 5: Test the Hotkeys on the Timeline

  1. Select both an audio clip and a video clip on the timeline.
  2. Press your custom hotkey for Ungroup Clips to separate the audio from the video.
  3. Select any independent clips and press your custom hotkey for Group Clips to link them back together.
