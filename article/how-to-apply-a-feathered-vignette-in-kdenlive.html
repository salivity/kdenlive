How to Apply a Feathered Vignette in Kdenlive

Applying a vignette with feathered edges in Kdenlive helps draw focus to the center of your frame by softly darkening or masking the outer edges of a video clip. This guide walks you through the step-by-step process of adding a vignette effect, adjusting its shape, and customizing the feathering for a natural transition using built-in Kdenlive tools.

Method 1: Using the Built-In Vignette Effect

The dedicated Vignette effect is the fastest way to add a darkened, feathered border to your footage.

  1. Select the Clip: Click on the target video clip in your timeline to select it.
  2. Open the Effects Panel: Navigate to the Effects tab in your workspace. If it is not visible, enable it via View > Effects.
  3. Search for Vignette: In the search bar of the Effects panel, type Vignette.
  4. Apply the Effect: Double-click the Vignette effect or drag and drop it onto your selected clip.
  5. Adjust Feathering and Size: Go to the Effect/Composition Stack panel to tweak the parameters:
    • Smoothness: Increases or decreases the feathering effect. Higher values create a softer, more gradual blend.
    • Radius: Determines how far inward the vignette extends from the edges toward the center.
    • Center X / Center Y: Shifts the focus point of the vignette if your subject is off-center.

Method 2: Using the Alpha Shapes Mask for Custom Transparency

If you want to create an alpha vignette mask where the edges feather into transparency or reveal a lower video track, use the Alpha Shapes effect.

  1. Select the Clip: Click on your video clip in the timeline.
  2. Add Alpha Shapes: Search for Alpha Shapes in the Effects panel and drag it onto your clip.
  3. Set the Shape: In the Effect Stack, set the Shape parameter to Ellipse.
  4. Configure the Feathering:
    • Transition Width: This controls the softness/feathering of the mask's boundary. Increase this slider to create a smooth gradient edge.
    • Size X and Size Y: Adjust the horizontal and vertical scale of the oval mask to frame your subject properly.
    • Position X and Position Y: Reposition the center of the mask over your focal point.
  5. Invert the Mask (Optional): If the effect cuts out the center instead of the borders, enable the Invert checkbox so only the outer perimeter is masked.
