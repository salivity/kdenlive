Fix Fisheye Distortion in Kdenlive With Defish

Action cameras typically use ultra-wide lenses that create severe barrel distortion, warping straight lines and bending the horizon. Kdenlive includes a dedicated "Defish" effect designed to reverse this distortion and flatten the field of view. This guide outlines the precise steps required to locate, apply, and configure the Defish effect on your action camera footage within Kdenlive.

1. Add Your Clip to the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your action camera footage into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag and drop the video clip onto a video track in the Timeline.

2. Find and Apply the Defish Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Type Defish into the search bar at the top of the Effects panel.
  3. Drag the Defish effect from the list and drop it directly onto your clip in the timeline, or double-click the effect while the clip is selected.

3. Adjust the Defish Parameters

Select the clip in the timeline and open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to access the Defish settings:

  • Amount: Adjust the slider to decrease the curvature. Moving the slider negative or positive will pull or push the distorted edges until curved lines (such as buildings or horizons) appear straight.
  • Scale / Zoom: Straightening a fisheye image often pulls the corners inward, exposing black borders. Increase the scale or zoom setting to crop past the borders and fill the screen frame.
  • Center X / Center Y: If the distortion is off-center, adjust the X and Y coordinates to align the lens center with the optical center of your footage.

4. Fine-Tune and Preview

  1. Scrub through the timeline using the Project Monitor to check different parts of the clip.
  2. Verify that horizontal and vertical lines in the background remain straight across movement.
  3. Make minor adjustments to the Amount parameter until the perspective looks natural.
