Adjust Composition Blend Modes in Kdenlive

In Kdenlive, adjusting blending modes allows you to blend visual elements from overlapping video tracks to create overlays, double exposures, lighting effects, and seamless transitions. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to add composition transitions to your timeline clips and modify their individual blend modes using the built-in composition properties.

Step 1: Add Overlapping Clips to the Timeline

  1. Place your base footage on a lower video track (e.g., Track V1).
  2. Place the secondary clip or overlay graphic directly above it on a higher video track (e.g., Track V2).
  3. Ensure the two clips overlap for the duration you want the blending effect to occur.

Step 2: Apply a Composition Transition

  1. Hover your cursor over the bottom-left or bottom-right corner of the upper clip on the timeline until the composition icon (a small red dot or arrow) appears.
  2. Click the icon to automatically insert a standard composition transition (such as Transform or Wipe) between the two tracks.
  3. Alternatively, open the Compositions tab in your interface, locate the desired composition (such as Composite and Transform or Cairo Blend), and drag it onto the transition area between the two tracks.

Step 3: Open the Composition Properties

  1. Click directly on the composition block located between the tracks on your timeline to highlight it.
  2. Navigate to the Effect/Composition Stack panel (usually located in the properties area of your workspace).

Step 4: Change the Blend Mode

  1. In the Effect/Composition Stack panel, look for the Compositing or Blend Mode dropdown menu.
  2. Click the menu to view the available blend modes, such as:
    • Multiply or Darken: Hides white areas and darkens the underlying video.
    • Screen or Lighten: Hides black areas and brightens the underlying video.
    • Overlay or Soft Light: Increases contrast and combines light and dark tones.
    • Color Dodge / Burn: Creates vibrant, high-contrast glow or burn effects.
  3. Select your preferred mode. The change will immediately reflect in the Clip/Project Monitor.

Step 5: Fine-Tune the Effect (Optional)

  • Opacity: Adjust the opacity slider within the composition properties to control the intensity of the blend.
  • Keyframes: Add keyframes to animate the blend mode's opacity over time for smooth fade-ins and fade-outs.
