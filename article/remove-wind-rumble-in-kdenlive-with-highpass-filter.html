Remove Wind Rumble in Kdenlive with Highpass Filter

Outdoor audio recordings frequently suffer from low-frequency wind noise, which can muddy dialogue and ruin an otherwise good take. In this guide, you will learn how to apply and configure the Highpass Filter in Kdenlive to cut out unwanted low-end rumble while keeping spoken voices clear and natural.

Step 1: Select the Audio Clip

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. Drag your footage or audio recording onto the Timeline.
  3. Click on the target audio clip (or video clip with embedded audio) to highlight it.

Step 2: Add the Highpass Filter

  1. Open the Effects tab (if it is not visible, go to View > Effects in the top menu).
  2. Click on the Audio effects category or use the search bar at the top of the Effects panel.
  3. Search for Highpass (or High Pass Filter / SOX Highpass Filter, depending on your Kdenlive version).
  4. Double-click the filter or drag and drop it directly onto your audio clip in the timeline.

Step 3: Configure the Frequency Settings

  1. Select the clip and open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the filter settings.
  2. Locate the Cutoff Frequency (measured in Hertz / Hz).
  3. Set the initial cutoff frequency:
    • 80 Hz to 100 Hz: Ideal for deep male voices or mild wind noise without affecting vocal warmth.
    • 100 Hz to 120 Hz: The standard sweet spot for most dialogue to eliminate standard wind and mic handling rumble.
    • 120 Hz to 160 Hz: Best for higher-pitched voices or aggressive wind noise (note that settings above 150 Hz may start making voices sound thin or tinny).

Step 4: Preview and Fine-Tune

  1. Play back the timeline while wearing headphones to accurately monitor the low frequencies.
  2. Adjust the frequency slider incrementally until the low-end rumble disappears while the speaker’s voice remains full and intelligible.
  3. Toggle the effect on and off using the eye icon or checkbox next to the filter name to compare the processed audio against the original.
