Diagonal Wipe Split Screen in Kdenlive

Creating a diagonal split-screen wipe effect in Kdenlive allows you to transition seamlessly between two video tracks using a dynamic angled cut. This article explains how to set up your timeline tracks, apply masking and wipe tools, and animate keyframes to achieve a clean diagonal split transition in your project.

Step 1: Arrange Video Clips on the Timeline

  1. Place your base clip on video track V1.
  2. Place the secondary clip on video track V2, directly above the clip on V1.
  3. Align the clips so they overlap at the exact duration where you want the diagonal split-screen transition to occur.

Step 2: Apply the Masking Effect

To create a precise diagonal split, use the Rotoscoping effect on the top clip:

  1. Select the clip on V2.
  2. Go to the Effects panel and search for Rotoscoping.
  3. Drag and drop the Rotoscoping effect onto the clip on V2.

Step 3: Draw the Diagonal Split

  1. In the Project Monitor, ensure the playhead is at the beginning of the transition.
  2. Click on the monitor to draw a polygon shape with a diagonal edge cutting across the frame.
  3. Close the shape by clicking on the first point you created. The clip on V2 will now be visible only within the outlined diagonal area, revealing V1 underneath.

Step 4: Animate the Diagonal Wipe (Optional)

If you want the diagonal split to move across the screen as an animated wipe:

  1. Open the Effect/Composition Stack panel with the V2 clip selected.
  2. Under the Rotoscoping parameters, enable keyframing for the mask.
  3. At the starting keyframe, drag the mask points completely off-screen in the direction the wipe should originate.
  4. Move the playhead to the end of the transition duration and add a new keyframe.
  5. Move the mask points across the screen until the top video fully covers or splits the frame as desired.
  6. Adjust the Feather parameter slightly (e.g., 2 to 5 pixels) to soften the diagonal edge if a smoother look is needed.

Alternative Method: Using the Wipe Composition

  1. Overlap the two clips on tracks V1 and V2.
  2. Click the bottom-left corner of the clip on V2 to add a Wipe composition between the tracks.
  3. In the composition settings, choose a diagonal gradient or luma matte from the Wipe file dropdown menu.
  4. Adjust the Softness and transition duration directly on the timeline.
