Adjust All Track Heights in Kdenlive

Managing your timeline layout in Kdenlive is essential for an efficient video editing workflow. This guide explains how to quickly expand, minimize, or resize the height of all audio and video tracks globally across the entire Kdenlive timeline using keyboard commands, modifier shortcuts, and custom hotkey assignments.

Using the Shift + Scroll Shortcut

The fastest way to resize all timeline tracks simultaneously is using the mouse wheel modifier shortcut:

  1. Hover your mouse cursor anywhere over the timeline track headers or empty timeline space.
  2. Hold down the Shift key on your keyboard.
  3. Scroll the Mouse Wheel Up to globally increase track heights or Mouse Wheel Down to globally decrease track heights.

Setting Dedicated Keyboard Shortcuts for Track Presets

Kdenlive allows you to assign direct keyboard-only shortcuts to instantly toggle global track heights between preset sizes (minimal, normal, and large).

To configure these shortcuts:

  1. In the top menu, go to Settings > Configure Keyboard Shortcuts (or press Ctrl + Alt + ,).
  2. In the search bar at the top of the dialog, type tracks.
  3. Locate the following global track actions:
    • Make All Tracks Minimal Height
    • Make All Tracks Normal Height
    • Make All Tracks Big
  4. Select the action you want to customize, choose the Custom radio button, and press the key combination you wish to assign (for example, Ctrl + Shift + Down for minimal, or Ctrl + Shift + Up for big).
  5. Click OK to save the settings.

Once assigned, pressing your custom hotkey combination anywhere in the timeline will instantly resize all tracks across the entire project without requiring manual mouse adjustments.
