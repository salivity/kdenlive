Adjust Low-End Cutoff on Master Channel in Kdenlive

Adjusting the low-end cutoff frequency on the master channel in Kdenlive allows you to remove unwanted low-frequency rumble, hum, or mud from your entire project's audio mix simultaneously. This is accomplished by opening the Master filter stack, applying a high-pass audio filter, and setting the precise cutoff frequency threshold to optimize overall audio clarity.

Step 1: Open the Master Filter Stack

  1. Look at the timeline interface in Kdenlive.
  2. Locate the Master track header, positioned above or at the far left of your individual audio/video tracks.
  3. Click on the Master button to open the Master Channel Filter Stack panel (if not already visible, enable it via Menu > Settings > Audio Mixer or View > Master Effects).

Step 2: Add a High Pass Filter

  1. Open the Effects tab in the project interface.
  2. Filter or search for Audio Effects.
  3. Locate the High Pass (or High Pass Filter) effect.
  4. Drag and drop the effect directly onto the Master panel, or double-click it while the Master track is active.

Step 3: Configure the Low-End Cutoff Frequency

  1. In the Master Filter Stack, find the controls for the newly added High Pass filter.
  2. Locate the Frequency or Cutoff Frequency slider.
  3. Set the frequency parameter to your desired cutoff point (commonly between 60 Hz and 120 Hz to eliminate sub-bass and handling noise without thinning speech).
  4. If available, adjust the Q or Roll-off Slope parameter to control how sharply the frequencies below the threshold are attenuated.

Step 4: Monitor and Refine

  1. Play back your project timeline with headphones or studio monitors.
  2. Toggle the effect on and off using the power icon in the filter stack to verify that the low-end rumble is removed without negatively affecting voices or essential low-end audio.
