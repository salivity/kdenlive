How to Create Animated Rounded PiP in Kdenlive

This guide outlines the process for building a smooth, animated picture-in-picture (PiP) effect with rounded corners in Kdenlive. By combining alpha masking tools to round the edges of your video with the Transform effect's keyframing capabilities, you can create a professional, dynamic overlay for tutorials, reaction videos, and presentations.

Step 1: Arrange the Timeline Tracks

  1. Place your main background video onto the lower video track (e.g., V1).
  2. Place the video or webcam footage intended for the picture-in-picture onto the track directly above it (e.g., V2).

Step 2: Apply the Rounded Corner Mask

To round the edges of the top video:

  1. Select the clip on V2.
  2. Open the Effects tab and search for the Alpha Shapes (or Shape Alpha) effect.
  3. Drag and drop the effect onto your top clip.
  4. In the effect settings:
    • Set Shape to Rectangle.
    • Adjust the Size X and Size Y sliders to crop the frame to your desired proportions.
    • Adjust the Transition Width or Border settings if you want a soft edge, or keep it low for a crisp edge.
    • Increase the Roundness / Corner Radius parameter to curve the rectangular corners.

Step 3: Position and Scale with the Transform Effect

  1. Search for the Transform effect in the Effects panel.
  2. Drag and drop it onto the V2 clip, placing it below or above the mask depending on your Kdenlive version (ensure the mask scales with the footage by keeping Transform active on the final composition).
  3. In the Transform controls, reduce the Scale (e.g., to 30% or 40%).
  4. Adjust the X and Y coordinates to position the PiP window into a corner of the frame.

Step 4: Animate the Picture-in-Picture

To make the PiP slide in, scale up, or move across the screen:

  1. Move the timeline playhead to the beginning of the clip on V2.
  2. In the Transform effect panel, add a keyframe.
  3. Move the PiP off-screen (by adjusting the X/Y coordinates) or set the Scale to 0% for a pop-in effect.
  4. Move the playhead forward by 0.5 to 1 second (15 to 30 frames).
  5. Add a second keyframe and set the final position and 100% target scale.
  6. Right-click the keyframes and select Smooth or Cubic interpolation to make the animation accelerate and decelerate naturally.

Step 5: Preview and Export

Play back the timeline to check the timing and smoothness of the entrance animation. You can duplicate the keyframes near the end of the clip and reverse them to make the rounded PiP animate off-screen when finished.
