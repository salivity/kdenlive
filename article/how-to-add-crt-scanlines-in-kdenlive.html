How to Add CRT Scanlines in Kdenlive

Simulating the look of a vintage cathode-ray tube (CRT) monitor or analog television is a popular stylistic choice in video editing. This guide explains the exact steps required to locate, apply, and configure the built-in Scanline effect in Kdenlive, allowing you to achieve a retro analog video display aesthetic quickly and efficiently.

1. Add Your Footage to the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and create or open your project.
  2. Import your video clip into the Project Bin.
  3. Drag and drop the clip onto a video track in the Timeline.

2. Locate the Scanline Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (if it is not visible, go to View > Effects in the top menu bar).
  2. In the search bar at the top of the Effects panel, type Scanline.
  3. Alternatively, navigate through the category folders to find it under the Stylize or Grain and Noise sections.

3. Apply the Effect to the Clip

  1. Click and drag the Scanline effect directly onto your video clip on the timeline.
  2. Alternatively, select the clip on the timeline and double-click the Scanline effect in the Effects list.

4. Adjust the Effect Parameters

  1. Select the clip on the timeline and open the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Customize the scanline properties to match your desired analog tube look:
    • Line Spacing / Frequency: Controls how dense the scanlines are across the vertical resolution of the screen.
    • Line Width / Thickness: Adjusts the thickness of each individual horizontal dark line.
    • Opacity / Intensity: Determines how strongly the dark lines blend over the original footage. Lower values create a subtle television effect, while higher values create a heavy retro monitor look.
    • Shift / Offset (if available): Allows you to vertically animate or shift the lines to simulate signal roll or screen refresh.

5. Enhance the Analog Tube Look (Optional)

To make the scanline simulation more convincing, stack these additional effects on the same clip:

  • RGB Shift / Chroma Shift: Creates chromatic aberration common in old glass tube televisions.
  • Vignette: Darkens the edges of the frame to mimic the curved curvature and falloff of CRT screens.
  • Glow or Blur: Adds a soft blooming effect to bright areas, simulating phosphor glow.

6. Preview and Render

  1. Play back the video in the Clip Monitor or Project Monitor to check the intensity of the lines.
  2. Adjust the zoom level in the preview window to 100% to view the scanlines at native resolution without scaling artifacts.
  3. Click the Render button (Ctrl + Enter) to export your final retro-styled video.
