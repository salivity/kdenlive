Custom JKL Playback Shortcuts in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to configure custom shortcut profiles in Kdenlive to achieve single-handed shuttle playback and timeline scrubbing. By remapping the standard multi-speed JKL navigation commands to a localized key cluster—such as the left hand's S, D, and F or J, K, and L—video editors can maintain continuous control over playback direction and speed while keeping their other hand free for mouse or keyboard-driven editing tasks.

Accessing the Keyboard Shortcuts Configuration

  1. Open Kdenlive.
  2. In the top menu bar, navigate to Settings and select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... (or press Ctrl + Alt + ,).
  3. In the search bar at the top of the shortcut configuration dialog, you will locate the commands required for shuttle playback.

Identifying the Required Shuttle Actions

Standard shuttle playback allows sequential speed increases (1x, 2x, 4x, 8x) in reverse or forward directions, alongside an instant pause. Search for and configure the following core playback actions:

  • Play / Pause (or Pause): Stops playback immediately.
  • Rewind (or Backward / Shuttle Backward): Decreases playback speed or begins playback in reverse, doubling reverse speed with consecutive presses.
  • Fast Forward (or Forward / Shuttle Forward): Initiates forward playback, doubling the forward speed with consecutive presses.

Mapping a One-Handed Layout

For single-handed operation (typically using the left hand to allow uninterrupted mouse usage with the right hand), map the commands to adjacent keys on the home row:

  1. Reverse Playback: Select the Rewind / Shuttle Backward action, click the Custom radio button, click the input box, and press your desired reverse key (e.g., S or A).
  2. Stop Playback: Select Play / Pause or Pause, choose Custom, and assign your center key (e.g., D or S).
  3. Forward Playback: Select Fast Forward / Shuttle Forward, choose Custom, and assign your forward key (e.g., F or D).

Note: If Kdenlive prompts you about a key conflict, confirm the overwrite if the conflicting function is not essential to your workflow, or assign an alternate key.

Saving and Managing Custom Shortcut Schemes

  1. In the Configure Keyboard Shortcuts dialog, click the Manage Schemes or Export button (represented by a save or preset icon).
  2. Choose New Scheme, name the profile (e.g., One-Handed Navigation), and click OK.
  3. Click Apply, then OK to close the settings window.

Your new profile is now active. Tapping the forward key will cycle through playback speeds (1x, 2x, 4x, etc.), tapping the reverse key will cycle through reverse playback speeds, and tapping the center key will instantly freeze the playhead on the current frame.
