How to Pan Panoramas in Kdenlive Using Transform

This guide explains how to create a smooth, cinematic panning animation across high-resolution panoramic images using Kdenlive. By leveraging the Transform effect and keyframe interpolation, you can smoothly navigate wide imagery without losing resolution or introducing jittery motion.

1. Import and Prepare the Panoramic Image

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your high-resolution panoramic image into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag the image onto a video track in the Timeline.
  3. Click and drag the right edge of the clip to set the desired duration for your pan. A longer duration creates a slower, more cinematic motion.

2. Apply the Transform Effect

  1. Select the clip on the timeline.
  2. Open the Effects tab (typically located in the project workspace panels).
  3. Search for Transform under the Transform, Distort and Perspective category.
  4. Drag and drop the Transform effect onto your image clip, or double-click it while the clip is selected.

3. Set the Starting Position

  1. Ensure the timeline playhead is at the very beginning (the first frame) of the clip.
  2. Go to the Effect Stack / Properties panel to view the Transform settings.
  3. Adjust the Size (or Scale) parameter to zoom into the panorama so that it fills the frame vertically without letterboxing.
  4. Adjust the X position coordinate to align the view with the side of the panorama where you want the pan to begin (typically the far left or far right).
  5. Ensure a keyframe is active at this starting point.

4. Set the Ending Position

  1. Move the playhead to the last frame (or near the end) of the clip.
  2. In the Transform properties, change the X position value to slide the image to its final framing position.
  3. Kdenlive will automatically generate a new keyframe at this position.

5. Configure Smooth Keyframe Interpolation

  1. Look at the keyframe ruler in the Transform effect properties.
  2. Right-click the starting keyframe and select Smooth (or Ease In / Ease Out, depending on your Kdenlive version) instead of Linear.
  3. Right-click the ending keyframe and set its interpolation to Smooth.
  4. This removes abrupt speed changes at the start and stop points, giving the pan a natural deceleration and acceleration.

6. Preview and Adjust Speed

  1. Play back the timeline to preview the animation.
  2. If the pan moves too fast, extend the clip length on the timeline and drag the ending keyframe further to the right.
  3. If motion blur is desired, you can combine this setup with the Motion Blur effect or adjust the project rendering settings.
