How to Use Edge Glow on Titles in Kdenlive

Adding an "Edge Glow" effect to titles in Kdenlive is a quick way to create stylized, neon, or highlighted outlines for your text. This guide outlines the exact steps to create a title clip, apply the Edge Glow filter, and adjust its parameters to achieve a vibrant, professional glowing text effect directly on your timeline.

1. Create and Position Your Title Clip

  1. Open your Kdenlive project.
  2. In the Project Bin, right-click an empty area and select Add Title Clip (or use the shortcut Alt + T).
  3. In the Title Clip editor window, type your desired text. Use a bold or thick font to make the edge glow effect more prominent.
  4. Set the text color and background. Ensure the background remains transparent so the glow applies strictly to the text contours.
  5. Click Create Title to save it to your Project Bin.
  6. Drag the newly created title clip onto a video track in the timeline above your background footage.

2. Add the Edge Glow Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (typically located in the workspace panels or via Menu > View > Effects).
  2. In the search bar at the top of the Effects panel, type Edge Glow.
  3. Drag and drop the Edge Glow effect directly onto your title clip in the timeline, or select the title clip and double-click the effect.

3. Configure the Glow Parameters

  1. Select the title clip on the timeline to reveal the effect settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Adjust the available parameters to fine-tune the look:
    • Threshold: Controls which parts of the title's edges trigger the glow. Lowering the threshold allows more of the edge region to glow.
    • Intensity / Brightness: Increases or decreases the luminescence and strength of the glowing outline.
    • Radius / Blur: Expands or contracts the spread of the glow around the text edges.
    • Color: Depending on the specific version of the filter, select the color tone you want the outline glow to emit.

4. Preview and Render

  1. Play through the timeline to preview the effect in the Project Monitor.
  2. If the text becomes hard to read over complex backgrounds, consider adding a slight drop shadow via the Title Clip editor or stacking a secondary color correction effect.
  3. Once satisfied with the outline appearance, render your project as normal.
