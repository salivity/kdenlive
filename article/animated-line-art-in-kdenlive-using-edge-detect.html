Animated Line Art in Kdenlive Using Edge Detect

Transforming standard video into animated line art is an effective way to create stylized, rotoscope-like animations in Kdenlive. By applying the "Edge Detect" filter to high-contrast footage, the software identifies luminance boundaries and converts them into distinct outlines. This guide walks you through preparing your footage, applying the filter, fine-tuning edge sensitivity, and styling the final animated line art.

Step 1: Prepare and Import High-Contrast Footage

Edge detection algorithms rely on distinct differences in brightness and color to identify boundaries.

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your source video into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag the clip onto an active video track in the timeline.
  3. If your video lacks distinct separation between the subject and background, open the Effects tab, add a Brightness (Curves) or Contrast effect, and boost the contrast before applying edge detection.

Step 2: Apply the Edge Detect Effect

  1. Select the video clip on the timeline.
  2. Go to the Effects panel (usually located on the left or accessible via View > Effects).
  3. In the search bar, type Edge Detect (or look under the Image Adjustment / Stylize categories).
  4. Drag and drop the Edge Detect effect onto your timeline clip.

Step 3: Configure the Edge Detect Parameters

Once applied, the effect options appear in the Effect/Composition Stack panel:

  • Algorithm / Function: Choose the calculation method (such as Sobel, Prewitt, or standard edge detection) based on which creates the cleanest line definition for your specific footage.
  • Threshold / Sensitivity: Adjust the threshold value to control line density. Lower values reveal subtle details and textures, while higher values isolate only the strongest outlines.
  • Color / Channel: Choose whether the detection processes all RGB channels or restricts itself to luminance/alpha to reduce artifacting.

Step 4: Refine the Line Art Appearance

Raw edge detection typically yields white or colored outlines on a solid black background. To refine the visual style:

  • Classic Ink on Paper Look: Add the Invert effect directly below the Edge Detect effect in the stack. This changes the background to white and the lines to dark outlines.
  • Remove Video Noise: Add a Threshold or Levels filter to crush faint noise, ensuring only bold, clean lines remain visible.
  • Monochrome Outlines: Add a Greyscale or Desaturate effect if you want pure black-and-white lines without chromatic fringe.

Step 5: Preview and Render

  1. Play back the timeline to ensure the edges track smoothly across motion frames without excessive jitter.
  2. Adjust the threshold parameters if fast movement causes lines to break up.
  3. Click the Render button, choose your preferred video format (such as MP4 or WebM), and export your animated line art video.
