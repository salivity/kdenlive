How to Map In and Out Hotkeys in Kdenlive

Customizing keyboard shortcuts in Kdenlive allows video editors to quickly define In and Out points (zones) for clips and project timelines without relying on mouse clicks. This guide provides a direct, step-by-step walkthrough on how to locate the shortcut configuration menu, search for the zone-marking commands, and map your preferred keys for an instant editing workflow.

Step 1: Open the Shortcut Configuration Menu

  1. Launch Kdenlive.
  2. Click on Settings in the top menu bar.
  3. Select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... (or press Ctrl + Alt + , on your keyboard).

Step 2: Locate the In and Out Marker Actions

In the search bar at the top of the Configure Shortcuts window, type the action you want to map:

  • For In points: Type Set Zone In
  • For Out points: Type Set Zone Out

Note: Depending on your Kdenlive version, these may also appear as "Mark In" and "Mark Out".

Step 3: Assign Your Custom Hotkeys

  1. Click on the action name (e.g., Set Zone In) to select it.
  2. In the lower section of the window, select the Custom radio button.
  3. Click the input button next to Custom (it may say "None" or show the current key).
  4. Press the key or key combination you wish to assign (commonly I for In and O for Out).
  5. If the chosen key is already assigned to another action, a conflict dialog will appear. Choose whether to reassign it or pick a different key.
  6. Repeat the process for Set Zone Out.

Step 4: Save and Apply

  1. Click OK or Apply at the bottom right to save your changes.
  2. Return to the timeline or clip monitor to test your new hotkeys. Pressing your designated In and Out keys will now instantly define the active zone.
