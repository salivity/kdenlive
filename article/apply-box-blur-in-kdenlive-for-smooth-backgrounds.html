Apply Box Blur in Kdenlive for Smooth Backgrounds

Creating a smooth, out-of-focus background texture in Kdenlive is a popular technique for framing vertical videos, enhancing text overlays, and softening busy footage. By applying the Box Blur effect to a lower video track, you can transform high-contrast video elements into soft, ambient color fields. This guide details the exact steps required to set up your timeline, apply the Box Blur effect, and configure the settings for a clean aesthetic.

Step 1: Set Up the Timeline Tracks

  1. Open Kdenlive and add at least two video tracks to your timeline (labeled V1 for the background and V2 for the foreground).
  2. Place your main subject clip on track V2.
  3. Place the footage you want to blur on track V1 directly beneath the foreground clip. If you are creating a matching blurred background for vertical video, duplicate the main clip from V2 onto V1.

Step 2: Scale the Background Clip

  1. Select the clip on track V1.
  2. Go to the Effects tab and search for Transform.
  3. Drag the Transform effect onto the clip on V1.
  4. In the effect parameters, increase the Size or Scale percentage until the background footage fills the entire screen canvas.

Step 3: Apply the Box Blur Effect

  1. In the Effects panel, type Box Blur into the search bar (found under the Blur and hide category).
  2. Drag and drop the Box Blur effect onto the background clip on track V1.

Step 4: Configure Blur Parameters for Smoothness

  1. Select the background clip to open its settings in the Effect / Composition Stack panel.
  2. Locate the Box Blur controls:
    • Horizontal Radius: Increase this value to spread the blur horizontally.
    • Vertical Radius: Increase this value to spread the blur vertically. Keep both values balanced to avoid directional motion-blur effects.
    • Power / Passes: Increase the number of iterations or passes if available to eliminate harsh blocky edges and produce a smoother gradient.
  3. Adjust the radius sliders until individual objects in the footage disappear, leaving only soft color shapes and abstract motion.

Step 5: Adjust Contrast and Darkness (Optional)

  1. Search for the Brightness (Keyframable) or Color Balance effect in the Effects panel.
  2. Drag it onto the blurred background clip on V1.
  3. Slightly reduce the brightness or adjust the opacity to ensure high contrast between your background texture and the foreground content on V2.
