Dual Screen HDMI Preview Setup in Kdenlive

Setting up dual-screen preview monitoring over HDMI in Kdenlive allows video editors to view full-screen video playback on a dedicated secondary monitor or TV while keeping the primary workspace clean and organized. This guide outlines the necessary hardware connection and software configuration steps to enable external monitor playback in Kdenlive on Linux and Windows systems.

Step 1: Connect and Configure Your Hardware

  1. Connect your secondary display to your computer using an HDMI cable.
  2. Power on the external monitor and select the correct HDMI input source.
  3. Open your operating system’s display settings (Windows Settings > System > Display, or Linux Display Configuration) and ensure the desktop is set to Extend these displays (or Join Displays), not duplicate/mirror.

Step 2: Undock and Move the Preview Monitor

Kdenlive uses a modular workspace interface, making it simple to place your project or clip preview on another screen.

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open your project.
  2. Locate the Project Monitor or Clip Monitor panel in the interface.
  3. Click the Undock icon (the small overlapping squares icon) on the top-right corner of the monitor panel, or click and drag the tab directly out of the main window.
  4. Drag the detached monitor window over to your HDMI-connected secondary screen.
  5. Double-click the title bar of the undocked window or press F11 to maximize it for full-screen playback.

Step 3: Use Dedicated Fullscreen Output (Alternative Method)

If you prefer a seamless, borderless full-screen feed without manual window resizing:

  1. Right-click inside the Project Monitor viewport.
  2. Select Switch to Fullscreen from the context menu (or press the assigned keyboard shortcut, typically F or Ctrl+Shift+F depending on your shortcut settings).
  3. If the preview appears on your primary screen, drag the window to the HDMI display or select the target display under Settings > Configure Kdenlive > Playback.

Step 4: Configure Video Drivers and Audio Routing (Optional)

  1. Go to Settings > Configure Kdenlive > Playback.
  2. Under the Audio and Video Output settings, verify that your backend driver (such as OpenGL or SDL) is active.
  3. If you want audio to route through your HDMI monitor’s built-in speakers, set the Audio Backend or your operating system's default audio output device to your HDMI device.
