Adjust Center Frequency in Kdenlive Parametric Equalizer

This guide explains how to locate and adjust the center frequency parameter using the Parametric Equalizer effect in Kdenlive. By following these step-by-step instructions, you will learn how to add the equalizer to an audio clip, navigate its frequency controls, and modify the target frequency range to clean up or enhance your project's audio.

1. Add the Parametric Equalizer to Your Clip

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and select the audio clip (or video clip with attached audio) on the timeline.
  2. Open the Effects panel (usually located in the top-left or side dock).
  3. In the search bar, type Parametric Equalizer or navigate to the Audio Correction category.
  4. Drag and drop the Equalizer (Parametric) effect onto your selected timeline clip, or double-click the effect to apply it.

2. Open the Effect Properties

  1. Click on the clip on the timeline to ensure it is actively selected.
  2. Switch to the Effect/Composition Stack panel (often located next to the Project Bin or Properties tab).
  3. Locate the Parametric Equalizer in the stack to view its adjustable parameters.

3. Adjust the Center Frequency

  1. Within the equalizer controls, locate the frequency slider corresponding to the band you want to alter (often labeled Center Frequency, Frequency, or Freq [Hz]).
  2. To set the value:
    • Drag the slider left to lower the center frequency (targeting bass/low-end sounds) or right to raise it (targeting treble/high-end sounds).
    • Double-click the numerical value next to the slider to type an exact frequency in Hertz (Hz) directly.

4. Fine-Tune Related Settings

To effectively use your adjusted center frequency, modify its complementary settings:

  • Gain (dB): Increase (boost) or decrease (cut) the volume level centered at your chosen frequency.
  • Bandwidth / Q Factor: Adjust how wide or narrow the frequency range around the center frequency will be affected. A high Q/narrow bandwidth targets precise problem frequencies (like hums or hisses), while a low Q/wide bandwidth applies a gentle, musical adjustment.

5. Preview and Refine

Play back the timeline using the Spacebar to monitor your changes in real-time. Toggle the effect on and off using the eye icon in the Effect Stack to compare the processed audio against the original track until you achieve the desired sound.
