How to Swap Left and Right Audio Channels in Kdenlive

This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step tutorial on how to use the "Swap Channels" audio effect in Kdenlive to reverse the left and right stereo fields of your audio tracks. Whether you need to correct a microphone channel mistake from recording or achieve a specific spatial audio effect for your video project, this article will show you exactly how to locate, apply, and manage this effect in Kdenlive.

Step 1: Add Your Clip to the Timeline

First, open your Kdenlive project and ensure the video or audio clip you want to edit is dragged onto the timeline. If your audio is grouped with a video track, you can apply the effect directly to the combined clip, or right-click and select Split Audio to work on the audio track independently.

Step 2: Open the Effects Panel

Locate the Effects tab, which is typically found in the lower-left utility area of the default Kdenlive workspace. If you cannot see it, enable it by going to the top menu and selecting View > Effects.

Step 3: Search for "Swap Channels"

In the search bar at the top of the Effects panel, type Swap channels. The effect will appear under the Audio / Channel manipulation category.

Step 4: Apply the Effect to Your Clip

Click and drag the Swap channels effect from the list and drop it directly onto your target audio clip on the timeline.

Step 5: Verify and Adjust

Once applied, the effect instantly reverses the left and right stereo channels. You can verify the effect is active by selecting the audio clip and looking at the Effect/Composition Stack panel (usually located in the top-middle or top-right of your workspace). Here, you can click the eye icon next to "Swap channels" to toggle the effect on and off, allowing you to compare the original stereo field with the reversed version.
