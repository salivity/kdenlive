Snap Playhead to Clip Start or End in Kdenlive

Navigating the timeline efficiently is crucial for precise video editing. This article provides a quick, step-by-step guide on how to snap the playhead to the exact beginning or end of a specific clip in Kdenlive using keyboard shortcuts and the built-in timeline snapping tools.

Method 1: Use Keyboard Shortcuts (Fastest)

Kdenlive features built-in keyboard shortcuts designed to jump the playhead directly to clip boundaries (cuts) on the timeline.

  1. Click on the timeline to make it active.
  2. Press Alt + Right Arrow to jump the playhead to the next cut, which represents the exact end of your current clip (or the start of the next one).
  3. Press Alt + Left Arrow to jump the playhead to the previous cut, which represents the exact beginning of your current clip.

Note: If you have a specific track targeted, the playhead will prioritize the cuts on that active track.

Method 2: Enable Timeline Snapping (Mouse Dragging)

If you prefer using the mouse, Kdenlive has a magnetic snapping feature that forces the playhead to lock onto clip edges when dragged close to them.

  1. Look at the toolbar located directly below the timeline.
  2. Ensure the Snap tool (represented by a magnet icon) is turned on. You can also toggle this feature on and off by pressing the Caps Lock key (or Shift + S depending on your Kdenlive version).
  3. Click and drag the playhead cursor along the ruler.
  4. As you approach the start or end of a clip, you will feel the playhead "stick" or snap directly to the boundary line. Release the mouse to set it in place.
