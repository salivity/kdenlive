How to Solo Audio Tracks in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to isolate and audition individual microphone channels in Kdenlive using track soloing features. By soloing specific audio tracks in either the timeline or the Audio Mixer, you can effectively monitor individual voice recordings, troubleshoot background noise, and balance multi-microphone setups without interference from other audio layers.

Method 1: Solo Tracks Directly from the Timeline

The fastest way to audition a single microphone channel during playback is through the timeline track headers.

  1. Locate the Track Header: Identify the audio track (e.g., Audio 1, Audio 2) containing the microphone channel you want to inspect.
  2. Click the Solo Icon: In the track header on the left side of the timeline, click the Solo button (represented by a headphone icon or a stylized "S" depending on your Kdenlive theme).
  3. Audition the Audio: Press the Spacebar to play the timeline. All other audio tracks will be temporarily muted, allowing you to hear only the selected microphone track.
  4. Disable Solo Mode: Click the Solo icon again to unmute the rest of the project.

Method 2: Audition Using the Audio Mixer

For precise level monitoring alongside solo listening, use Kdenlive's built-in Audio Mixer.

  1. Open the Audio Mixer: Go to the top menu and select View > Audio Mixer (or ensure the tab is visible in your workspace).
  2. Find the Channel Strip: Look for the vertical fader strip corresponding to the track of the target microphone.
  3. Engage Solo: Click the S (Solo) button located on that channel's strip.
  4. Monitor Levels: Play your timeline to audition the audio while watching the peak meters to check for clipping or low signal levels. Click S again to return to the full mix.

Handling Multi-Channel Microphone Clips

If your recording contains multiple microphone feeds inside a single stereo or multi-channel audio file, you must separate them onto individual tracks before soloing:

  1. Split the Channels: Right-click the clip in the Project Bin or Timeline, go to Clip Jobs, and choose Extract Audio or right-click the timeline clip and select Split Audio to separate channels.
  2. Assign to Dedicated Tracks: Place each extracted microphone stream onto its own audio track (such as Track A1 for Mic 1 and Track A2 for Mic 2).
  3. Solo Individual Tracks: Use the timeline or mixer solo buttons described above to audit each speaker's microphone independently for bleed, noise floor, and clarity.
