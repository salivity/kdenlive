Export OGG Vorbis Audio from Kdenlive

Exporting a dedicated audio master track from Kdenlive is a straightforward process that extracts the mixed audio from your timeline without rendering video. This guide details the exact steps required to configure the render settings, select the OGG Vorbis preset, and export a high-quality, standalone audio file for your project.

Step 1: Open the Render Dialog

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and ensure your audio tracks are mixed and unmuted as desired.
  2. Click the Render button in the main toolbar, or use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Return (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Return (macOS). Alternatively, select Project > Render from the top menu.

Step 2: Select the Audio Preset

  1. In the Render window, locate the Presets panel on the left.
  2. Expand the Audio only category.
  3. Select OGG Vorbis from the list of available audio formats.

Step 3: Configure Render Settings

  1. Output file: Click the folder icon next to the "Output file" field to choose the destination directory and name your audio file (e.g., master_track.ogg).
  2. Quality/Bitrate: Adjust the quality slider if necessary. By default, Kdenlive provides standard high-quality Vorbis encoding, but you can increase the quality value for higher fidelity.
  3. Render zone: Under the "Source" or "Range" section, ensure Full Project is selected to export the entire timeline, or choose Selected Zone if you only wish to render a specific in/out region.

Step 4: Render the Audio File

  1. Click the Render to File button at the bottom of the window.
  2. The Job Queue tab will appear and display the export progress.
  3. Once the progress bar reaches 100%, your dedicated .ogg master track will be saved to your specified directory, ready for distribution or further mastering.
