How to Pin Effects to Favorites in Kdenlive

Finding your most-used video and audio effects in Kdenlive can slow down your editing workflow. This guide provides a quick, step-by-step walkthrough on how to pin specific effects to the "Favorite Effects" tab in Kdenlive, allowing you to access your go-to tools instantly.

Step 1: Open the Effects Panel

First, ensure that the Effects panel is visible in your Kdenlive workspace. If you cannot see it, go to the top menu and select View, then check the box next to Effects. The panel will appear, usually docked alongside your Project Bin.

Step 2: Locate Your Desired Effect

Use the search bar at the top of the Effects panel to search for the specific effect you want to pin (for example, "Transform" or "Color Correction"). Alternatively, you can browse through the categorized folders like "Motion," "Color," or "Audio."

Step 3: Add the Effect to Favorites

Once you find the effect, you can add it to your favorites using one of two methods:

  • The Star Icon: Hover your mouse over the effect. A small star icon will appear next to its name. Click this star to highlight it.
  • Right-Click Menu: Right-click on the effect name and select Add to Favorites from the context menu.

Step 4: Access Your Pinned Effects

At the top of the Effects panel, you will see a row of category icons. Click on the Star icon tab. This is your "Favorite Effects" tab. All of the effects you pinned will now be listed here for quick drag-and-drop access directly onto your timeline clips.

How to Remove an Effect from Favorites

If you want to clean up your favorites list, simply go to the Favorites tab (the star icon), right-click on the effect you want to remove, and select Remove from Favorites, or click the highlighted star icon next to the effect name to deselect it.
