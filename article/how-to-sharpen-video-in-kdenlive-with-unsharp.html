How to Sharpen Video in Kdenlive with Unsharp

This guide explains how to restore clarity and enhance fine details on compressed video footage using the Unsharp effect in Kdenlive. Heavily compressed video files often suffer from soft edges and lost texture, but by applying and properly configuring the Unsharp filter, you can increase local edge contrast to make your footage look significantly crisper without introducing excessive visual noise.

Step 1: Add Your Clip to the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and load your project.
  2. Drag the compressed video clip from the Project Bin onto a video track in the Timeline.
  3. Select the clip on the timeline to ensure it is active for editing.

Step 2: Locate and Apply the Unsharp Filter

  1. Open the Effects panel (if it is not visible, enable it via View > Effects in the top menu).
  2. Click on the search bar in the Effects panel and type Unsharp.
  3. Locate the Unsharp (or Unsharp Mask) filter under the Blur and Sharpen or Stylize categories.
  4. Drag the Unsharp effect directly onto your video clip in the timeline, or double-click the effect while the clip is selected.

Step 3: Configure the Effect Parameters

Once applied, open the Properties / Effect Stack panel to view the Unsharp settings. To properly enhance fine details on compressed video without magnifying compression artifacts, adjust the following sliders carefully:

  • Size (Radius): This controls the width of the edges being enhanced. For fine detail enhancement on compressed video, keep this value low (typically between 3 and 7). Higher values create thick, unnatural halo rings around objects.
  • Scale (Amount / Strength): This sets the intensity of the sharpening effect. Start with a low value (around 0.2 to 0.8) and gradually increase it until the details look natural. Avoid over-sharpening, which causes the video to look pixelated or jagged.
  • Threshold: This parameter determines how different adjacent pixel values must be before sharpening is applied. Increasing the threshold prevents the filter from sharpening background compression noise, macroblocks, or subtle gradients while still enhancing well-defined edges.

Step 4: Preview and Fine-Tune

  1. Set your Project Monitor zoom to 100% (1:1) to inspect the fine textures accurately.
  2. Toggle the effect on and off using the checkbox next to the Unsharp effect name in the Effect Stack panel to compare the original footage with the sharpened output.
  3. If high-contrast outlines (halos) appear around objects, decrease the Size or Scale.
  4. If compression noise in flat areas (like skies or walls) becomes too noticeable, increase the Threshold.
