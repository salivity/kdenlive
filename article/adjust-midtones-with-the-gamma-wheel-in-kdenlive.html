Adjust Midtones with the Gamma Wheel in Kdenlive

Adjusting the midtone balance is a crucial step in video color grading to ensure natural skin tones, correct exposure, and set the overall mood of a scene. In Kdenlive, midtones are controlled using the "Gamma" color wheel within the color correction effects. This guide walks you through navigating the color grading workspace, understanding the function of the Gamma wheel, and making precise chromatic and luminance adjustments to balance the midtones in your footage.

1. Access the Color Grading Tools

To access the color wheels in Kdenlive:

  1. Switch to the Color workspace by selecting it from the workspace selector in the top-right corner of the interface.
  2. Select the clip on your timeline that requires color correction.
  3. Open the Effects tab and navigate to the Color and Image Correction category.
  4. Add the Color Grading (or Lift/Gamma/Gain) effect to your clip.

The effect panel will display three main color wheels: Lift (Shadows), Gamma (Midtones), and Gain (Highlights).

2. Understand the Role of the Gamma Wheel

The Gamma wheel selectively targets the middle exposure range of your image (roughly between 20 IRE and 80 IRE). While the Lift and Gain controls anchor your extreme blacks and whites, the Gamma wheel lets you:

  • Modify Midtone Luminance (Brightness): Adjust the exposure of subjects, faces, and backgrounds without clipping highlights or crushing shadows.
  • Shift Midtone Chrominance (Color Cast): Correct unwanted color tints or introduce specific hues to skin tones and intermediate scene elements.

3. Adjust Midtone Brightness (Luminance)

Next to the Gamma color wheel, there is a dedicated vertical slider for luminance (lightness):

  • To brighten midtones: Move the Gamma slider upward. This elevates subject exposure without blowing out specular highlights.
  • To darken midtones: Move the Gamma slider downward. This increases contrast and saturation across the mid-range without crushing pure black values.

4. Adjust Midtone Color Balance (Chrominance)

In the center of the circular Gamma wheel, there is a central control point (crosshair):

  1. Identify the Color Shift: Determine if your midtones appear too warm (orange/yellow), cool (blue), or have a tint (magenta/green).
  2. Move the Control Point: Click and drag the point in the opposite direction of the unwanted color cast to neutralize it (e.g., drag toward cyan/blue to counter excess red/yellow).
  3. Fine-Tune Skin Tones: Drag slightly toward the orange/red vector if human skin appears washed out or overly pale.
  4. Make Subtle Adjustments: Keep movements close to the center point. Small adjustments on the Gamma wheel result in significant color shifts across the image.

5. Verify Adjustments with Scopes

To ensure accurate balance, use Kdenlive's built-in monitoring scopes located in the workspace:

  • Waveform Monitor: Watch the center portion of the waveform expand or contract as you move the Gamma luminance slider.
  • RGB Parade: Check that the red, green, and blue levels align closely in the middle vertical zone to confirm neutral midtone balance.
  • Vectorscope: Verify that skin tones line up along the standard skin-tone indicator line.
