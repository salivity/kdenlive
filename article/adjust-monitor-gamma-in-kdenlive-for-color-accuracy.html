Adjust Monitor Gamma in Kdenlive for Color Accuracy

Adjusting your monitor gamma in Kdenlive ensures that the colors and luminance levels you see in the preview monitor match the final exported video across different displays. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on navigating Kdenlive’s preferences, configuring the display gamma profile, and calibrating your preview settings to achieve consistent, broadcast-standard color accuracy.

Step 1: Open the Configuration Menu

  1. Launch Kdenlive.
  2. In the top menu bar, click on Settings and select Configure Kdenlive... (or press Ctrl + Shift + , on Linux/Windows).

Step 2: Navigate to Color Management and Playback

  1. In the left sidebar of the Preferences window, select Playback.
  2. Locate the Color Management or Display Profile section. Depending on your version of Kdenlive, color-handling settings may also reside under the Timeline or Monitors tabs.

Step 3: Set the Monitor Gamma and Color Profile

  1. Enable Color Management: Ensure the color management toggle is checked so Kdenlive can translate project colors to your monitor space.
  2. Select the Display Profile: If you use a calibrated monitor with an ICC profile, select your system display profile from the dropdown menu.
  3. Adjust Preview Gamma:
    • Set the gamma to 2.4 if you are mastering video for standard broadcast television or dark viewing environments (Rec.709 standard).
    • Set the gamma to 2.2 if you are producing content primarily intended for the web, mobile devices, and computer monitors (sRGB standard).

Step 4: Configure the Video Driver Output

  1. Under the same Playback tab, check the Video Driver (such as OpenGL or DirectX depending on your OS).
  2. Ensure the driver is set to render in full range or auto-detect, preventing unintended contrast expansion or crushed shadow details in the preview window.

Step 5: Apply and Verify Changes

  1. Click Apply, then click OK to close the configuration window.
  2. Restart Kdenlive if prompted, ensuring the new display gamma settings take effect.
  3. Use the built-in color scopes (go to View > Color Scopes and enable Vectorscope and RGB Parade) alongside your preview monitor to confirm that your visual adjustments correspond accurately to the digital signal values.
