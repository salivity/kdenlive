Map Hotkeys to Lock All Video Tracks in Kdenlive

Locking tracks in Kdenlive prevents accidental edits, cuts, or shifts to finalized footage while you work on complex timeline arrangements. This guide provides a straightforward method to configure a custom keyboard shortcut that instantly toggles or sets track locks across all video tracks simultaneously, streamlining your editing workflow and eliminating the need to click each track header individually.

Step 1: Open the Keyboard Shortcut Manager

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open any project.
  2. In the top application menu, click on Settings.
  3. Select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... from the dropdown menu (or press Ctrl + Alt + , on default Linux/Windows installations).

Step 2: Locate the Track Locking Action

  1. In the search bar at the top of the Configure Keyboard Shortcuts window, type Lock All Video Tracks or Lock.
  2. Locate the specific command in the list:
    • Lock All Video Tracks: Locks every video track on your timeline simultaneously.
    • Unlock All Video Tracks: Releases locks on all video tracks.
    • Lock/Unlock All Video Tracks (or Toggle Lock All Video Tracks, depending on your Kdenlive version): Switches the lock state back and forth.

Step 3: Assign Your Custom Hotkey

  1. Click on the action you wish to configure to highlight it.
  2. Under the Shortcuts section at the bottom, select the Custom radio button.
  3. Click the button labeled None (or displaying the existing keybinding).
  4. Press the key combination you want to assign on your keyboard (for example, Shift + Alt + L or Ctrl + Shift + V).
  5. If the shortcut conflicts with an existing command, Kdenlive will notify you. Reassign the key or choose an alternate combination.

Step 4: Apply and Test the Shortcut

  1. Click OK to save the changes and close the settings dialog.
  2. Return to your timeline and press the configured shortcut.
  3. Check your track headers on the left of the timeline. The padlock icon across all video tracks should immediately close to indicate they are locked. Pressing your toggle or unlock hotkey will immediately restore them to an editable state.
