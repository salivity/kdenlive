Change Voice Pitch in Kdenlive with Pitch Scaler

This article provides a quick, step-by-step guide on how to use the "Pitch Scaler" filter in Kdenlive to adjust the pitch of an audio track. By using this specific effect, you can make a voice sound higher (like a chipmunk) or lower (like a deep giant) while keeping the video and audio playback speed completely normal.

Step 1: Add Your Clip to the Timeline

First, open Kdenlive and import your video or audio clip into the Project Bin. Drag and drop the clip onto your timeline. If your audio is embedded in a video clip, you can apply the effect directly to the video track, as Kdenlive will automatically target the audio portion.

Step 2: Locate the Pitch Scaler Effect

Navigate to the Effects tab (usually located in the bottom-left or top-left panel near the Project Bin). In the search bar of the Effects panel, type "Pitch Scaler". You will find it categorized under the Audio Effects or Audio Correction section.

Step 3: Apply the Effect to Your Clip

Click and hold the Pitch Scaler effect, then drag and drop it directly onto the audio or video clip in your timeline. Once applied, a red star icon or an effect badge will appear on the clip, indicating the filter is active.

Step 4: Adjust the Pitch Settings

With the clip selected on your timeline, look at the Properties or Effect/Composition Stack panel (usually on the top-right side of the interface). Here, you will see the controls for the Pitch Scaler:

  • Pitch: This is the main slider. The default value is usually set to 0 or 1.00 (normal pitch).
  • To raise the pitch (higher voice): Drag the slider to the right (positive values). This will make the voice sound higher without speeding up the video.
  • To lower the pitch (deeper voice): Drag the slider to the left (negative values). This will make the voice sound deeper without slowing down the video.

Step 5: Preview and Fine-Tune

Play the timeline to hear the changes in real-time. Fine-tune the slider until you achieve the desired vocal effect. Because the Pitch Scaler uses advanced audio resampling, the timing of your video and audio will remain perfectly synchronized.
