How to Change Default Transition Duration in Kdenlive

This article provides a quick guide on how to configure the default duration settings for transition overlaps and mixes in Kdenlive. By modifying these timeline preferences, you can streamline your editing workflow and ensure that newly added transitions automatically match your preferred frame length or timecode without requiring manual resizing on the timeline.

Step-by-Step Procedure to Adjust Transition Durations

1. Open the Configuration Menu

  • Launch Kdenlive.
  • On Windows and Linux, click on Settings in the top menu bar and select Configure Kdenlive...
  • On macOS, click on Kdenlive in the menu bar and select Preferences... (or press Cmd + ,).

2. Navigate to Timeline Settings

  • In the left-hand sidebar of the configuration window, click on the Timeline tab.

3. Adjust the Default Durations

  • Within the Timeline settings panel, locate the Default Durations section.
  • Find the field labeled Transitions (or Mixes / Compositions, depending on your Kdenlive version).
  • Enter your desired duration value using standard timecode format (HH:MM:SS:FF) or frame counts.
  • Optional: You can also adjust the default durations for other timeline elements here, such as Color clips, Images, or Audio / Video fades.

4. Save and Apply Settings

  • Click the Apply button at the bottom right of the window.
  • Click OK to close the configuration dialog.

Applying Transitions with the New Duration

Once configured, any new transition or mix applied to overlapping clips will automatically adhere to the new duration setting. You can apply transitions by:

  • Dragging a transition from the Compositions tab directly onto the overlap area between two tracks.
  • Using the Mix tool (keyboard shortcut U by default) when two adjacent clips on the same track touch or overlap.
