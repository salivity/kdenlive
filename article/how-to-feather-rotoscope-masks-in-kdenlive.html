How to Feather Rotoscope Masks in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to adjust the alpha feather radius on rotoscoped masks in Kdenlive to create smooth, blended edges. By modifying the feathering parameters within the Rotoscoping effect properties, you can eliminate harsh boundaries around your subject and integrate cutouts naturally into your project.

Step 1: Open the Rotoscoping Parameters

Select the clip containing your rotoscoped mask on the timeline. Navigate to the Effect/Composition Stack panel (usually located in the top-left or properties dock) to view the applied Rotoscoping effect controls.

Step 2: Locate the Feathering Settings

Scroll down past the keyframe controls in the effect panel to find the edge blending settings. You will see parameters labeled Feathering, Feather width, or Feather passes, depending on your Kdenlive version.

Step 3: Adjust the Feather Radius

Increase the Feather width slider to expand the alpha blur radius:

  • Low Values (1–5 px): Provide a subtle, anti-aliased edge suitable for crisp subjects.
  • Higher Values (10+ px): Create a broad, soft gradient transition, ideal for out-of-focus subjects or ambient lighting blends.

Step 4: Choose the Feather Direction

If your version provides feather direction options, select how the radius expands relative to your drawn vector path:

  • Both: Expands the soft edge equally inward and outward from the drawn spline.
  • In: Keeps the feathering strictly inside the perimeter of your mask.
  • Out: Expands the alpha gradient outward from the drawn path.

Step 5: Keyframe the Feathering (Optional)

If your subject changes focal distance or moves throughout the shot, adjust the feather width over time. Add keyframes in the Rotoscoping effect timeline and change the feather width values at specific points to maintain accurate depth of field.
