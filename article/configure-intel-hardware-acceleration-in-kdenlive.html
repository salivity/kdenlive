Configure Intel Hardware Acceleration in Kdenlive

Configuring hardware acceleration in Kdenlive for modern Intel processors leverages Intel Quick Sync Video (QSV) and VA-API to significantly reduce export times and improve timeline playback smoothness. This guide walks you through verifying your drivers, configuring playback decoding settings, and enabling hardware-accelerated rendering profiles inside Kdenlive.

1. Verify Intel GPU Drivers

Before configuring Kdenlive, ensure your system recognizes your modern Intel integrated or discrete GPU:

  • Windows: Install the latest Intel Graphics Drivers (supporting Quick Sync and DirectX 11/12).
  • Linux: Install the Intel media driver (intel-media-driver / iHD) and the VA-API tools (libva-utils or vainfo) via your package manager to verify VA-API support.

2. Enable Hardware Decoding for Timeline Playback

  1. Open Kdenlive.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Configure Kdenlive from the top menu.
  3. In the sidebar, click on Playback.
  4. Locate the Decoding or Hardware Accelerated Decoding section.
  5. Set the hardware decoding backend:
    • Linux: Select VAAPI (or Auto).
    • Windows: Select D3D11VA or DXVA2.
  6. Check the box to enable preview scaling (e.g., 720p or 360p) to combine hardware decoding with lower timeline resolution for optimal real-time editing performance.

3. Detect and Enable Hardware Encoding Profiles

  1. In the Configure Kdenlive window, click on Render (or Hardware Acceleration depending on your Kdenlive release).
  2. Look for the Hardware Acceleration subsection and click Check Hardware Acceleration or Detect.
  3. Kdenlive will run automated FFmpeg tests to scan for compatible codecs.
  4. Ensure the system detects Intel-compatible codecs:
    • h264_vaapi or hevc_vaapi (Linux)
    • h264_qsv or hevc_qsv (Windows / supported Linux configurations)

4. Exporting Using Intel Hardware Encoding

  1. Close the configuration window and click the Render button (or press Ctrl + Return).
  2. In the Render widget, expand the Hardware Accelerated or Hardware Encoding category.
  3. Select an Intel-supported profile:
    • Choose VAAPI Intel H264 or VAAPI Intel HEVC on Linux.
    • Choose MP4-H264/QSV or HEVC/QSV on Windows.
  4. Adjust your quality (CRF or Bitrate) parameters as needed.
  5. Click Render to File.
