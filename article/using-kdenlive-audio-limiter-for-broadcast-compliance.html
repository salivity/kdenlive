Using Kdenlive Audio Limiter for Broadcast Compliance

Achieving broadcast-standard audio requires strict control over peak volume levels to avoid distortion and comply with regulatory delivery specifications. This guide explains how to locate, apply, and configure the Audio Limiter effect in Kdenlive across your master track to prevent digital clipping, meet industry peak limits (such as EBU R128 or ATSC A/85 standards), and ensure consistent, professional sound output.

Broadcast Compliance Audio Basics

Broadcast regulations typically require true peak levels to remain below a specific ceiling to prevent distortion during transmission and playback across various consumer devices:

  • EBU R128 (Europe/International): Maximum True Peak of -1.0 dBFS (or -1.0 dBTP).
  • ATSC A/85 (North America): Maximum True Peak of -2.0 dBFS (or -2.0 dBTP).

An audio limiter automatically caps any signal that attempts to exceed your defined threshold without altering the dynamics of audio that sits safely below it.

Applying the Limiter to the Master Audio Track

For broadcast compliance, limiters should be applied to the entire mix rather than individual clips to catch unexpected peaks from overlapping audio layers.

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. In the Timeline, locate the Master track header at the bottom left of the tracks area.
  3. Open the Effects panel (go to Menu > Settings > Audio Layout or View > Effects if it is not visible).
  4. In the Effects search bar, type Limiter.
  5. Locate the Fast Lookahead Limiter (or standard Limiter) under the Audio Correction effects category.
  6. Drag and drop the effect directly onto the Master track header.

Configuring Limiter Parameters for Broadcast

Select the Master track to view the effect controls in the Effect/Composition Stack panel, then configure the following settings:

  • Limit (dB): Set this to your required broadcast ceiling (e.g., -1.0 dB for EBU R128 or -2.0 dB for ATSC A/85). This ensures no part of the audio mix exceeds this threshold.
  • Input Gain (dB): Adjust this only if the overall mix is too quiet before limiting. Increase it carefully so the limiter gently controls peaks without crushing the dynamic range.
  • Release Time (s): Set between 0.05 s (50 ms) and 0.1 s (100 ms). A fast release time prevents audible "pumping" artifacts while maintaining transparent limiting on sudden loud sounds.

Monitoring and Verification

  1. Open the Audio Mixer via View > Audio Mixer.
  2. Play through the loudest sections of your timeline (e.g., explosions, intense music, sudden dialogue spikes).
  3. Observe the Master channel meters. The peaks should strictly stay at or below the defined limit value.
  4. Export a test render and verify the rendered file's true peak levels in an audio analysis tool to confirm full compliance before final delivery.
