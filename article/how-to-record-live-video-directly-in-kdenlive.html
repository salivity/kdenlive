How to Record Live Video Directly in Kdenlive

Recording live video directly into Kdenlive streamlines your video editing workflow by eliminating the need for external recording software. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to configure Kdenlive's capture settings, set up your webcam or camcorder via a capture card, and record high-quality live footage straight into your project bin.

Step 1: Enable the Capture Monitor

Before configuring your device, you need to display the recording interface within Kdenlive.

  1. Open Kdenlive.
  2. In the top menu, go to View.
  3. Ensure that Screen Grab or Clip Monitor is checked.
  4. If using the Clip Monitor, click on the Record tab (often represented by a red circle icon) at the bottom of the monitor window to switch to the capture interface.

Step 2: Configure the Capture Plugin Settings

Kdenlive relies on underlying system drivers (like FFmpeg, Video4Linux2 on Linux, or DirectShow on Windows) to communicate with your camcorder or capture card.

  1. Go to Settings in the top menu and select Configure Kdenlive.
  2. In the configuration window, click on the Capture tab in the left-hand sidebar.
  3. You will see different capture methods. For live video from a camera or USB capture card, select the FFmpeg or V4L2 (Video4Linux) tab, depending on your operating system:
    • Linux users: Use the V4L2 tab for hardware webcams and camcorders.
    • Windows users: Use the FFmpeg tab configured to capture from your DirectShow video devices.

Step 3: Select Your Video and Audio Devices

Within the Capture settings page, you must tell Kdenlive which hardware device to use.

  1. Video Device: Click the dropdown menu next to "Device" or "Video Device" and select your connected camcorder, webcam, or capture card.
  2. Video Format: Choose your desired resolution and frame rate (e.g., 1920x1080 at 30fps) supported by your camera.
  3. Audio Device: Select your microphone or the audio input coming from your camcorder/capture card to ensure synchronized sound.
  4. Encoding Profile: Choose an export format for the live recording. H.264 / AAC (MP4) is highly recommended for a balance of high quality and low CPU usage during recording.
  5. Click Apply and then OK to save the settings.

Step 4: Record Live Video into Your Project

With the configuration complete, you are ready to start recording.

  1. Look at the Clip Monitor (switched to the Record tab). You should now see a live preview of your camcorder’s feed.
  2. Click the red Record button to start capturing.
  3. Once you are finished, click the Stop button.
  4. Kdenlive will automatically save the recorded video file to your designated capture folder and instantly add the clip to your Project Bin, ready to be dragged onto the timeline for editing.
