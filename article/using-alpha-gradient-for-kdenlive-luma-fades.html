Using Alpha Gradient for Kdenlive Luma Fades

The Alpha Gradient filter in Kdenlive is a specialized video effect used to generate linear, customizable alpha masks that drive custom luma fades and wipe transitions. By creating a progressive transparency gradient across a clip, this filter enables editors to control precisely how, where, and in what direction one video track dissolves or wipes into another without relying solely on static, pre-rendered luma wipe files.

The Role of Alpha Gradient in Luma Transitions

A standard luma fade relies on luminance (brightness) values—ranging from pure black to pure white—to determine the progression of opacity over time. The Alpha Gradient filter fulfills this by programmatically generating an adjustable grayscale ramp directly on the track's alpha channel.

Instead of an instant or uniform opacity change, the filter introduces a directional spread where the transition sweeps smoothly across the screen according to the defined gradient coordinates.

Key Functions and Parameters

When crafting custom luma fades, the Alpha Gradient filter provides several controls that dictate how the transition renders:

  • Position (Start and End Coordinates): Defines the geometric path of the fade. By adjusting the start and end points, you determine whether the wipe moves horizontally, vertically, or diagonally across the frame.
  • Tilt/Angle: Rotates the boundary of the gradient, allowing for dynamic angular transitions across the visual canvas.
  • Transition Softness: Regulates the width of the blur along the gradient line. A narrow width produces a sharp, defined wipe edge, whereas a wide spread creates a soft, gradual luma dissolve.
  • Inversion: Flips the gradient direction instantly, reversing whether the clip appears from left-to-right, right-to-left, or inside-out.

Practical Application

In a typical editing workflow, the Alpha Gradient filter is applied alongside keyframes or composition modes (such as Wipe or Composite and Transform). By keyframing the position or intensity of the gradient over the duration of a cut, the alpha values shift dynamically from fully hidden to fully visible, delivering smooth, mathematically precise custom wipes natively inside Kdenlive.
