How to Invert Luminance in Kdenlive

Inverting only the luminance channel of an asset in Kdenlive allows you to reverse the brightness values—turning highlights into shadows and shadows into highlights—while keeping the original colors and hues intact. This guide explains the quickest and most accurate way to invert luminance using the built-in Curves effect.

Step 1: Add Your Asset to the Timeline

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. Drag your video or image asset from the Project Bin onto a video track in the Timeline.
  3. Select the clip on the timeline so it is highlighted.

Step 2: Apply the Curves Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Use the search bar to find the Curves or Bézier Curves effect under the Color and Image Correction category.
  3. Double-click the effect or drag and drop it directly onto your selected clip.

Step 3: Set the Channel to Luma

  1. Go to the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the settings for the applied effect.
  2. Find the Channel dropdown menu (which defaults to RGB or All).
  3. Change the channel selection to Luma (or Y depending on your Kdenlive version).

Step 4: Invert the Curve

  1. Locate the diagonal line spanning from the bottom-left to the top-right in the curve graph.
  2. Click and drag the bottom-left point (representing black/shadows) all the way up to the top-left corner.
  3. Click and drag the top-right point (representing white/highlights) all the way down to the bottom-right corner.

The diagonal line is now reversed from top-left to bottom-right. The brightness values of your clip are inverted, while the original chrominance (color) information remains unaffected.
