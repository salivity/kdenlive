Add Dynamic Timecode Display in Kdenlive

Adding a visible, running timecode to your video is essential for editing reviews, synchronization, and professional client feedback. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step tutorial on how to embed a dynamic timecode display onto a video clip using Kdenlive, the popular free and open-source video editing software. You will learn which effect to apply, how to configure the dynamic text settings, and how to customize the appearance of the timecode on your timeline.

Step 1: Add Your Clip to the Timeline

Open Kdenlive and import your video clip into the Project Bin. Drag and drop the clip onto your target video track in the timeline.

Step 2: Apply the Dynamic Text Effect

To generate a running timecode, you will use Kdenlive's built-in text generator effect.

  1. Locate the Effects tab (usually found in the bottom-left or bottom-middle panel). If you do not see it, enable it by going to the top menu and selecting View > Effects.
  2. In the search bar of the Effects panel, type Dynamic Text.
  3. Drag the Dynamic Text effect from the list and drop it directly onto your video clip in the timeline.

Step 3: Configure the Timecode Display

Once the effect is applied, the Effect/Composition Stack panel (typically on the top right) will display the settings for the Dynamic Text effect.

  1. Locate the text input box within the effect settings. By default, it may show generic text or project frame numbers.
  2. Click the insert keyword button (often represented by a small arrow or "+" icon next to the text field) and select Timecode. Alternatively, you can manually type %timecode into the text box.
  3. The preview monitor will now display a frame-accurate, real-time running timecode that corresponds directly to the playback of your clip.

Step 4: Customize Appearance and Position

You can style and position the timecode to make it easily readable:

  • Font and Size: Choose a highly legible font (such as Arial or Courier) and adjust the size so it is visible without obstructing crucial video elements.
  • Colors: Set the text color to white and enable the background rectangle (usually black with slight transparency) to ensure the timecode remains readable against light and dark backgrounds.
  • Alignment and Position: Use the alignment buttons to center the text, or use the horizontal (X) and vertical (Y) offset sliders to position the timecode in a corner of the screen, such as the bottom-right or top-center.
