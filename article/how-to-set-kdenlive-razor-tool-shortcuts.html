How to Set Kdenlive Razor Tool Shortcuts

Customizing keyboard shortcuts in Kdenlive significantly speeds up your video editing workflow, especially for frequently used actions like splitting clips. This guide walks you through the exact steps required to locate, rebind, and save a custom keyboard shortcut for the Razor tool and related cut commands in Kdenlive.

Step 1: Open the Shortcut Configuration Menu

  1. Launch Kdenlive.
  2. In the top application menu bar, click on Settings.
  3. Select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... from the dropdown menu (or press Ctrl + Alt + , on your keyboard).

Step 2: Locate the Razor Tool Action

  1. In the configuration window that appears, locate the Search bar at the top.
  2. Type Razor into the search box.
  3. You will see the action listed, typically named Razor tool (defaulted to X).

Note: If you want to cut the clip directly at the playhead without switching tools, search for Cut Clip or Cut All Clips (often assigned to Shift + R by default).

Step 3: Assign a Custom Key

  1. Click on the Razor tool action to highlight it.
  2. In the lower part of the window, switch the radio button from Default to Custom.
  3. Click the button next to Custom (it will typically display None or the current custom key).
  4. Press the key or key combination you wish to use (such as C, S, or any preferred modifier combination).

Step 4: Handle Conflicts and Save

  1. If the key you pressed is already assigned to another command, a conflict dialog will appear. Choose whether to reassign the shortcut to the Razor tool or pick a different key.
  2. Click OK or Apply at the bottom right of the window to save your new settings.

Your new shortcut is immediately active. You can now press your assigned key on the timeline to switch directly to the Razor tool.
