How to Adjust Preview Scaling Resolution in Kdenlive

Editing high-resolution video files in Kdenlive can often cause lag, stuttering, and dropped frames during timeline playback. This article provides a straightforward, step-by-step guide on how to adjust the preview scaling resolution in Kdenlive to significantly improve your editing performance. By lowering the playback resolution within the editor, you can enjoy a smoother editing experience without affecting the quality of your final rendered video.

To adjust the preview scaling resolution in Kdenlive, follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Locate the Project Monitor

Open your project in Kdenlive. Look at the Project Monitor window, which is the video player interface usually located in the top-right quadrant of the default workspace where you preview your timeline.

Step 2: Access the Preview Scaling Menu

At the bottom of the Project Monitor window, you will see a toolbar containing playback controls. Look for the Preview Scaling button. This icon typically resembles a computer monitor with a dropdown arrow next to it, or it may display the current scaling setting (such as "1:1").

Step 3: Select a Lower Resolution

Click on the Preview Scaling button to open the dropdown menu. You will be presented with several resolution options. Select a lower resolution to reduce the strain on your computer's CPU and GPU:

  • Off (1:1): Displays the video at its native project resolution.
  • 720p: Scales the preview down to 1280x720.
  • 540p: Scales the preview down to 960x540.
  • 360p: Scales the preview down to 640x360 (recommended for older or lower-spec computers).
  • 270p: Scales the preview down to 480x270 for maximum performance.

Once you select a lower resolution (such as 360p), Kdenlive will instantly lower the playback quality in the Project Monitor. You should immediately notice smoother scrubbing and playback on your timeline.

Important Note on Final Render Quality

Changing the preview scaling resolution only affects what you see while editing inside the software. It does not degrade the quality of your source files, nor does it affect the final output. When you are ready to export your finished video, Kdenlive will automatically render the project using the original, full-resolution files at maximum quality.
