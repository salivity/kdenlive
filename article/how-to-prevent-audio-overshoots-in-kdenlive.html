How to Prevent Audio Overshoots in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to properly apply and configure the Audio Limiter effect in Kdenlive. By placing a limiter on your master audio track, you can manage dynamic peaks, avoid digital clipping, and guarantee zero digital overshoots when rendering your final project.

Step 1: Open the Master Audio Track

To catch all possible overshoots across multiple audio layers, the limiter must be applied globally to the output rather than to individual clips.

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. In the Timeline panel, look at the track headers on the left.
  3. Click on the Master track header (or open the Audio Mixer tab and select the Master channel).
  4. Open the Effects tab/panel to view the effect stack for the Master track.

Step 2: Add the Limiter Effect

  1. Go to the Effects library.
  2. Type Limiter into the search bar.
  3. Locate the Limiter effect (such as the standard LADSPA/FFmpeg Limiter or Fast Lookahead Limiter).
  4. Drag and drop the effect directly onto the Master track effect stack.

Step 3: Configure Effect Parameters to Prevent Overshoots

Digital audio rendering—especially when compressing to formats like AAC or MP3—can introduce inter-sample peaks that push audio above 0 dBFS if your ceiling is set too close to the maximum.

  1. Set the Limit / Ceiling (dB): Adjust the maximum output ceiling to -1.0 dB (or at minimum -0.5 dB). Setting this slightly below 0.0 dB ensures safety against inter-sample overshoots during lossy compression upon export.
  2. Set the Input Gain: Keep the input gain at 0.0 dB unless your overall mix is too quiet. If you increase input gain, do so moderately to avoid excessive squashing of the dynamic range.
  3. Release Time: Set the release time between 50 ms and 100 ms for a natural sound that quickly recovers after sudden volume spikes without noticeable "pumping."
  4. Lookahead (if available): Ensure lookahead is enabled (typically 5–10 ms) so the limiter anticipates transient spikes before they reach the output buffer.

Step 4: Verify Levels and Export

  1. Play through the loudest sections of your timeline while monitoring the Audio Mixer master meters.
  2. Ensure that the master output peaks never exceed your defined ceiling (e.g., -1.0 dB).
  3. Open the Render dialog (Ctrl + Return or Menu > Project > Render).
  4. Select your desired output profile and render the project. The master limiter will enforce the ceiling throughout the entire render process, guaranteeing an export free of digital clipping.
