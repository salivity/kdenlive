How to Style and Format Subtitles in Kdenlive

This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to customize, style, and format subtitles directly within the Kdenlive video editor. You will learn how to enable the subtitle track, edit text, and use the subtitle styling options to adjust fonts, sizes, colors, and backgrounds to match your video's aesthetic.

Step 1: Enable the Subtitle Track

Before you can style subtitles, you must add them to your timeline.

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. Click on the Subtitle icon in the timeline toolbar (located just above the timeline tracks), or go to the top menu and select Project > Subtitles > Add Subtitle.
  3. A dedicated subtitle track will appear at the very top of your timeline.

Step 2: Add and Edit Subtitle Text

  1. Double-click on the subtitle track at the specific timestamp where you want the subtitle to appear.
  2. A subtitle block will be created. Double-click this block to open the Subtitles panel (usually on the right or left side of your screen).
  3. Type your text directly into the text field in the Subtitles panel.

Step 3: Access the Subtitle Style Settings

Kdenlive applies styling globally to all subtitles on the track to ensure visual consistency.

  1. In the Subtitles panel, click on the Style button (represented by a gear or brush icon, depending on your Kdenlive version).
  2. This opens the Subtitle Styling window, where you can customize the visual appearance of your text.

Step 4: Customize the Font, Color, and Background

Within the styling window, you can adjust the following parameters:

  • Font and Size: Choose your preferred font family from the dropdown menu and set the font size. Bold and italic options are also available here.
  • Text Color: Click the color picker to change the text color. High-contrast colors like white or yellow are recommended for readability.
  • Shadow and Outline: To make your text stand out against busy backgrounds, enable the Outline (stroke) or Shadow options. You can adjust the color, thickness, and blur of the outline or shadow.
  • Background Box: If you prefer a solid or semi-transparent background behind your text, enable the Background option. Adjust the color and opacity (alpha channel) to ensure the background is not too distracting.

Step 5: Adjust Alignment and Margins

To position your subtitles correctly on the screen:

  1. Locate the Alignment settings in the styling window.
  2. Choose whether you want the text aligned to the left, center, or right.
  3. Set the vertical and horizontal margins (usually offset from the bottom of the screen) to ensure the text does not get cut off by safe-area boundaries.

Once you are satisfied with the look, click OK or Apply. The changes will instantly apply to every subtitle block on your timeline.
