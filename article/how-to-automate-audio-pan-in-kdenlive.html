How to Automate Audio Pan in Kdenlive

Automating an audio pan sweep in Kdenlive allows you to smoothly transition sound from the left speaker to the right speaker across a clip's duration. This guide details the exact steps required to apply Kdenlive’s audio panning effect, set keyframes, and configure the spatial movement to achieve a seamless dynamic stereo sweep.

Step 1: Place Your Audio on the Timeline

  1. Import your audio or video file into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag and drop the clip onto an audio or video track in the timeline.

Step 2: Add the Pan Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (typically located in the workspace panels or accessible via Menu > View > Effects).
  2. Search for Pan or browse to Audio Correction / Audio Channels.
  3. Drag the Pan (or Pan (stereo to stereo) / Balance) effect directly onto your target clip on the timeline.

Step 3: Open Effect Stack and Enable Keyframes

  1. Click the clip on the timeline to open the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. In the Pan effect controls, ensure keyframing is active (the timeline ruler with a keyframe bar appears beneath the effect parameters).

Step 4: Configure the Starting Keyframe (Left Pan)

  1. Move the timeline playhead to the exact starting point where you want the pan sweep to begin.
  2. In the Effect Stack, add a keyframe by clicking the Add keyframe icon (plus icon) if one is not already present at the playhead.
  3. Adjust the Pan slider all the way to the Left (minimum value / 0%).

Step 5: Configure the Ending Keyframe (Right Pan)

  1. Move the timeline playhead forward to the point where the sweep should finish.
  2. Add another keyframe at this new position.
  3. Adjust the Pan slider completely to the Right (maximum value / 100%).

Step 6: Preview and Fine-Tune

  1. Play back the timeline to listen to the audio transition smoothly from the left channel to the right channel over the designated timeframe.
  2. To change the speed of the sweep, drag the keyframe markers closer together for a faster transition or farther apart for a slower sweep.
