How to Use Screen Blend Mode in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to configure track composite modes to use the "Screen" blend mode in Kdenlive for seamless transparent overlay effects. By using the Screen blending mode, you can easily remove black backgrounds from stock footage—such as light leaks, dust particles, fire, or lens flares—and blend them naturally over your primary video.

Step 1: Arrange Clips on the Timeline

  1. Place your base background video onto a lower video track (for example, V1).
  2. Place the overlay video (the footage containing the effect on a pure black background) on the track directly above your base video (for example, V2).
  3. Align the overlay clip to the exact timeframe where you want the effect to appear.

Step 2: Add a Composition to the Overlay

  1. Hover your cursor over the bottom-left or bottom-right corner of the overlay clip on V2 until the pointer changes to a composition icon, then click to add a composition.
  2. Alternatively, right-click the overlay clip on V2, navigate to Insert Composition, and select Composite and Transform (or Cairo Blend).
  3. Stretch the composition box across the entire length of the overlay clip so the blending effect applies throughout the duration of the clip.

Step 3: Change the Blend Mode to Screen

  1. Click on the added composition block on the timeline to open its settings in the Properties panel.
  2. Locate the Composition or Blend Mode drop-down menu (often set to Over or Normal by default).
  3. Select Screen from the list of available blending modes.
  4. The black background on the V2 clip will instantly become transparent, leaving only the bright elements superimposed over the V1 footage.

Step 4: Fine-Tune the Overlay

  • Opacity: Adjust the opacity slider in the Properties panel if the overlay effect is too intense.
  • Transform: Adjust the position, size, or rotation within the Composite and Transform settings to properly align the overlay element with your scene.
