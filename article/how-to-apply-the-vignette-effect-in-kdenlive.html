How to Apply the Vignette Effect in Kdenlive

Applying a vignette in Kdenlive is an effective way to darken or soften the edges of your footage, naturally guiding the audience's gaze directly toward the center of the frame. This guide outlines the exact, step-by-step process required to locate, add, and adjust the Vignette effect in Kdenlive to achieve a focused, cinematic composition.

1. Place Your Footage on the Timeline

Open your Kdenlive project and ensure your target video or image clip is placed on a video track in the timeline. Click on the clip to select it so that it is highlighted.

2. Locate the Vignette Effect

Open the Effects panel (usually located in the bottom-left or tabbed alongside the Project Bin). In the search bar at the top of the Effects panel, type Vignette. The effect will appear under the image adjustment/stylize categories.

3. Add the Effect to Your Clip

Apply the effect using either of the following methods:

  • Drag and drop the Vignette effect directly onto the selected clip on the timeline.
  • Double-click the Vignette effect while the target clip is active.

4. Adjust the Vignette Parameters

Once applied, open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to fine-tune the effect. Modify the available controls to direct focus toward the center:

  • Radius: Controls the size of the clear center area. Reduce the radius to bring the edge darkening closer to the center, or expand it to keep the darkening strictly at the outer corners.
  • Smoothness / Softness: Determines how gradually the dark edges blend into the bright center. Increase smoothness for a subtle, natural gradient, or decrease it for a sharper border.
  • Center X / Center Y: Adjusts the focal point of the vignette if your subject is slightly off-center.
  • Opacity / Intensity: Regulates the darkness level of the vignette overlay. Keep this moderate to maintain a realistic look.

5. Preview and Render

Play back your timeline in the Clip/Project Monitor to check the result. Fine-tune the radius and smoothness until your subject in the center stands out naturally, then proceed with editing or rendering your project.
