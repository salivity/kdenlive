Adjust Mask Feather Radius in Kdenlive Rotoscoping

Adjusting the feather radius on custom bezier masks in Kdenlive softens the harsh edges of a cut, allowing isolated elements to blend smoothly with other video layers. This guide provides a direct, step-by-step walkthrough on how to use the built-in feathering and pass settings within Kdenlive’s Rotoscoping effect to control edge softness and transition blur on any custom mask shape.

1. Apply the Rotoscoping Effect

  1. Select the video or image clip on your timeline that requires masking.
  2. Open the Effects tab and search for Rotoscoping.
  3. Drag and drop the Rotoscoping effect onto your chosen clip.

2. Draw Your Bezier Mask

  1. Move the playhead over the clip and click inside the Project Monitor to place your bezier control points.
  2. Click to create sharp corner points, or click and drag to generate curved bezier handles.
  3. Close the mask by clicking directly on the first control point.

3. Adjust the Feather Width (Radius)

Once the mask shape is closed, open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the Rotoscoping parameters:

  • Feather width: Locate the Feather width slider. Increasing this value expands the radius of the edge blur, softening the transition between the masked area and the background.
  • Feather passes: Increase the Feather passes value (e.g., from 1 up to 5 or more) to improve the smoothness and gradient quality of the blur. Higher pass values eliminate banding along the feathered edge.

4. Configure Feather Direction and Mode

  • Feathering Type / Direction: Set whether the feather blurs inward (In), outward (Out), or symmetrically on both sides of the bezier line (Both), depending on whether you need to preserve the inner details of the subject.
  • Mode / Alpha Operation: Choose how the mask interacts with the alpha channel (such as Write on clear, Add, or Subtract) to ensure the feathered cut accurately reveals or hides underlying timeline tracks.
