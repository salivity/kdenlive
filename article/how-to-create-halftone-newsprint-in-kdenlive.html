How to Create Halftone Newsprint in Kdenlive

This guide provides a straightforward tutorial on how to use Kdenlive's built-in Halftone filter to achieve a vintage printed newspaper look. By combining the halftone screen pattern with basic color grading and contrast adjustments, you can quickly transform modern digital video into stylized, retro print media.

Step 1: Add Your Footage to the Timeline

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open your project.
  2. Drag your video or image asset from the Project Bin onto a video track in the Timeline.

Step 2: Apply the Halftone Filter

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Type Halftone in the search bar.
  3. Drag the Halftone effect from the list directly onto your clip in the timeline, or onto the clip's entry in the Effect/Composition Stack.

Step 3: Adjust the Halftone Parameters

Select the clip to open the Effect/Composition Stack panel and fine-tune the settings to match a newsprint aesthetic:

  • Pattern / Matrix Size: Adjust the dot size. Smaller values create fine newspaper print, while larger values make the dots visibly stylized.
  • Frequency / Scale: Controls how tightly packed the dot matrix is across the frame.
  • Angle: Rotate the dot screen pattern slightly (e.g., 15° to 45°) to mimic authentic offset printing angles.
  • Intensity / Blend: Set the blend mode or intensity to ensure the dots properly replace standard pixel shading.

Step 4: Convert to Monochrome or Aged Newsprint Color

Authentic newspaper imagery requires high contrast and desaturated or sepia tones.

  1. In the Effects panel, search for and apply the Color Correction or Lift/Gamma/Gain effect above or below the Halftone filter.
  2. Reduce the Saturation to 0 for a standard black-and-white print look.
  3. If simulating aged or yellowed paper, add a slight yellow/orange tint to the highlights and mids.
  4. Apply the Levels or Curves effect to crush the blacks and boost the whites, increasing the contrast so the halftone dot pattern becomes sharp and distinct.

Step 5: Add Paper Texture (Optional)

To complete the vintage look:

  1. Place a paper texture image on a video track directly above your halftone clip.
  2. Apply a Composition (such as Multiply or Overlay) between the paper texture and the video track.
  3. Lower the opacity of the texture layer until the halftone dots blend naturally with the paper background.
