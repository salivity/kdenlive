Kdenlive Zoom Timeline to Fit Project

When editing video in Kdenlive, managing your timeline view is essential for maintaining an efficient workflow. This article explains the fastest methods to instantly zoom your Kdenlive timeline so that your entire project duration fits perfectly within the visible window, using keyboard shortcuts, interface buttons, and menu navigation.

The Keyboard Shortcut (Fastest Method)

The absolute quickest way to fit your entire project into the timeline window is by using a keyboard shortcut.

  • Press Ctrl + Page Down on your keyboard.

This single keystroke instantly adjusts the zoom level so that every clip, from the very beginning to the final frame of your project, is visible on your screen without the need for horizontal scrolling.

The "Zoom to Fit" Icon

If you prefer using your mouse, Kdenlive provides a dedicated button in the user interface.

  1. Look at the bottom-right corner of the Kdenlive window, right next to the timeline zoom slider.
  2. Click the Zoom to Fit icon (which looks like a magnifying glass with horizontal arrows, or a small fit-to-page symbol).
  3. The timeline will immediately scale to show the entire duration of your project.

The Menu Bar Option

You can also access this function through the main application menu.

  1. Click on View in the top menu bar.
  2. Hover over Zoom.
  3. Click on Zoom to Fit.

Using any of these three methods will save you from manually dragging the zoom slider, allowing you to quickly get a bird's-eye view of your entire editing project.
