Create Room Reverb Acoustics in Kdenlive

This guide details the process of applying and fine-tuning the Audio Reverb effect in Kdenlive to create intimate room acoustic profiles. By adjusting key parameters such as room size, damping, and the wet/dry balance, you can simulate realistic, close-quarters environments—such as small offices, bedrooms, or podcast studios—to give dialogue and sound effects natural depth without sounding washed out.

Step 1: Add Your Audio to the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your audio or video files into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag the clip onto an audio track on the timeline.
  3. Select the clip on the timeline to ensure it is active.

Step 2: Locate and Apply the Reverb Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Navigate to the Audio Correction or Audio Effects category.
  3. Search for Reverb (or Freeverb, depending on your installed LADSPA/audio plugins).
  4. Drag and drop the effect directly onto your selected audio clip in the timeline, or double-click the effect while the clip is selected.

Step 3: Configure Parameters for an Intimate Room Profile

Select the clip and open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to adjust the reverb properties. Use the following baseline settings to achieve a close, realistic room acoustic:

  • Room Size: Set between 0.1 and 0.3 (or 10% to 30%). Small values replicate compact spaces with minimal reflection delays, avoiding cavernous echoes.
  • Damping: Set to a moderate-to-high value between 0.5 and 0.8 (or 50% to 80%). Higher damping rolls off high-frequency reflections, simulating acoustic absorption from carpets, curtains, and furniture.
  • Wet Level / Mix: Set between 0.05 and 0.15 (or 5% to 15%). Keeping the wet signal subtle ensures the audio maintains clarity and intimacy.
  • Dry Level: Keep at 1.0 (or 100% / 0 dB) to ensure the original recording remains prominent and clear.
  • Width (if available): Set to a moderate or narrow level (around 0.3 to 0.5) to keep the reflections centered and consistent with a small physical space.

Step 4: Preview and Fine-Tune

  1. Play back the timeline section around the clip to evaluate the acoustic blend.
  2. Toggle the effect on and off using the effect's power icon to compare the dry original with the processed audio.
  3. Make micro-adjustments to the Wet Level until the reverb is felt as natural space rather than an obvious effect.
