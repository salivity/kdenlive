How to Share Assets Across Kdenlive Projects

Managing recurring assets like intros, transitions, lower thirds, and audio clips across different video editing projects can be tedious. Kdenlive solves this problem with its built-in "Library" widget, which allows editors to save, organize, and reuse timeline segments and project clips seamlessly across multiple project files. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to enable, configure, and utilize the Kdenlive Library to streamline your editing workflow and maintain brand consistency across all your videos.

Step 1: Enable the Library Panel

Before you can use the Library, you must ensure the panel is visible in your Kdenlive interface.

  1. Open Kdenlive.
  2. In the top menu, click on View.
  3. Locate and check the box next to Library.
  4. The Library panel will now appear in your workspace, usually nested as a tab near the Project Bin or the Effects panel.

Step 2: Add Assets to the Library

The Library folder can store individual clips, audio files, complex timeline selections, or entire sequences.

  • To copy from the Project Bin: Click and drag any video, audio, or graphic clip from your Project Bin directly into the Library panel.
  • To copy from the Timeline: Highlight a clip or a group of clips on your timeline. Copy them (Ctrl+C), then click inside the Library panel and paste them (Ctrl+V). Alternatively, you can select the timeline clips, right-click, and choose Add Timeline Selection to Library.
  • Name the Asset: Kdenlive will prompt you to name the expanded asset. Choose a descriptive name (e.g., "Standard Intro 2024") and click OK. The asset is saved as a .mlt XML file in your system's local Kdenlive directory, making it permanently accessible.

Step 3: Reuse Assets in a New Project

Once an asset is stored in your Library, it remains there regardless of what project you open or close.

  1. Open or start a new Kdenlive project.
  2. Go to the Library panel.
  3. Locate the asset you want to use.
  4. Drag and drop the asset from the Library panel directly into your new Project Bin or directly onto your Timeline.
  5. If the asset contains multiple tracks or complex cuts, Kdenlive will import it as a grouped clip or expanded sequence, preserving all your original transitions, cuts, and effects.

Step 4: Organize and Manage Library Files

To keep your workspace clean as your asset collection grows, you can organize the Library folder:

  • Create Folders: Right-click inside the Library panel and select Create Folder to categorize your assets (e.g., "Intros," "Lower Thirds," "Sound Effects"). You can drag and drop your saved assets into these folders.
  • Delete Assets: If you no longer need an asset, right-click it in the Library panel and select Delete. This removes it from your library but will not affect projects where the asset has already been imported and rendered.
