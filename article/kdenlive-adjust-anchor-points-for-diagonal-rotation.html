Kdenlive: Adjust Anchor Points for Diagonal Rotation

This guide explains how to adjust transform anchor points and animate diagonal rotations in Kdenlive. By modifying the rotation center offset and setting keyframes with the Transform effect, you can create smooth, off-center diagonal spin and tilt animations for your video clips or graphic elements.

Step 1: Apply the Transform Effect

  1. Place your video clip, image, or title graphic onto the Kdenlive timeline.
  2. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  3. Search for Transform in the search bar.
  4. Drag and drop the Transform effect onto your timeline clip.

Step 2: Set the Anchor Point (Rotation Center)

By default, Kdenlive rotates elements around their exact center point (50% X, 50% Y). To create an off-axis or diagonal rotation, shift the center:

  1. Select the clip on the timeline to open its effect parameters in the Effect / Composition Stack.
  2. Locate the Rotation Center X and Rotation Center Y (or Anchor / Offset controls, depending on your Kdenlive version).
  3. Change the coordinates to define your custom pivot point:
    • Setting the anchor to a corner (e.g., top-left or bottom-right) allows the element to swing along a diagonal path.
    • You can also drag the center crosshair directly in the Clip Monitor overlay to position the pivot visually.

Step 3: Animate the Diagonal Rotation with Keyframes

  1. Move the timeline playhead to the starting point of your animation.
  2. In the Transform effect parameters, click Add Keyframe.
  3. Set your initial rotation angle (e.g., Rotate: 0°).
  4. To tilt the rotation diagonally:
    • Adjust the Rotate X and Rotate Y values if using 3D transformation modes, or combine standard Rotate (Z-axis) with simultaneous diagonal movement along the X and Y position coordinates.
  5. Move the playhead forward to where the animation should conclude.
  6. Add a new keyframe and enter the final rotation value (e.g., 45°, 90°, or 360°).
  7. Adjust the final X and Y position coordinates if you want the object to travel diagonally across the screen while rotating.

Step 4: Fine-Tune Interpolation

  1. Right-click the keyframes in the Transform effect timeline.
  2. Switch the keyframe interpolation from Linear to Smooth to prevent abrupt starts and stops.
  3. Preview the playback in the Project Monitor to confirm the diagonal trajectory and rotation speed.
