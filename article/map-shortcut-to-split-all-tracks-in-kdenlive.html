Map Shortcut to Split All Tracks in Kdenlive

Customizing keyboard shortcuts in Kdenlive significantly accelerates your video editing workflow. This guide explains how to map a dedicated keyboard shortcut to split every unlocked audio and video track simultaneously at the current timeline playhead position, allowing you to slice your entire timeline without cutting individual clips manually.

Step 1: Open the Shortcut Configuration Menu

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open your project.
  2. In the top application menu bar, click on Settings.
  3. Select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... (or press the default shortcut Ctrl + Alt + , depending on your operating system).

Step 2: Locate the Multi-Track Split Command

  1. In the search bar at the top of the shortcut settings window, type Razor all or Cut all.
  2. Locate the command labeled Razor all tracks or Cut All Clips in the action list.

Step 3: Assign Your Custom Shortcut

  1. Click on the action to select it.
  2. Select the Custom radio button in the shortcut details pane.
  3. Click on the button next to "Custom" (which usually shows "None" or an existing keybind).
  4. Press the key combination you wish to use on your keyboard (for example, Shift + X or Ctrl + Shift + R).
  5. If the shortcut conflicts with an existing command, Kdenlive will prompt you to reassign it or choose a different combination.
  6. Click OK to apply and save your changes.

Step 4: Test the Shortcut on the Timeline

Position the timeline playhead across multiple video and audio clips. Press your newly assigned shortcut. Kdenlive will instantly create a cut across all tracks at that exact frame. Note that any tracks marked with the lock icon will remain intact and will not be cut.
