Adjust Low Frequency Boost in Kdenlive 3-Band EQ

This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on adjusting the low-frequency boost using the 3-Band Equalizer audio effect in Kdenlive. By modifying this parameter, you can easily increase the bass presence in your audio tracks or cut out unwanted low-end rumble directly from your timeline.

Step 1: Select Your Audio Clip

Open your Kdenlive project and click on the audio clip—or video clip containing audio—on the timeline that you want to adjust.

Step 2: Add the 3-Band Equalizer Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (typically located in the bottom-left or top-left panel depending on your workspace layout).
  2. Use the search bar in the Effects panel to search for 3-Band Equalizer (found under the Audio Correction category).
  3. Drag and drop the 3-Band Equalizer effect onto your selected timeline clip, or double-click the effect while the clip is selected.

Step 3: Open the Effect/Composition Stack

Navigate to the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the properties of the applied equalizer.

Step 4: Adjust the Low Gain Parameter

Inside the 3-Band Equalizer controls, locate the Low (or Low Gain) slider:

  • To Boost Low Frequencies: Drag the slider to the right or enter a positive value. This amplifies bass elements such as deep voices, kick drums, or basslines.
  • To Reduce Low Frequencies: Drag the slider to the left or enter a negative value. This helps eliminate low-frequency background noise, wind rumble, or excessive boominess.

Step 5: Preview and Fine-Tune

Play back the timeline using the Spacebar to monitor the audio adjustments in real time. Tweak the slider until the low end balances naturally with the mid and high frequencies.
