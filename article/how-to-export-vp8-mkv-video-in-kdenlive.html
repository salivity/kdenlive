How to Export VP8 MKV Video in Kdenlive

Exporting a video project using the open-source VP8 video codec wrapped in a Matroska (MKV) container in Kdenlive is a straightforward process achieved through the Render settings. This guide outlines the exact steps to locate the rendering dialog, configure or create a custom preset with the appropriate codec parameters, set output paths, and execute the final export.

Step 1: Open the Render Dialog

  1. Open your finished project on the Kdenlive timeline.
  2. Click the Render button in the main top toolbar, or use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Enter (Command + Return on macOS), or navigate to Project > Render from the top menu.

Step 2: Configure the VP8 and MKV Preset

While Kdenlive includes default WebM (VP8/VP9) presets, exporting specifically to an .mkv extension with VP8 may require adjusting a profile or creating a custom one:

  1. In the Render dialog, click the Create New Preset icon (the small "+" or document icon near the presets list).
  2. Set the Group Name to Custom or Generic.
  3. Enter a Preset Name, such as VP8 MKV.
  4. Set the Extension field to mkv.
  5. In the Parameters box, enter the FFmpeg rendering arguments: 
    f=matroska vcodec=libvpx acodec=libvorbis vb=%bitrate+'k' ab=%audiobitrate+'k'
    (Note: You can replace libvorbis with libopus if you prefer Opus audio).
  6. Click OK to save the preset.

Step 3: Select Destination and Adjust Settings

  1. Highlight your newly created VP8 MKV preset in the list.
  2. In the Output file field at the top, click the folder icon to select the save destination and name your exported .mkv file.
  3. Choose your render range:
    • Full Project: Exports the entire timeline.
    • Selected Zone: Exports only the designated in/out range.
  4. Under the Quality or Speed sliders, adjust the encoding speed and target bitrate according to your requirements. Lower speed presets typically yield better compression efficiency.

Step 4: Render the Video

  1. Click the Render to File button at the bottom of the dialog.
  2. Monitor the progress in the Job Queue section. Once the progress bar reaches 100%, your VP8-encoded MKV video is ready in your chosen destination directory.
