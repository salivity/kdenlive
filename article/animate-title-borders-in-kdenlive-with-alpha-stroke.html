Animate Title Borders in Kdenlive with Alpha Stroke

This guide explains how to create dynamic, animated outlines around transparent text and graphics in Kdenlive using the Alpha Stroke effect. By applying this effect to transparent title clips and animating its parameters with keyframes, you can produce glowing, expanding, or tracing border animations that enhance your video projects.

Step 1: Create a Transparent Title Clip

  1. In the Project Bin, click the Add Clip dropdown and select Create Title Clip.
  2. Design your text or vector shape in the Title Editor.
  3. Ensure the background color is set to fully transparent (alpha value at 0).
  4. Click Create Clip and drag your new title onto an upper video track in your timeline.

Step 2: Apply the Alpha Stroke Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab in Kdenlive.
  2. Search for Alpha Stroke (located under the Alpha, Mask and Keying category).
  3. Drag and drop the Alpha Stroke effect onto your title clip in the timeline.

Step 3: Configure the Stroke Properties

  1. Select the title clip to display its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack.
  2. Choose your desired border color by clicking the color picker in the effect settings.
  3. Adjust the Thickness (or Size) parameter to define the baseline width of the border around your transparent pixels.

Step 4: Animate the Border Using Keyframes

  1. In the Effect Stack, click the clock or keyframe icon next to the Thickness or Spread parameter to enable keyframing.
  2. Move the playhead to the beginning of the clip and set the thickness value to 0 (or your desired starting point).
  3. Move the playhead forward in time (e.g., 1 to 2 seconds) and increase the thickness value to create an expanding border effect.
  4. To create a continuous pulsing effect, add alternating keyframes between low and high thickness values throughout the duration of the clip.
  5. To create an outline reveal, adjust the offset or combine the effect with an animated mask.

Step 5: Fine-Tune and Preview

  1. Adjust the Softness parameter if you prefer a blurred, neon-glow border rather than a sharp edge.
  2. Preview your animation in the Clip or Project Monitor to ensure smooth keyframe transitions.
