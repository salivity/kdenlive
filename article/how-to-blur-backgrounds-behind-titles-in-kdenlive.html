How to Blur Backgrounds Behind Titles in Kdenlive

Adding a Gaussian Blur to your background footage is an effective way to improve text legibility and create a professional title screen in Kdenlive. This process involves separating your title and background footage across multiple video tracks, applying the blur effect directly to the underlying media, and adjusting the blur intensity to keep your text prominent and easy to read.

Step 1: Arrange Tracks on the Timeline

  1. Ensure your timeline has at least two active video tracks (labeled V1 and V2).
  2. Place your background video or image footage on the lower track (V1).
  3. Right-click in the Project Bin area, select Add Title Clip, enter your title text, customize its font and size, and click Create Title.
  4. Drag the newly created Title Clip onto the upper track (V2), aligning it directly above the section of background video where you want the text to appear.

Step 2: Split the Background (Optional)

If you only want the background to be blurred while the title is on screen:

  1. Move the timeline playhead to the start of the title clip on track V2.
  2. Select the razor tool or press Shift + R to cut the background clip on track V1.
  3. Move the playhead to the end of the title clip and cut the background clip again.

Step 3: Apply the Gaussian Blur Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Use the search bar in the Effects panel and type Gaussian Blur.
  3. Drag the Gaussian Blur effect from the list directly onto the background clip on track V1 (or onto the split segment beneath your title).

Step 4: Adjust the Blur Properties

  1. Click on the background clip on track V1 to open its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Locate the Gaussian Blur parameters.
  3. Adjust the Radius or Kernel size slider to increase or decrease the intensity of the blur.
  4. Fine-tune the blur level until your background details are obscured sufficiently, ensuring the title text on track V2 stands out clearly.

Step 5: Animate the Blur with Keyframes (Optional)

To create a smooth transition from a clear background to a blurred background:

  1. In the Gaussian Blur settings within the Effect Stack, enable keyframes by clicking the clock/keyframe icon.
  2. Set the initial keyframe at the start with the blur radius set to 0.
  3. Move a few frames forward and add a second keyframe with your desired blur level to create a soft fade-in effect.
