Configure True Peak vs RMS Audio Meters in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to configure the timeline audio level meters in Kdenlive to switch between True Peak and RMS (Root Mean Square) monitoring. Understanding and setting the appropriate meter type ensures accurate audio monitoring, helping you prevent digital clipping while maintaining balanced average loudness across your video projects.

Step-by-Step Configuration

  1. Open the Audio Mixer Panel
    Ensure the Audio Mixer or Master Level meter is visible in your workspace. If it is hidden, go to the top menu and select View > Audio Mixer.

  2. Access Meter Settings
    Locate the level meters next to your timeline tracks or inside the Audio Mixer panel. Right-click directly on the meter bars, or click the Options (three vertical dots or hamburger menu) icon located at the top-right corner of the Audio Mixer tab.

  3. Select the Metering Mode
    From the context menu, find the meter display or measurement mode options. Choose between:

    • True Peak: Displays instantaneous inter-sample peak levels to detect digital clipping and potential distortion after digital-to-analog conversion.
    • RMS (Root Mean Square): Displays the average power of the audio signal over time, providing a better representation of human-perceived loudness.

  4. Alternative Method via Preferences
    If the option is not accessible directly from the context menu in your version:

    • Go to Settings > Configure Kdenlive... in the top menu.
    • Navigate to the Playback or Audio section.
    • Locate the Audio Metering / Peak Program Meter (PPM) settings.
    • Select your preferred standard (Peak / True Peak / RMS / IEC) from the dropdown menu.
    • Click Apply and then OK.

  5. Verify the Playback Response
    Play your project timeline using the Spacebar. The meter will now update dynamically according to your chosen measurement mode, allowing you to master audio levels effectively.
