How to Adjust Stereo Balance in Kdenlive

Adjusting the stereo balance of individual audio clips in Kdenlive allows you to control how sound is distributed between the left and right audio channels. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on applying and configuring the "Pan" effect to balance audio or create dynamic panning transitions for specific clips on your timeline.

1. Add Your Audio Clip to the Timeline

Drag and drop your audio clip (or video clip containing audio) onto an audio track in the Kdenlive timeline. Select the specific clip you want to adjust by clicking on it.

2. Locate the Pan Effect

Open the Effects panel in Kdenlive (if it is not visible, enable it via View > Effects). In the search bar at the top of the Effects panel, type Pan or navigate to the Audio Correction / Audio Channels category.

3. Apply the Effect to the Clip

Drag the Pan effect directly onto the selected audio clip on your timeline. Alternatively, with the clip selected, double-click the Pan effect in the effects library.

4. Adjust the Stereo Position

With the clip selected, look at the Effect/Composition Stack panel:

  • Center Balance: By default, the slider sits in the center (0.50 or 0, depending on the Kdenlive version), distributing audio equally to both speakers.
  • Pan Left: Drag the slider toward the left to shift the audio output primarily or entirely to the left channel.
  • Pan Right: Drag the slider toward the right to shift the audio output to the right channel.

5. (Optional) Create Dynamic Panning with Keyframes

If you want the audio to move from one side to the other over time:

  1. Click the clock icon next to the Pan parameter in the Effect Stack to enable keyframes.
  2. Place the playhead at the start point and set your initial pan value.
  3. Move the playhead forward in the clip and adjust the pan slider to a new value to automatically generate a keyframe.
  4. Kdenlive will smoothly interpolate the balance between these two points.

6. Preview and Verify

Play back the timeline while wearing headphones or using stereo monitors to ensure the audio balance is placed accurately within the stereo field.
