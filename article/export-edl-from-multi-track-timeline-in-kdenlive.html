Export EDL from Multi-Track Timeline in Kdenlive

This guide outlines the precise steps to export an Edit Decision List (EDL) from a multi-track sequence in Kdenlive. You will learn how to access the export menu, configure your export settings, and properly manage multi-track timelines to ensure compatibility with other non-linear editing systems and color-grading suites.

Understanding EDL Track Limitations

The standard EDL format (CMX 3600) is traditionally designed for single-track video sequences along with up to four audio channels. Because Kdenlive projects often contain complex multi-track arrangements, Kdenlive exports EDLs on a per-track basis or requires you to designate which track to process.

Step-by-Step Export Procedure

  1. Open Your Project: Launch Kdenlive and load the multi-track sequence you want to export.
  2. Organize the Timeline: Ensure your target video and audio cuts are cleaned up. Remove any disabled or muted clips that you do not want included in the export.
  3. Access the EDL Export Tool:
    • Go to the top menu bar and click Project.
    • Select Export EDL... from the dropdown menu.
  4. Configure Export Parameters:
    • Select Target Track: In the EDL export dialog, choose the specific video or audio track you wish to export. If you have cuts across multiple tracks, you will need to repeat this export process for each track individually or flatten your sequence prior to exporting.
    • Framerate Verification: Confirm that the project framerate matches the target software’s timeline framerate to prevent timecode drift.
  5. Save the File:
    • Click the file browser icon to choose your destination folder.
    • Name your file with the .edl extension (e.g., sequence_v1.edl).
    • Click Save or Export to generate the file.

Best Practices for Multi-Track Projects

  • Flatten Before Exporting: If your multi-track sequence represents a single composite edit (such as B-roll overlaid on an A-roll track), flatten the necessary cuts onto a single primary track before exporting to generate a clean, continuous EDL.
  • Export Individual Tracks for Conform: If you are migrating a multi-camera or layered project to a finishing suite like DaVinci Resolve, export separate EDL files for each track (e.g., Track_V1.edl, Track_V2.edl) and reassemble the stack in the destination software.
  • Verify Timecode References: Ensure source media files use consistent reel names and continuous timecode tracks, as missing reel data in Kdenlive will result in generic identifiers in the final EDL output.
