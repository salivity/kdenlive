Adjust Audio Scrubbing Buffer in Kdenlive

Audio scrubbing allows video editors to monitor dialogue and sound cues while moving the playhead across the timeline, but insufficient buffer allocation often leads to stuttering and audio dropouts. In Kdenlive, you can resolve these performance issues by fine-tuning the internal audio playback buffer settings. This guide outlines the exact steps to configure your audio buffer for smooth, responsive timeline scrubbing.

Step-by-Step Guide to Adjust Audio Buffer Settings

  1. Open Kdenlive Settings

    • On Linux or Windows: Click on Settings in the top menu bar and select Configure Kdenlive...
    • On macOS: Click on Kdenlive in the application menu bar and select Preferences...

  2. Navigate to the Playback Tab

    • In the left sidebar of the configuration window, select the Playback section.

  3. Locate the Audio Output Settings

    • In the Playback configuration panel, locate the Audio Driver and Audio Buffer settings.
    • Depending on your system, the driver may be set to SDL, ALSA, PulseAudio, or WASAPI. Ensure your active driver is correctly assigned to your default audio device.

  4. Increase the Audio Buffer Size

    • Find the buffer size or buffer duration slider/input field.
    • Increase the buffer value incrementally (e.g., from 100 ms to 200 ms or higher).
    • A larger buffer provides more headroom for your CPU to process audio frames during rapid scrubbing, eliminating stutter and digital crackle.

  5. Apply the Changes

    • Click Apply, then click OK to close the preferences window.
    • In some cases, you may need to restart Kdenlive for the buffer changes to take full effect with your audio driver.

Additional Optimization Tips for Smoother Scrubbing

  • Enable Proxy Clips: If your project contains high-resolution video files (such as 4K H.264/H.265), the CPU overhead from video decoding can starve audio processing threads. Enable proxy clips under Project > Project Settings > Proxy to reduce decoding load.
  • Match Audio Sample Rates: Ensure your project audio sample rate (e.g., 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz) matches your operating system's audio interface settings to prevent real-time resampling stutter.
  • Disable Waveform Generation During Scrubbing: If the timeline feels sluggish, navigate to Settings > Configure Kdenlive > Timeline and adjust audio thumbnail generation options.
