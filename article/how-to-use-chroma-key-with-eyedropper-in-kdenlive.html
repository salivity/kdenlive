How to Use Chroma Key with Eyedropper in Kdenlive

This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough for removing green or blue screen backgrounds in Kdenlive. By applying the Chroma Key effect and using the built-in color picker eyedropper tool, you can quickly sample your background color and replace it with underlying footage or images on your timeline.

Step 1: Arrange Footage on the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and import both your green screen foreground video and the replacement background media into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag the replacement background media onto the lower video track (e.g., Track V1).
  3. Drag the green screen clip directly above the background media on the higher video track (e.g., Track V2).

Step 2: Add the Chroma Key Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (typically located in the bottom-left panel).
  2. Type Chroma Key in the search bar.
  3. Drag and drop the Chroma Key: Basic or Chroma Key: Advanced effect directly onto the foreground clip located on Track V2.

Step 3: Use the Eyedropper to Sample the Color

  1. Click on the foreground clip on Track V2 to display its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. In the Chroma Key effect settings, locate the Color property.
  3. Click the Color Picker (eyedropper) icon next to the color box.
  4. Move your cursor over to the Project Monitor preview window.
  5. Click on an evenly lit section of the green or blue background near your main subject to sample and remove the color instantly.

Step 4: Refine the Keying Settings

  1. Variance / Threshold: Adjust the variance or delta sliders to expand or restrict the range of the keyed color until all background noise disappears without cutting into the subject.
  2. Edge Softness: Increase the feather or edge softness parameters slightly to blend rough borders around the subject.
  3. Keying Mode (for Advanced Chroma Key): Toggle between normal display and alpha view to inspect the matte for any remaining dark spots or transparent holes in your subject.
