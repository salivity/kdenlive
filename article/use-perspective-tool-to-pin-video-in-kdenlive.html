Use Perspective Tool to Pin Video in Kdenlive

In Kdenlive, pinning a video clip onto an angled or tilted surface—such as a laptop monitor, billboard, or smartphone screen—is achieved using the Perspective effect (also known as corner pinning). This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough on how to arrange your video tracks, apply the Perspective tool, adjust the four corner handles to match the plane of your target surface, and keyframe the effect if the target object is moving.

Step 1: Set Up the Timeline Tracks

  1. Import both the background footage (the scene containing the angled surface) and the overlay video (the clip you want to project onto the surface) into your Project Bin.
  2. Drag the background footage onto Track V1.
  3. Drag the overlay video onto Track V2, placing it directly above the background clip so that it appears on top in the preview.

Step 2: Apply the Perspective / Corners Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab in Kdenlive.
  2. Search for Perspective or Corners in the search bar (located under the Transform, Distort and Perspective category).
  3. Drag and drop the effect directly onto the overlay clip on Track V2.

Step 3: Align the Video Corners to the Angled Surface

  1. Select the clip on Track V2 and look at the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Ensure the Show On-Screen Edit Controls option is enabled in the Clip Monitor or Project Monitor.
  3. You will see four control points representing the four corners of the overlay video:
    • Top Left (X1, Y1)
    • Top Right (X2, Y2)
    • Bottom Left (X3, Y3)
    • Bottom Right (X4, Y4)
  4. Click and drag each corner handle in the Project Monitor to align precisely with the respective four corners of the angled surface in the background footage.
  5. Use the numeric coordinate sliders in the effect settings for fine pixel-by-pixel precision.

Step 4: Track Moving Objects (Optional)

If the camera moves or the target surface shifts over time:

  1. Move the timeline playhead to the beginning of the clip.
  2. Set your initial corner positions to create the first keyframe.
  3. Enable keyframing in the effect panel, advance the playhead along the timeline, and adjust the corner points to match the surface's movement. Kdenlive will automatically interpolate the perspective between keyframes.

Step 5: Enhance Realism

To make the pinned video blend naturally into the environment:

  1. Apply the Curves or Color Correction effects to the overlay clip to match the lighting and contrast of the background footage.
  2. Adjust the opacity slightly or add a slight Gaussian Blur if the background surface is out of focus or has strong environmental reflections.
