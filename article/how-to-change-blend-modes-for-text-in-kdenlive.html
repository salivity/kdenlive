How to Change Blend Modes for Text in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to adjust blend modes on text overlays created with the integrated Title Editor in Kdenlive. While the built-in title tool lets you design and format text elements, blending modes such as Screen, Multiply, or Overlay are applied on the timeline using compositing effects. Follow the steps below to blend your text overlays seamlessly into background footage.

Step 1: Create the Text Clip

  1. In the Project Bin, right-click an empty area and select Add Title Clip (or navigate to Project > Add Title Clip).
  2. Enter your text and configure the font, size, alignment, and base color in the Title Editor window.
  3. Click Create Title to add the text clip to your Project Bin.

Step 2: Place the Clip on the Timeline

  1. Drag your background video footage onto a lower video track (such as V1).
  2. Drag your Title Clip onto the video track directly above the footage (such as V2).

Step 3: Apply a Compositing Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Search for Transform or Composite and Transform.
  3. Drag the effect directly onto the Title Clip on track V2.

Step 4: Select and Adjust the Blend Mode

  1. Click on the Title Clip in the timeline to open its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Locate the Blend Mode or Composition dropdown menu within the applied effect.
  3. Select the desired blend mode from the list:
    • Screen: Keeps light pixels and makes dark backgrounds transparent, ideal for light text.
    • Multiply: Multiplies luminance values, ideal for dark text on lighter footage.
    • Overlay / Soft Light: Combines Multiply and Screen effects to blend text into the textures of the background.
    • Difference / Exclusion: Inverts the text colors based on the underlying video colors.
  4. Adjust the Opacity slider within the same panel to fine-tune the intensity of the blend.
