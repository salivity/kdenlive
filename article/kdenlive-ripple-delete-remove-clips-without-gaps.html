Kdenlive Ripple Delete: Remove Clips Without Gaps

This article provides an overview of the "Ripple Delete" function in Kdenlive, explaining how it works to streamline your video editing workflow. You will learn the mechanics behind removing timeline selections without leaving empty gaps, how it differs from a standard delete, and the most efficient ways to use this feature to keep your project synchronized.

What is Ripple Delete?

In Kdenlive, the primary function of the Ripple Delete (often referred to as Extract) command is to remove a selected clip, cut section, or timeline zone and automatically shift all subsequent clips backward to close the resulting gap.

When you perform a standard deletion, the selected media disappears, leaving an empty space (a gap) on the timeline where the clip once resided. Closing this gap manually requires selecting all downstream clips and dragging them leftward. Ripple Delete combines both actions into a single step: it deletes the asset and instantly pulls all following media forward along the timeline.

Standard Delete vs. Ripple Delete

  • Standard Delete (Lift): Removes the selected clip or zone while maintaining the exact positions of all other clips. This leaves a blank space on the track.
  • Ripple Delete (Extract): Removes the selected clip or zone and immediately shifts all subsequent media to the left by the exact duration of the deleted section, ensuring a continuous, unbroken timeline.

How to Use Ripple Delete in Kdenlive

There are multiple ways to execute a ripple delete in Kdenlive depending on your editing workflow:

  1. Extract Timeline Zone: Mark an In point (I) and an Out point (O) on the timeline to define the region you want to cut. Click the Extract button in the timeline toolbar or press Shift + X (default shortcut). This cuts the designated section across all active tracks and collapses the timeline to remove the blank space.
  2. Remove Space Between Clips: If a gap already exists, right-click on the blank space on the track and select Remove Space. Kdenlive will delete the empty area and shift all following clips on that track to close the gap.
  3. Clip Removal via Context Menu: Right-click the clip or region you wish to remove and choose the ripple delete option, or use the assigned keyboard shortcut to remove the segment without leaving empty frames.

Benefits of Ripple Delete

Using Ripple Delete significantly speeds up the editing process, particularly during rough cuts and pacing adjustments. It eliminates repetitive manual repositioning, reduces the likelihood of leaving unintended black frames in the final render, and ensures that edits remain tight and seamless. When working with multi-track projects, ensure unrelated tracks are locked to prevent unwanted shifting of audio or b-roll layers.
