Animate Subtitles with Keyframes in Kdenlive

Animating a subtitle box across the screen in Kdenlive is a straightforward process achieved by combining title clips with the Transform effect. While Kdenlive features a dedicated subtitle track for standard text, moving and keyframing text boxes requires using a graphic Title Clip on a standard video track. By applying keyframes to adjust spatial coordinates over time, you can create smooth, dynamic subtitle movements such as sliding lower-thirds, scrolling captions, or floating dialog boxes.

Step 1: Create the Subtitle Box Clip

  1. Right-click an empty area in the Project Bin and select Add Title Clip.
  2. To create the background box, click the Add Rectangle icon (or press R), then click and drag in the canvas area. Set your desired fill color and opacity in the properties panel.
  3. Click the Add Text icon (or press T), click over your rectangle, and type your subtitle text. Customize the font, size, and alignment so that it fits neatly inside your box.
  4. Click Create Clip to save it to your Project Bin.
  5. Drag the newly created Title Clip from the Project Bin onto a video track above your main footage in the timeline.

Step 2: Apply the Transform Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (typically located next to the Project Bin).
  2. Search for the Transform effect under the Transform, Distort and Perspective category.
  3. Drag and drop the Transform effect directly onto your Title Clip in the timeline.

Step 3: Set Keyframes for Position Animation

  1. Select the Title Clip in the timeline and open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to view the Transform settings.
  2. Move the timeline playhead to the exact point where you want the subtitle animation to start.
  3. In the Transform parameters, click the Add Keyframe button (plus icon) if a keyframe is not automatically created.
  4. Adjust the X (horizontal) and Y (vertical) values—or drag the bounding box directly in the Project Monitor—to place the subtitle box at its starting position (for example, off-screen).
  5. Move the timeline playhead forward to the point where the movement should stop.
  6. Adjust the X and Y values to the target location on screen. Kdenlive will automatically generate a second keyframe at this position.

Step 4: Fine-Tune Movement and Timing

  • Adjust Timing: In the keyframe ruler within the Effect Stack, click and drag keyframes left or right to speed up or slow down the transition.
  • Smooth the Motion: Right-click a keyframe point and change its interpolation type from Linear to Smooth (or use Bezier handles) to achieve natural acceleration and deceleration.
  • Exit Animation: To move the subtitle off-screen later, add a third keyframe at the current position to hold the text, move the playhead to the end of the clip, and create a fourth keyframe with the final exit coordinates.
