Motion Tracking Text in Kdenlive

Attaching text elements to moving subjects in Kdenlive is achieved using the built-in Motion Tracker effect combined with the Transform effect. By analyzing a selected region of a video clip, Kdenlive generates keyframe tracking data that can be copied directly onto a text or title clip, allowing the graphic element to dynamically follow the movement of any subject seamlessly.

Step 1: Set Up the Video and Text Clips

  1. Import your source video into the Project Bin and drag it onto the primary video track (e.g., V1).
  2. Create a new title clip by clicking Add Clip > Add Title Clip. Design your text element and click Create Title.
  3. Drag the title clip onto the track directly above your video (e.g., V2) and align its duration with the section of the video you want to track.

Step 2: Apply and Configure the Motion Tracker

  1. Select the video clip on V1.
  2. Go to the Effects panel, search for Motion Tracker, and apply it to the video clip.
  3. In the Motion Tracker settings in the Properties panel, select a tracking algorithm (such as KCF or CSRT for balanced accuracy and speed).
  4. In the Project Monitor, adjust the rectangular bounding box so it tightly encloses a distinct, high-contrast feature of the subject you wish to follow.
  5. Move the timeline playhead to the beginning of the action and click the Analyze button to generate the motion path keyframes.

Step 3: Copy the Tracking Keyframe Data

  1. Once the analysis is complete, locate the keyframe section inside the Motion Tracker effect settings.
  2. Click the Options menu (or clipboard icon) in the tracker settings and select Copy Keyframes to Clipboard.

Step 4: Apply the Motion Path to the Text

  1. Select the text clip on V2.
  2. From the Effects panel, add the Transform effect to the text clip.
  3. In the Transform effect settings, click the Options menu and choose Import Keyframes from Clipboard (or paste them directly).
  4. Verify that the keyframe mapping aligns with the start of your tracking sequence.

Step 5: Adjust Position and Offset

  1. Use the Transform effect parameters (X/Y coordinates, scale, and rotation) to adjust the text's relative position so it rests where desired relative to the tracked subject.
  2. Play back the timeline to ensure the text smoothly follows the path. If necessary, manually adjust individual keyframes within the Transform effect to correct any minor drifting.
