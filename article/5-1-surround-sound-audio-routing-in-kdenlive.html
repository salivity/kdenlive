5.1 Surround Sound Audio Routing in Kdenlive

Configuring 5.1 surround sound directly in Kdenlive involves organizing timeline tracks for discrete channel mapping, using channel-routing audio effects, and rendering through a six-channel profile. While Kdenlive primarily defaults to stereo editing, you can achieve a true 5.1 surround sound master (Front Left, Front Right, Center, LFE/Subwoofer, Surround Left, Surround Right) by systematically routing each audio stem to its corresponding output channel index.

1. Structure Your Audio Tracks

Begin by creating dedicated audio tracks in the timeline for each individual component of the 5.1 mix. Standard 5.1 surround configurations follow the SMPTE/ITU channel order:

  • Track 1: Front Left (FL) – Channel 1
  • Track 2: Front Right (FR) – Channel 2
  • Track 3: Center (C) – Channel 3 (Dialogue and focused Foley)
  • Track 4: LFE / Subwoofer – Channel 4 (Low-frequency effects, typically filtered below 120 Hz)
  • Track 5: Surround Left (SL) – Channel 5 (Ambience and rear sound effects)
  • Track 6: Surround Right (SR) – Channel 6 (Ambience and rear sound effects)

Place your mono or dedicated stereo assets onto their respective tracks according to where they should sit in the soundstage.

2. Apply Channel Routing Effects

To ensure audio on each track routes strictly to its designated output channel rather than downmixing to stereo, apply channel mapping effects to the tracks.

  1. Open the Effects tab in Kdenlive and search for Audio Matrix or Channel Copy / Map (available via MLT and Sox audio plugins).
  2. Add the effect directly to the track header to apply it to all clips placed on that track.
  3. Configure the matrix to route the source audio into only the intended channel index:
    • Center Track: Route input signal strictly to Output 3.
    • LFE Track: Route input signal to Output 4. Apply a Low Pass filter effect set between 80 Hz and 120 Hz to remove higher frequencies.
    • Surround Left & Right: Route inputs strictly to Output 5 and Output 6.
    • Front Left & Right: Route inputs to Output 1 and Output 2.

If you are panning stereo sound effects dynamically, use LADSPA surround pan plugins available within Kdenlive to automate movement across the 6 discrete channels.

3. Balance and Master Levels

Surround sound formats require specific gain calibration to prevent distortion and maintain standard playback balance:

  • Dialogue (Center): Keep dialogue levels around -24 LKFS/LUFS (integrated broadcast standard) or peaks around -12 dB to -6 dB.
  • LFE Channel: Ensure the bass channel does not clip; keep headroom around -10 dB to -3 dB.
  • Surround Ambience: Keep rear channel levels roughly 3 dB lower than front channels unless delivering a specific high-impact rear effect.

4. Configure Render Settings for 6-Channel Output

To export the final master with all 6 discrete channels intact:

  1. Click Render (or press Ctrl + Enter).
  2. Choose a format that natively supports multichannel audio, such as MP4, MKV, or ProRes / DNxHD (using PCM audio).
  3. In the Render dialog, click Edit Profile to view the underlying FFmpeg parameters.
  4. Under the Audio properties:
    • Set Channels to 6.
    • Set the Audio Codec to ac3, eac3, aac, or uncompressed pcm_s24le.
    • If using AC-3 or AAC, set the audio bitrate to at least 448 kb/s or 640 kb/s to preserve multichannel fidelity.
  5. Enter the custom FFmpeg audio argument -channel_layout 5.1 if your target container requires strict metadata tags.
  6. Render the project to produce your fully mastered 5.1 surround sound video file.
