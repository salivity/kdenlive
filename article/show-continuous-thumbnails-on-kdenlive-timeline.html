Show Continuous Thumbnails on Kdenlive Timeline

By default, Kdenlive displays thumbnail previews only at the beginning and end of clips to conserve system performance. Configuring your timeline video tracks to display continuous thumbnail ribbons gives you a continuous, frame-by-frame visual preview across the entire duration of your video clips. This guide provides clear, step-by-step instructions on how to enable continuous thumbnails for individual timeline tracks and set it as the default behavior across all projects.

Enable Continuous Thumbnails for a Specific Track

To change thumbnail behavior on an active timeline track:

  1. Locate the Track Header on the far left side of the timeline corresponding to the video track you want to modify (e.g., Video 1).
  2. Right-click on the empty space within the track header to open the context menu.
  3. Hover over the Thumbnails option.
  4. Select All Frames from the submenu.

The track will immediately regenerate its visual cache to display a continuous ribbon of thumbnail images along the length of all clips placed on that specific track.

Set Continuous Thumbnails as the Global Default

If you want all newly created video tracks to automatically display continuous thumbnail ribbons:

  1. Open the top application menu and go to Settings > Configure Kdenlive.
  2. Select the Timeline category from the sidebar on the left.
  3. In the main settings panel, locate the Thumbnails section.
  4. Set the Video thumbnails dropdown menu to All frames (or continuous).
  5. Click Apply, then click OK to save the changes.

Adjusting Thumbnail Heights for Better Visibility

Continuous thumbnails are easiest to view when tracks are taller:

  • Hover your mouse cursor over the dividing line between two track headers until the cursor changes to a resize arrow, then click and drag down to increase the track height.
  • Alternatively, right-click any track header and select a preset height option to expand track visibility across the entire project.
