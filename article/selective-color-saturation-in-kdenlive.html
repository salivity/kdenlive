Selective Color Saturation in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to isolate specific color ranges and adjust their saturation selectively in Kdenlive. By targeting particular hues within your video footage, you can boost, mute, or completely desaturate chosen colors without altering the rest of the scene, allowing for precise secondary color grading directly within your timeline.

1. Add the Target Effect

  1. Place your video clip onto the Kdenlive timeline.
  2. Open the Effects tab (navigate to View > Effects if it is closed).
  3. Search for Color Selection or Hue Selection in the search bar.
  4. Drag and drop the effect onto the clip in your timeline.

2. Isolate the Color Range

  1. Click the clip in the timeline to open its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Select the eyedropper icon next to the color parameter and click the specific color in the Clip Monitor that you want to adjust.
  3. Adjust the Hue Tolerance and Hue Range sliders to expand or contract the selected color spectrum.
  4. Check the Show Mask or Mask Preview option to display the isolated area as a high-contrast matte. This helps ensure only the intended parts of the frame are selected.

3. Modify the Saturation

  1. Turn off the mask preview to view the full image.
  2. Locate the Saturation or Chroma slider within the effect controls.
  3. Increase the saturation value to make the targeted color pop, or lower it toward zero to mute or create a selective black-and-white effect.
  4. Adjust the Lightness or Gain parameters if necessary to balance the exposure of the saturated area.

4. Feather and Smooth the Edges

  1. Adjust the Softness or Edge Feathering sliders to eliminate harsh boundaries between the modified color and surrounding pixels.
  2. Preview the playback across the entire clip to verify that the selection remains clean and stable despite lighting variations or motion.
