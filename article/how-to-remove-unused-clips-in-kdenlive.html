How to Remove Unused Clips in Kdenlive

Before archiving your Kdenlive video projects, it is essential to clean up your workspace to save storage space and keep your files organized. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to identify and purge unused clips from your Project Bin, ensuring your archived project contains only the media actually used in your final timeline.

Step 1: Open Your Kdenlive Project

Launch Kdenlive and open the project you wish to clean up and archive. Make sure your timeline is in its final state, as any clips not currently placed on the timeline will be targeted for removal.

Step 2: Use the "Remove Unused Clips" Function

Kdenlive features a built-in tool that automatically detects which assets in your Project Bin are not being used in any timeline tracks.

  1. Go to the top menu bar and click on Project.
  2. From the dropdown menu, select Remove Unused Clips.
  3. Kdenlive will instantly scan your timeline and delete all unused video, audio, and image assets from your Project Bin.

Note: This process only removes the clips from your Kdenlive project interface. It does not delete the original media files from your computer's hard drive.

Step 3: Verify the Project Bin

Look over your Project Bin to ensure that only the active assets remain. If you have nested folders or bins, they should now only contain the media files that are actively used in your edit.

Step 4: Save and Archive the Project

Once the unused clips are purged, you can safely archive your project with a minimal file footprint.

  1. Go to Project in the top menu.
  2. Select Archive Project.
  3. Choose your destination folder, select your compression options, and click Start.

By purging the unused clips first, Kdenlive will only copy the required media files into your archive folder, saving significant disk space.
