Kdenlive Timeline Smooth Scrolling Playback

Configuring Kdenlive's timeline to use continuous smooth scrolling ensures that the playhead remains smoothly centered or moves fluidly across the screen during video playback instead of jumping page-by-page. This guide provides the exact steps needed to adjust Kdenlive's timeline settings to achieve seamless playback scrolling.

Step-by-Step Configuration

  1. Open the Configuration Menu

    • On Windows or Linux, navigate to the top menu bar and click Settings > Configure Kdenlive...
    • On macOS, click Kdenlive in the top menu bar and select Preferences... (or press Ctrl + Shift + , / Cmd + Shift + ,).

  2. Navigate to Timeline Settings

    • In the left sidebar of the configuration window, click on the Timeline tab.

  3. Adjust Timeline Scrolling Behavior

    • Locate the section labeled Timeline Scrolling or Playback Scrolling.
    • Check the option to Scroll timeline during playback.
    • From the dropdown menu or selection options, select Smooth scroll (or Center playhead continuously, depending on your Kdenlive version).

  4. Apply and Save Settings

    • Click the Apply button in the lower-right corner of the window.
    • Click OK to close the preferences dialog.

Verifying the Setup

  • Return to your main editing workspace and position the playhead on your timeline.
  • Press the Spacebar to start playback.
  • The timeline tracks will now scroll smoothly and continuously in real time as the playhead advances, eliminating abrupt page-flipping transitions.
