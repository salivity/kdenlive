How to Adjust Kdenlive Timeline Scrubbing Speed

This guide explains how to adjust the timeline scrubbing and shuttle speed when navigating video projects using keyboard controls in Kdenlive. By configuring playback settings and shortcut jump intervals, you can fine-tune how quickly the playhead moves across your timeline using J-K-L shuttle keys or directional frame-stepping shortcuts.

Adjusting J-K-L Shuttle Playback Speed

In Kdenlive, the standard keyboard scrubbing method uses the J (rewind), K (pause), and L (fast-forward) keys. Pressing J or L multiple times increases the playback speed exponentially (e.g., 2x, 4x, 8x).

To adjust shuttle playback behavior:

  1. Open Kdenlive and go to the top menu.
  2. Click Settings and select Configure Kdenlive... (or press Ctrl + Shift + ,).
  3. Select the Playback tab from the left sidebar.
  4. Locate the Shuttle and Speed settings.
  5. Adjust the speed multiplier steps and maximum playback speed limits to match your preferred scrubbing pace.
  6. Click Apply and then OK to save your changes.

Adjusting Timeline Jump and Step Intervals

If you prefer scrubbing frame-by-frame or skipping larger sections using arrow keys, you can customize the step increments:

  1. Go to Settings > Configure Kdenlive....
  2. Select the Timeline tab.
  3. Under the timeline navigation options, locate settings for frame steps and time skips.
  4. Set the default interval for small jumps (typically standard arrow keys) and large jumps (typically Shift + arrow keys) to your desired number of frames or seconds.
  5. Click Apply and OK.

Customizing Custom Scrubbing Shortcuts

To assign dedicated keys for specific scrubbing speeds:

  1. Go to Settings > Configure Keyboard Shortcuts....
  2. Type shuttle, forward, or backward into the search bar.
  3. Select actions such as Forward 1 Frame, Forward 1 Second, or specific shuttle velocity triggers.
  4. Assign custom keys or modifier combinations that allow you to scrub at the exact speed your workflow requires.
  5. Click OK to apply the shortcut layout.
