How to Brighten Midtones in Kdenlive Using Gamma

This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to isolate and brighten the midtones of a video clip in Kdenlive using the "Lift/Gamma/Gain" effect. By adjusting the gamma levels independently, you can enhance the exposure of your footage's midtones without overexposing the highlights or darkening the shadows.

To brighten the midtones of your clip in Kdenlive, follow these steps:

  1. Select Your Clip: Click on the video clip in your timeline that you want to edit.
  2. Open the Effects Tab: Locate the "Effects" panel (usually found in the tabbed menu near the Project Bin). If you do not see it, enable it by going to the top menu and selecting View > Effects.
  3. Locate the Effect: In the search bar of the Effects panel, type Lift/Gamma/Gain. This effect is the standard tool for independent color and exposure adjustments in Kdenlive.
  4. Apply the Effect: Drag and drop the "Lift/Gamma/Gain" effect directly onto your selected timeline clip.
  5. Adjust the Gamma: Open the Properties (or Effect Stack) panel to view the effect settings. Locate the Gamma section, which specifically controls the midtones.
    • To brighten the midtones, increase the Gamma value by dragging the slider to the right or typing a higher number into the value box.
    • Alternatively, if you are using the color wheels, drag the center point of the Gamma wheel slightly toward the brighter, outer edge to lift the midtone exposure.
  6. Preview the Results: Watch the Project Monitor to ensure the midtones are brightened to your satisfaction while the shadows (controlled by "Lift") and highlights (controlled by "Gain") remain untouched.
