Configure Kdenlive Timeline Snapping Tolerance

This guide explains how to adjust the timeline snapping tolerance in Kdenlive to achieve precise audio editing and clip alignment. While Kdenlive applies snap settings globally across both video and audio tracks, modifying the pixel threshold allows you to control how close an audio clip or cut must be to markers, the playhead, or other clips before magnetically locking into place.

Steps to Change Timeline Snapping Distance

  1. Open Kdenlive.
  2. In the top application menu, go to Settings and select Configure Kdenlive... (on macOS, select Kdenlive > Preferences...).
  3. In the configuration window, select the Timeline category from the left sidebar.
  4. Locate the Snapping section.
  5. Adjust the Snap distance value (measured in pixels):
    • Lower values (e.g., 5–10 px): Reduce magnetic pull, requiring you to drag audio clips very close to targets before they lock. This is ideal for fine adjustments near other clips.
    • Higher values (e.g., 20–30 px): Increase magnetic pull, making it easier to snap cuts and clips quickly without zooming in.
  6. Click Apply and then OK to save your changes.

Snapping Controls for Fine Audio Edits

  • Toggle Snapping On/Off: Click the Snap icon (horseshoe magnet) located in the timeline toolbar, or press the default keyboard shortcut Shift + S.
  • Zoom Level Impact: Snapping tolerance is calculated in screen pixels rather than time units. To achieve frame-level or millisecond-level precision without changing global settings, zoom into the timeline using Ctrl + Mouse Wheel or the zoom slider in the bottom right corner.
  • Temporary Snap Disable: You can temporarily disable snapping while dragging an audio clip by holding down the designated modifier key (or toggling snap off) to place audio seamlessly between snap points.
