Hide Waveforms and Show Clip Names in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to disable background audio waveforms and thumbnails on Kdenlive timeline video tracks, leaving only the clip names visible. Disabling these visual elements declutters your workspace, simplifies clip identification, and reduces system resource usage during editing.

Disable Audio Waveforms Globally on the Timeline

To turn off background audio waveforms across all timeline tracks:

  1. Open Kdenlive and navigate to the top menu bar.
  2. Click on Timeline.
  3. Locate the Show Audio Thumbnails (or Audio Waveforms) option in the dropdown menu.
  4. Click it to uncheck the option.

Once unchecked, Kdenlive will immediately remove the background audio waveforms from all clips on the timeline.

Disable Waveforms and Thumbnails in Preferences

To ensure audio waveforms and video thumbnails remain hidden by default for future sessions:

  1. Go to Settings > Configure Kdenlive... from the main menu.
  2. Select the Timeline tab from the left sidebar.
  3. In the display settings section, locate Thumbnails.
  4. Uncheck Audio thumbnails.
  5. To hide video frame previews and show only clip names, set Video thumbnails to No thumbnails.
  6. Click Apply, then OK to save the changes.

Disable Waveforms per Individual Track

If you only want to remove waveforms from specific video or audio tracks:

  1. Right-click the track header on the left side of the timeline (where the track name, mute, and lock icons are located).
  2. Uncheck Show Audio Thumbnails in the context menu.
  3. If applicable, uncheck Show Video Thumbnails to display only the plain clip block with its label.
