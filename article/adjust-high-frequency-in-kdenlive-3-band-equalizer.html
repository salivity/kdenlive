Adjust High Frequency in Kdenlive 3-Band Equalizer

This guide explains how to locate and adjust the high-shelf frequency setting within the 3-Band Equalizer audio effect in Kdenlive. By modifying the high crossover threshold, you can define the exact frequency range affected by the high gain control, allowing for precise control over the treble frequencies in your audio tracks.

Step 1: Add the 3-Band Equalizer Effect

  1. Select the audio clip or video clip with audio on your timeline.
  2. Open the Effects tab in the interface.
  3. Use the search bar to locate the 3-Band Equalizer effect (found under the Audio Correction or Audio category).
  4. Drag and drop the effect onto the clip, or double-click the effect while the clip is selected.

Step 2: Open the Effect Stack

  1. Ensure the clip remains selected.
  2. Go to the Effect/Composition Stack panel (usually located next to the Project Bin or Properties tab).
  3. Expand the 3-Band Equalizer section to view its available sliders and input fields.

Step 3: Adjust the High-Shelf Frequency

  1. Locate the High Crossover (or High Frequency) parameter slider in the effect settings. This setting sets the cutoff frequency where the high-shelf filter begins.
  2. Drag the slider to the left to lower the starting frequency, or to the right to increase it (measured in Hz).
  3. Alternatively, click directly on the numeric field to type an exact frequency value (for example, 5000 for 5 kHz).

Step 4: Fine-Tune the High Gain

  1. Adjust the High Gain slider to either boost (positive values) or cut (negative values) the audio levels at and above the high-shelf frequency you selected.
  2. Play back the timeline in real time to monitor changes and fine-tune the high crossover frequency until the treble sounds balanced.
