How to Enable Audio Mixer Panel in Kdenlive

This guide provides a quick and straightforward walkthrough on how to enable and display the audio mixer panel in Kdenlive. Whether you accidentally closed the panel or are setting up your workspace for the first time, you will learn the exact menu paths to restore this essential tool for monitoring and adjusting your video project's audio levels.

Step-by-Step Guide to Enable the Audio Mixer

To bring back or open the audio mixer panel in Kdenlive, follow these simple steps:

  1. Open the View Menu: Move your cursor to the top menu bar of the Kdenlive window and click on View.
  2. Locate the Audio Mixer: In the drop-down menu, look for the option labeled Audio Mixer.
  3. Enable the Panel: Click on Audio Mixer to check the box next to it.

Once selected, the audio mixer panel will immediately appear on your screen, typically docked on the right side or near the project monitor.

How to Dock and Arrange the Audio Mixer

If the audio mixer appears as a floating window or is not in your preferred position, you can easily integrate it into your workspace:

  • Drag and Drop: Click and hold the title bar of the Audio Mixer panel, then drag it over other panels. You will see colored guide zones showing where the panel can be docked. Release the mouse button to drop it into place.
  • Tabbed View: Drag the Audio Mixer directly on top of another panel (such as the Effects or Project Bin) to combine them into a tabbed layout, saving screen space.

Resetting the Workspace Layout

If you cannot find the menu option or your workspace is too cluttered, you can reset Kdenlive to its default audio-editing layout:

  1. Go to the top menu and select Window.
  2. Hover over Workspaces.
  3. Select Audio. This action automatically opens and positions the audio mixer alongside your timeline in an optimized layout for sound editing.
