Using Kdenlive Spacer Tool to Close Timeline Gaps

The Spacer Tool in Kdenlive is a dedicated editing utility designed to shift clips across single or multiple tracks simultaneously, making it an essential feature for managing and closing gaps. This article explains how the Spacer Tool functions on multi-track timelines, how it preserves project synchronization, and how to effectively use it to remove unwanted empty spaces in your video edits.

Understanding the Spacer Tool Function

When editing complex multi-track projects in Kdenlive, deleting a clip often leaves empty spaces (gaps) across various video and audio tracks. Manually selecting and dragging individual clips to close these gaps can easily cause audio and video to fall out of synchronization.

The Spacer Tool solves this problem by acting as a universal push-and-pull mechanism. When activated, it selects every clip to the right of your cursor position across all unlocked tracks, allowing you to move entire timeline sections forward or backward as a single cohesive unit.

How the Spacer Tool Closes Multi-Track Gaps

To close gaps across multiple tracks using the Spacer Tool:

  1. Activate the Tool: Select the Spacer Tool from the timeline toolbar or press the keyboard shortcut M.
  2. Position the Cursor: Click on the timeline where the gap ends—specifically on the first clip located immediately to the right of the empty space.
  3. Drag to Close the Gap: Click and drag to the left until the gap is closed against the preceding clips.
  4. Release: Once aligned, release the mouse button. All clips across all active tracks shift left together, eliminating the gap without disrupting relative track alignment.

Key Behaviors and Capabilities

  • Multi-Track Synchronization: By moving all downstream clips simultaneously, audio tracks (voiceovers, sound effects, background music) and secondary video tracks remain perfectly aligned with the main timeline.
  • Track Locking Support: If you have elements that should not move—such as a continuous background music track—you can lock that specific track before using the Spacer Tool. The tool will bypass locked tracks and only move content on unlocked tracks.
  • Flexible Directionality: Beyond closing gaps by dragging left, the Spacer Tool can also drag content to the right, allowing you to insert new footage into the middle of a project without overwriting existing edits.
