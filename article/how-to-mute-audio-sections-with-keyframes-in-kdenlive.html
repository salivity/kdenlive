How to Mute Audio Sections with Keyframes in Kdenlive

This article provides a step-by-step guide on how to mute specific sections of your audio in Kdenlive using keyframes. You will learn how to apply the keyframable Volume effect, add keyframes to isolate the exact parts of the timeline you want to silence, and adjust the audio levels to create seamless, muted intervals without needing to split your clips.

Step 1: Add the Volume Effect to Your Clip

To mute specific sections of a clip using keyframes, you must use the keyframable volume effect.

  1. Open your Kdenlive project and locate your audio or video clip on the timeline.
  2. Go to the Effects tab (usually located in the bottom-left or top-left panel).
  3. Search for Volume in the search bar.
  4. Drag and drop the Volume (keyframable) effect directly onto your audio track or video clip.

Step 2: Access the Effect Stack

Once the effect is applied, you need to open the properties to add keyframes.

  1. Click on the clip in your timeline to select it.
  2. Open the Properties / Effect Stack panel (usually in the top-right or center-left of the interface).
  3. You will see the Volume effect options with a timeline ruler and keyframe controls.

Step 3: Create Keyframes for the Muted Section

To mute a specific section, you need to create four keyframes to transition the volume down to silence and then back up to normal.

  1. Move the timeline playhead to the point just before you want the mute to start. Click the Add Keyframe icon (the small double-arrow or plus/diamond icon in the effect stack). Keep this volume at 0.00 dB (normal volume).
  2. Move the playhead a frame or two forward to the exact start of your desired mute section. Add a second keyframe and drag the volume slider all the way to the left (-50.00 dB or the lowest setting) to completely silence the audio.
  3. Move the playhead to the end of the section you want muted. Add a third keyframe and ensure the volume remains at the lowest setting (-50.00 dB).
  4. Move the playhead a frame or two forward to where the normal audio should resume. Add a fourth keyframe and bring the volume slider back up to 0.00 dB.

Step 4: Choose Keyframe Interpolation (Optional)

By default, Kdenlive will smoothly transition (fade) the volume down and up. If you want an instant cut to silence rather than a fade:

  1. Right-click on the second and third keyframes in the effect stack timeline.
  2. Change the keyframe type/interpolation from Linear to Discrete. This will make the volume drop to mute and return to normal instantly.
