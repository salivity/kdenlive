How to Adjust Audio Compressor Ratio in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to apply the Audio Compressor effect in Kdenlive and configure its compression ratio specifically for spoken dialogue. By following these steps, you will learn where to locate the effect, how to apply it to your clips or entire audio tracks, and how to dial in the appropriate ratio alongside essential threshold and gain settings to achieve consistent, broadcast-ready voice levels.

1. Apply the Audio Compressor Effect

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and ensure your dialogue is placed on an audio track.
  2. Go to the Effects panel (usually located in the bottom-left or accessible via View > Effects).
  3. Type Compressor into the search bar or navigate to the Audio Correction category.
  4. Drag the Compressor effect directly onto an individual audio clip, or drag it onto the track header to apply compression to the entire dialogue track.

2. Open the Effect Stack

  1. Select the clip or track containing the effect.
  2. Open the Properties / Effect Stack panel (accessible via View > Effect Stack if not visible).
  3. Locate the Compressor controls in the list of active effects.

3. Adjust the Compression Ratio for Dialogue

  1. Locate the Ratio parameter slider within the Compressor controls.
  2. Set the ratio to a value between 2.0:1 and 4.0:1, which is the standard range for natural-sounding dialogue:
    • 2:1 to 3:1: Ideal for mild compression to smooth out natural speaking dynamics without noticeable processing.
    • 4:1: Best for voice recordings with wide volume swings, such as energetic podcasts or commentary.
  3. Avoid ratios above 6:1 for standard dialogue, as aggressive ratios can introduce unwanted audio pumping and unnatural breathing sounds.

4. Configure Complementary Compressor Settings

To ensure the adjusted ratio works effectively, set the accompanying parameters:

  • Threshold: Lower the threshold slider (typically between -18 dB and -12 dB) until the compression actively triggers during louder spoken words while leaving quiet parts untouched.
  • Attack Time: Set between 10 ms and 25 ms to allow initial vocal consonants to pass through clearly before compression begins.
  • Release Time: Set between 50 ms and 100 ms so the compressor releases smoothly between spoken words without abrupt volume shifts.
  • Gain (Makeup Gain): Increase the output gain slightly (usually +2 dB to +5 dB) to compensate for the overall volume reduction caused by the compression.

5. Monitor and Finalize Audio Levels

  1. Play back the timeline and monitor the audio output using the Audio Mixer tool.
  2. Ensure spoken dialogue peaks consistently between -12 dB and -6 dB.
  3. Fine-tune the Ratio and Threshold settings until loud spikes are contained and overall vocal clarity remains balanced throughout the sequence.
