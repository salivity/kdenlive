Kdenlive Audio Track Routing: Export Dialogue and Music Stems

Exporting discrete audio stems for dialogue and music is an essential step for post-production audio mixing and mastering. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on configuring Kdenlive’s timeline, master audio channels, and rendering settings to route dialogue and music tracks to dedicated output channels, as well as an alternative workflow for exporting separate stem files.

Method 1: Multi-Channel Audio Routing (Single File Output)

To output both dialogue and music into a single video or audio file with distinct channels (e.g., Channels 1–2 for Dialogue, Channels 3–4 for Music), set up a 4-channel project.

1. Set Up the Project Audio Channels

  1. Go to Project > Project Settings.
  2. Navigate to the Audio tab.
  3. Change the Channels setting from 2 (Stereo) to 4 Channels (or more, depending on your needs).
  4. Click OK.

2. Organize the Timeline Tracks

  1. Label your audio tracks for clarity. Right-click an audio track header and rename it (e.g., A1 - Dialogue, A2 - Dialogue, A3 - Music L/R).
  2. Place all dialogue assets strictly on the dialogue tracks.
  3. Place all background music and score clips strictly on the music tracks.

3. Route Audio via Track Effects

To send specific tracks to specific output pairs, use audio channel matrix effects:

  1. Select the Audio Mixer tab or open the Effects panel.
  2. For dialogue tracks (A1, A2):
    • Add the Channel Matrix or Stereo to 4 Channels effect to the track.
    • Map the inputs to Output Channels 1 and 2, setting the levels for Output Channels 3 and 4 to zero.
  3. For music tracks (A3, A4):
    • Add the Channel Matrix effect to the track.
    • Map the inputs to Output Channels 3 and 4, setting the levels for Output Channels 1 and 2 to zero.

4. Render the Multi-Channel File

  1. Open the Render window (Project > Render or Ctrl + Return).
  2. Choose a format that supports multi-channel audio, such as MOV (ProRes or DNxHD), MKV, or WAV (for audio-only).
  3. Under the Audio parameters in the render preset, verify that the audio channel count is set to 4.
  4. Click Render to File. The output file will contain Channels 1–2 mapped to dialogue and Channels 3–4 mapped to music.

Method 2: Exporting Individual Stem Files (Batch/Solo Workflow)

If your delivery requirements call for separate .wav files for dialogue and music instead of a single multi-channel container, use track muting.

1. Export the Dialogue Stem

  1. Mute all music and sound effect tracks by clicking the Mute Track (speaker icon) on their track headers, leaving only the dialogue tracks active.
  2. Open the Render dialog (Ctrl + Return).
  3. Under the Audio Only category, select WAV 48kHz.
  4. Name the output file ProjectName_Stem_Dialogue.wav and render.

2. Export the Music Stem

  1. Unmute the music tracks and mute all dialogue tracks.
  2. In the Render dialog, set the output name to ProjectName_Stem_Music.wav.
  3. Click Render to File.

Following either method ensures clean separation between dialogue and music for downstream audio post-production.
