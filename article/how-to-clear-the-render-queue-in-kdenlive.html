How to Clear the Render Queue in Kdenlive

When exporting video in Kdenlive, background rendering jobs can occasionally freeze or crash, blocking subsequent exports from processing. This guide outlines the straightforward steps required to stop a frozen export, clear the render daemon queue within the application, and terminate stubborn background rendering processes in your operating system.

1. Stop the Render from the Job Queue

  1. Open Kdenlive and click the Render button in the main toolbar, or press Ctrl + Return.
  2. Navigate to the Job Queue tab at the top or bottom of the Render dialog window (depending on your Kdenlive version).
  3. Identify the stalled export job.
  4. Select the job and click the Abort Job button (represented by a red stop icon or a cross).
  5. If the job status changes to "Stopped" or "Failed," select it and click the Clean or Remove (trash can) button to clear it from the list.

2. Close and Restart the Render Daemon Window

If Kdenlive has detached the render job to the standalone Render Daemon window:

  1. Locate the Kdenlive Render Queue window running in your taskbar.
  2. Select the frozen job from the list.
  3. Click the Stop button, then click Remove Job.
  4. Close the Render Queue window and reopen the Render dialog within your main Kdenlive project window to resume exporting.

3. Terminate Hanging Background Processes

If the user interface is completely unresponsive or the queue refuses to clear, the underlying rendering engine (melt) may still be locked in memory.

On Linux:

  1. Open a terminal.
  2. Run the following command to terminate the background rendering engine: 
    killall kdenlive_render melt
  3. Restart Kdenlive.

On Windows:

  1. Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open the Task Manager.
  2. Look for kdenlive_render.exe or melt.exe under the Processes or Details tab.
  3. Right-click the process and select End task.
  4. Restart Kdenlive.

4. Remove Lingering Render Scripts (Optional)

If Kdenlive attempts to restart the frozen job upon launch, clear the temporary render script directory:

  • Linux: Navigate to ~/.local/share/kdenlive/scripts/ and delete any pending .mlt or .sh files associated with the frozen export.
  • Windows: Navigate to %APPDATA%\kdenlive\scripts\ or your configured temporary cache folder and delete any pending .bat or .mlt export scripts.
