Kdenlive Keyboard Shortcuts for Faster Video Editing

Mastering keyboard shortcuts is one of the most effective ways to speed up your video editing workflow in Kdenlive. This guide highlights the essential keyboard shortcuts for navigating the Kdenlive interface, managing your timeline, and editing clips efficiently.

Playback and Navigation Shortcuts

Navigating the timeline quickly is crucial for precise editing. Use these shortcuts to control playback and move the playhead:

  • Spacebar: Play or pause the playback.
  • J: Rewind playback (press multiple times to increase speed).
  • K: Pause playback.
  • L: Fast forward playback (press multiple times to increase speed).
  • Left / Right Arrow: Move the playhead backward or forward by a single frame.
  • Shift + Left / Right Arrow: Move the playhead backward or forward by one second.
  • Home / End: Jump directly to the very beginning or the end of the timeline.

Timeline Editing and Tool Selection

Switching between tools using your mouse slows down your progress. Use these hotkeys to swap tools and perform edits on the fly:

  • S: Activate the Selection Tool (normal cursor).
  • X: Activate the Razor Tool (used to cut clips manually).
  • Shift + R: Cut the selected clip precisely at the current playhead position.
  • Delete: Remove the selected clip from the timeline, leaving a gap.
  • Shift + Delete: Ripple delete (removes the selected clip and automatically closes the resulting gap).
  • G: Group selected clips together.
  • Ctrl + G: Ungroup the selected clips.

Project and Interface Controls

These shortcuts help you manage your project files and adjust the visual layout of the Kdenlive interface:

  • Ctrl + Mouse Wheel Scroll: Zoom in and out of the timeline for detailed or broad views.
  • I: Set the In point for a specific rendering or preview zone.
  • O: Set the Out point for a specific rendering or preview zone.
  • Ctrl + Z: Undo the last action.
  • Ctrl + Shift + Z: Redo the last undone action.
  • Ctrl + S: Save your current project.
