How to Create Scrolling Credits in Kdenlive

Creating animated scrolling credits in Kdenlive is a straightforward process handled entirely within the software's built-in Title Editor. This guide explains how to create a new title clip, enter and format your credit text, enable the native vertical scrolling animation, and adjust the playback speed directly on the project timeline for a professional finish.

Step 1: Create a New Title Clip

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. In the Project Bin, click the drop-down arrow next to the Add Clip button (or right-click in an empty area of the Project Bin).
  3. Select Add Title Clip from the menu to open the integrated Title Editor window.

Step 2: Enter and Format the Credit Text

  1. Click the Text tool (represented by a 'T' icon) in the toolbar.
  2. Click inside the canvas area to create a text box.
  3. Type or paste your credits into the box.
  4. Use the formatting controls at the top of the editor:
    • Font & Size: Choose a clear, readable font (such as standard Sans-Serif fonts) and an appropriate font size.
    • Alignment: Center-align the text horizontally to achieve the standard film-credit look.
    • Positioning: Use the alignment tools in the toolbar to center the text block horizontally within the frame boundaries.
  5. If the credits list is long, resize the text bounding box downwards to ensure all text remains within the same text element.

Step 3: Enable the Scrolling Animation

  1. Locate the Animation tab or options on the right side of the Title Editor.
  2. Check the box labeled Scroll (or Vertical scroll depending on your Kdenlive version).
  3. Set the scroll parameters:
    • Start position: Adjust the offset if you want the text to begin completely off-screen at the bottom.
    • End position: Set the end parameter so the text completely exits the top of the frame.
  4. Click Create Clip (or Apply) at the bottom right to save the title and close the editor.

Step 4: Adjust Scrolling Speed on the Timeline

  1. Drag the newly created Title Clip from the Project Bin onto a video track above your background video, or onto an empty track if you want a solid black background.
  2. The scrolling speed is directly determined by the duration of the clip on the timeline:
    • To slow down the credits: Drag the right edge of the Title Clip on the timeline to make it longer.
    • To speed up the credits: Shorten the duration of the clip on the timeline.
  3. Play back the timeline to preview the animation and adjust the clip length until the reading pace is comfortable.
