Adjust Alpha Shapes in Kdenlive: Feather and Curvature

The Alpha Shapes effect in Kdenlive is a powerful tool for generating custom masks, vignettes, and cutouts directly on your video clips. This guide explains how to apply the Alpha Shapes effect to your footage and properly configure its Feather and Curvature parameters to achieve soft edges and smooth geometric contours.

1. Apply the Alpha Shapes Effect

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and place your target video clip or image onto the timeline.
  2. Open the Effects panel (usually located near the project bin or accessible via View > Effects).
  3. Search for Alpha Shapes (found under the Alpha, Mask and Keying category).
  4. Drag and drop the Alpha Shapes effect onto your timeline clip.

2. Adjust the Curvature Parameter

The Curvature slider modifies the sharpness or roundness of the shape's corners:

  1. Select the clip on the timeline to display its parameters in the Effect Stack panel.
  2. Locate the Shape dropdown menu and choose your base geometry (such as Rectangle or Ellipse).
  3. Find the Curvature slider:
    • Low values (0%): Maintain sharp, straight, and angular edges (ideal for strict boxes or hard-edged polygons).
    • High values (up to 100%): Round off the corners, turning sharp rectangles into stadium shapes or smooth rounded rectangles.
  4. Drag the slider or input an exact numerical value until the contour matches your desired shape.

3. Adjust the Feather Parameter

The Feather control softens the boundary of your alpha mask to create smooth transitions:

  1. In the Effect Stack panel, locate the Feather parameter.
  2. Adjust the slider to set your edge softness:
    • At 0% (Minimum): The transition between the visible area and the transparent area is sharp and distinct.
    • Higher percentages: The boundary dissolves into a gradual gradient blur, blending the masked clip naturally into the background or track below.
  3. Combine the Feather setting with the Size X and Size Y controls to ensure the soft falloff does not clip off-screen or cut into unwanted areas of your subject.

4. Keyframing for Dynamic Transitions

To animate the softness or shape changes over time:

  1. Click the Keyframe (clock) icon next to the Curvature or Feather parameters in the Effect Stack.
  2. Move the playhead to different points on the clip's mini-timeline.
  3. Change the values at each point to create dynamic edge fades or morphing cutouts throughout your video.
