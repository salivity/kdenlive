Kdenlive Fullscreen Preview on Second Monitor

Using a dedicated secondary monitor for video playback in Kdenlive creates an efficient editing workspace. This guide explains how to designate your external display for preview output and map a custom keyboard shortcut to toggle fullscreen playback instantly.

Step 1: Designate the Secondary Monitor in Kdenlive

Before assigning a shortcut, configure Kdenlive to direct its fullscreen output to your external monitor.

  1. Open Kdenlive and navigate to Settings > Configure Kdenlive... in the top menu.
  2. In the configuration window, select the Playback tab from the left sidebar.
  3. Locate the Fullscreen output section.
  4. Open the dropdown menu and select your secondary monitor (e.g., Screen 2).
  5. Click Apply, then click OK to save the changes.

Step 2: Map the Fullscreen Toggle Shortcut

With the output destination set, assign a keyboard shortcut to toggle the monitor preview.

  1. Go to Settings > Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... in the top menu.
  2. In the search box at the top of the shortcut window, type fullscreen.
  3. Locate the appropriate monitor action in the results:
    • Project Monitor: Fullscreen (or Switch to Fullscreen / Fullscreen Output depending on your Kdenlive version).
  4. Select the action and choose the Custom radio button.
  5. Click the shortcut button (it will show None or the existing key) and press your preferred key combination on your keyboard (such as F12 or Ctrl + Shift + F).
  6. Click OK to apply the shortcut.

Step 3: Test and Use the Shortcut

Press your newly assigned shortcut key while editing. The Project Monitor feed will immediately switch to fullscreen on your designated secondary monitor without affecting the layout of your primary editing interface. Pressing the shortcut again will exit fullscreen mode.
