Customize Kdenlive Timeline Zoom and Pan

Navigating the Kdenlive timeline efficiently is crucial for a fast video editing workflow. This article provides a straightforward guide on how to customize your mouse scroll wheel behavior for zooming and panning within the Kdenlive timeline, allowing you to tailor the editing interface to your personal preferences.

Default Kdenlive Mouse Scroll Behaviors

By default, Kdenlive maps specific timeline navigation functions to the mouse scroll wheel combined with modifier keys:

  • Vertical Scroll: Scrolling the wheel up or down moves the timeline vertically through the audio and video tracks.
  • Horizontal Scroll (Panning): Holding Shift while scrolling the wheel pans the timeline left and right along the timecode.
  • Zooming In/Out: Holding Ctrl while scrolling the wheel zooms into or out of the timeline, centering the zoom on the current position of your mouse cursor.

How to Customize Timeline Navigation Settings

If you want to alter how Kdenlive handles these inputs, you can adjust the settings directly in the configuration menu.

  1. Open the Configuration Window:
    • On Windows or Linux, go to the top menu and select Settings > Configure Kdenlive.
    • On macOS, select Kdenlive > Preferences from the menu bar.
  2. Navigate to Timeline Settings:
    • In the left-hand sidebar of the configuration window, click on the Timeline category.
  3. Adjust Mouse and Zoom Preferences:
    • Within the Timeline settings tab, look for the navigation and zoom options. Here you can toggle preferences such as how the zoom behaves relative to the timeline playhead or mouse cursor.
    • You can also check or uncheck options regarding horizontal scrolling and scroll speed sensitivity depending on your operating system and mouse drivers.
  4. Apply Changes:
    • Click Apply and then OK to save your new settings.

Changing Keyboard and Mouse Shortcuts

For advanced customization, you can remap the shortcuts associated with panning and zooming:

  1. Go to Settings > Configure Keyboard Shortcuts (or Configure Shortcuts).
  2. In the search bar at the top of the dialog, type Zoom or Pan.
  3. Select the action you wish to change (such as "Zoom In" or "Zoom Out").
  4. Click on the custom shortcut option to assign a new key combination or mouse click modifier to the action.
  5. Click OK to save the changes.

Using the Middle Mouse Button for Fast Panning

In addition to using the scroll wheel, you can pan across the Kdenlive timeline by clicking and holding the Middle Mouse Button (pressing down on the scroll wheel itself) and dragging your mouse left or right. This allows you to bypass modifier keys entirely when navigating large projects.
