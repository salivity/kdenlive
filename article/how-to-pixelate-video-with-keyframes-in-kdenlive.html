How to Pixelate Video with Keyframes in Kdenlive

Obscuring confidential information in Kdenlive involves isolating a specific region of your video, applying a pixelate filter, and using keyframes to ensure the blur follows moving objects throughout the clip. This step-by-step guide explains how to set up your timeline tracks, apply the necessary effects, and animate the masked area with keyframes for complete privacy protection.

Step 1: Duplicate the Video Track

  1. Import your video footage into your Kdenlive project and place it on track V1.
  2. Right-click on the track header area and choose Insert Track if you do not already have an empty video track above it (V2).
  3. Copy the clip from V1 and paste it onto V2, ensuring that both clips are aligned at the exact same start and end timestamps.

Step 2: Apply the Pixelate Effect

  1. Select the top video clip on V2.
  2. Open the Effects tab and search for Pixelize (or Blur if you prefer a smooth blur over a blocky pixel effect).
  3. Drag and drop the Pixelize effect onto the clip on V2.
  4. In the Effect Stack panel, adjust the Pixel Width and Pixel Height sliders until the confidential data is completely unreadable.

Step 3: Isolate the Confidential Area with a Mask

  1. With the top clip on V2 still selected, go back to the Effects panel and search for Alpha Shapes (or Rotoscoping for complex/custom shapes).
  2. Drag and drop the Alpha Shapes effect onto the V2 clip, placing it directly below the Pixelize effect in the Effect Stack.
  3. In the Alpha Shapes settings, change the Shape parameter to Rectangle or Ellipse depending on the object you want to conceal.
  4. Adjust the Size X, Size Y, Position X, and Position Y parameters so the pixelated area covers only the sensitive information.

Step 4: Animate the Mask Using Keyframes

  1. In the Effect Stack under the Alpha Shapes (or Rotoscoping) properties, locate the keyframe timeline ruler.
  2. Move the playhead to the first frame where the confidential information appears, and ensure a starting keyframe is placed.
  3. Advance the playhead forward by several frames or seconds using the timeline controls.
  4. Adjust the Position X and Position Y sliders to reposition the mask over the target. Kdenlive will automatically create a new keyframe at the current playhead position.
  5. Repeat this process across the duration of the clip, adjusting positions as necessary to ensure the mask accurately tracks the moving data.

Step 5: Review the Result

  1. Play back the video from the beginning of the sequence in the Clip/Project Monitor.
  2. Verify that the pixelated zone remains anchored over the confidential area at all times and that no unblurred frames expose sensitive details.
