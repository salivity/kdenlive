How to Render Timecodes in Kdenlive with Dynamic Text

Burning a visible timecode onto your video draft is essential for client reviews, frame-accurate feedback, and editorial collaboration. This guide walks you through applying and configuring Kdenlive's Dynamic Text effect to display source and project timecodes clearly across your footage before exporting your final review copy.

Step 1: Choose Where to Apply the Effect

Depending on whether you need the timeline timecode or individual source clip timecodes, choose your target:

  • For Project/Timeline Timecode: Add an Adjustment Layer (or Color Clip) on the topmost video track spanning the length of your edit, or apply the effect directly to the master output track.
  • For Source Clip Timecodes: Apply the effect directly to individual video clips on the timeline to display their specific native recorded timecodes.

Step 2: Add the Dynamic Text Effect

  1. Select your target clip, track, or adjustment layer in the timeline.
  2. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  3. Search for Dynamic Text in the search bar.
  4. Drag and drop the Dynamic Text effect onto your selected element.

Step 3: Configure the Timecode Parameters

Open the Effect/Composition Stack panel to adjust the Dynamic Text settings:

  1. Text Field: Enter #timecode# into the text box (or select the Timecode preset from the dropdown menu if available in your Kdenlive version).
  2. Timecode Format: Choose your preferred display format (such as SMPTE: HH:MM:SS:FF or simple frame counts).
  3. Font & Size: Set a legible font and adjust the font size so the numbers remain readable on mobile screens and review monitors without obstructing the visual content.
  4. Color & Background:
    • Set the text color to White or Yellow.
    • Enable the Background option and set it to semi-transparent Black (e.g., 50% to 75% opacity) to ensure readability over both light and dark scenes.
  5. Positioning: Adjust the horizontal and vertical alignment sliders to position the timecode counter cleanly in the lower-third, upper-right, or bottom center of the frame.

Step 4: Preview and Export the Review Draft

  1. Play back your timeline to ensure the timecode updates smoothly with each passing frame.
  2. Open the Render dialog (Project > Render or Ctrl + Return).
  3. Select a lightweight review preset (such as standard MP4-H264/AAC) to keep the file size small for fast client uploads.
  4. Click Render to File to generate your timecoded review draft.
