How to Set In and Out Points in Kdenlive

Setting In and Out points (defining a clip zone) in Kdenlive's Clip Monitor allows editors to select precise segments of source footage before placing them onto the timeline. Utilizing keyboard shortcuts significantly speeds up this rough-cutting phase, eliminating the need for manual mouse trimming. This guide explains the exact keyboard shortcuts and steps required to mark, adjust, clear, and insert clip zones within Kdenlive.

1. Load the Clip into the Clip Monitor

Double-click any video or audio asset in the Project Bin to open it inside the Clip Monitor window.

2. Navigate Through the Footage

Use standard playback shortcuts to locate the start and end of the desired segment:

  • Spacebar: Play or Pause playback.
  • L: Play forward (press repeatedly to increase playback speed).
  • J: Play in reverse (press repeatedly to increase reverse speed).
  • K: Pause playback.
  • Left Arrow / Right Arrow: Step backward or forward by a single frame.
  • Shift + Left Arrow / Shift + Right Arrow: Step backward or forward by one second.

3. Mark the In and Out Points

  • Set In Point: Move the playhead to the frame where your selected footage should begin and press I.
  • Set Out Point: Move the playhead to the frame where your selected footage should end and press O.

The selected duration will now be highlighted as a colored zone along the Clip Monitor's playback bar.

4. Insert or Overwrite to the Timeline

Once your In and Out points are established, send the selected zone directly to the timeline using these shortcuts:

  • V: Insert the clip zone at the current timeline playhead position (moves existing timeline clips forward).
  • B: Overwrite the timeline at the current timeline playhead position.

5. Clear or Reset In and Out Points

To remove or reset your marked points:

  • Press Shift + I to clear the marked In point.
  • Press Shift + O to clear the marked Out point.
  • Press Ctrl + Shift + I or right-click the monitor scrubber to clear the entire zone.
