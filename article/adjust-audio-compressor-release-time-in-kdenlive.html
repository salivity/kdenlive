Adjust Audio Compressor Release Time in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to locate, apply, and configure the Audio Compressor effect in Kdenlive, focusing specifically on adjusting the release time parameter. Modifying the release time allows you to control how quickly compressed audio levels return to their original volume after falling below the set threshold, ensuring natural-sounding dynamic range across your timeline.

Step 1: Add the Audio Compressor Effect

  1. Select the audio clip or video clip with audio on your timeline.
  2. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  3. Search for Compressor or navigate to the Audio category.
  4. Drag and drop the Compressor effect onto your chosen clip or track.

Step 2: Access the Effect Stack

  1. Ensure the clip remains selected.
  2. Open the Effect/Composition Stack panel (found under View > Effect/Composition Stack).
  3. You will see the parameters for the Compressor effect, including Threshold, Ratio, Attack, Release, and Gain.

Step 3: Adjust the Release Time

  1. Locate the Release (or Release time) slider within the effect properties.
  2. Drag the slider or click into the numerical box to enter a specific value in milliseconds (ms).
  3. Short Release (e.g., 50 ms – 100 ms): The compressor quickly stops reducing volume once audio drops below the threshold. Use this for fast-paced audio or dynamic dialogue to prevent subsequent quiet words from being attenuated.
  4. Long Release (e.g., 200 ms – 500 ms+): The compressor slowly recovers volume, creating smooth transitions and avoiding rapid, distracting volume fluctuations (often referred to as "pumping").

Step 4: Preview and Fine-Tune

Play the timeline to monitor the audio changes in real time. Adjust the release time in combination with the threshold and attack settings until the compression smoothly blends with the background audio and natural dynamics of the clip.
