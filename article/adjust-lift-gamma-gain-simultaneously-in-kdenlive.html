Adjust Lift Gamma Gain Simultaneously in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to adjust the lift, gamma, and gain wheels together inside Kdenlive's color grading workspace. Adjusting these three tonal ranges simultaneously allows editors to quickly normalize exposure, apply cohesive color tints across an entire image, and maintain balanced contrast throughout shadows, midtones, and highlights without manually tweaking each wheel one by one.

Applying the Color Grading Effect

  1. Select your target clip on the Kdenlive timeline.
  2. Open the Effects tab in the sidebar (typically located alongside the Project Bin).
  3. Search for the Color Grading effect (or Color Wheels) under the Color and Image Correction category.
  4. Drag and drop the effect onto your selected timeline clip or double-click to apply it.

Accessing the Simultaneous Wheel Controls

Once the Color Grading effect is loaded in the Effect/Composition Stack panel, you will see three color wheels:

  • Lift: Controls dark areas and shadows.
  • Gamma: Controls middle gray values and midtones.
  • Gain: Controls bright areas and highlights.

To adjust these properties simultaneously:

  1. Use Master Sliders: Beneath or alongside each color wheel, locate the global/master brightness and luma sliders. Adjusting the master level under the primary control adjusts the baseline value across the spectrum.
  2. Lock/Link Parameter Toggle: Enable the Link or All chain icon located at the top-right corner of the Color Grading effect window. When toggled on, dragging the color picker point in one wheel will offset the chromatic values uniformly across the Lift, Gamma, and Gain wheels.
  3. Keyboard Modifiers: When dragging the central control point inside a color wheel, hold down the Shift key (or Ctrl depending on your OS configuration) to apply proportional adjustments across all three tonal ranges simultaneously.

Fine-Tuning and Monitoring

  • Open the Vectorscope and RGB Parade panels via View > Scopes to observe real-time signal changes across all color channels.
  • Use the numerical input boxes below the linked wheels for precision adjustments when mouse control is too coarse.
  • If a unified adjustment skews the shadows or highlights too heavily, unlink the wheels by clicking the chain icon again to refine individual Lift, Gamma, or Gain parameters separately.
