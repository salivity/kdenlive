Adjust Audio Gate High-Pass in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to locate and adjust the high-pass frequency setting within the Audio Gate effect in Kdenlive. Setting this parameter correctly ensures that low-end rumble and background hums do not accidentally trigger your audio gate, resulting in cleaner voiceovers and dialogue in your video projects.

Step 1: Apply the Audio Gate Effect

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and locate your audio or video clip on the timeline.
  2. Select the clip by clicking on it.
  3. Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  4. Search for Audio Gate or Gate in the audio effects category and drag it onto your selected clip.

Step 2: Open the Effect Stack

  1. Ensure the clip remains selected on the timeline.
  2. Switch to the Effect/Composition Stack panel (typically located in the properties area).
  3. Find the Audio Gate effect listed in the stack to view its adjustable parameters.

Step 3: Locate and Adjust the High-Pass Frequency

  1. In the Audio Gate control list, find the parameter labeled High-pass, HPF, or High-pass frequency.
  2. Adjust the slider or double-click the numerical box to manually enter a value in Hertz (Hz).
  3. Set the frequency to filter out unwanted low-end sounds (such as mic rumble or HVAC hum). For standard vocal tracks, setting this between 80 Hz and 150 Hz typically prevents low frequencies from opening the gate without thinning out the voice.

Step 4: Preview and Fine-Tune

  1. Play the timeline to monitor the audio in real time.
  2. Check that the gate opens cleanly when dialogue occurs and remains closed during silent or noisy background moments.
  3. Readjust the frequency value alongside the gate's Threshold and Release settings until the transition sounds natural.
