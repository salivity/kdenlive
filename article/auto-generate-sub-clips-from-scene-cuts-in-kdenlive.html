Auto Generate Sub-Clips from Scene Cuts in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to use Kdenlive's automatic scene detection feature to split continuous video footage into distinct sub-clips within the Project Bin. By utilizing this automated process, you can rapidly organize long takes, recorded streams, or pre-rendered footage into individual scene cuts without manually slicing media on the timeline.

Step 1: Import Your Source Video

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. Drag and drop your video file into the Project Bin, or click the Add Clip or Folder button.

Step 2: Open the Scene Detection Tool

  1. In the Project Bin, right-click the video clip you want to process.
  2. Hover over Clip Jobs in the context menu.
  3. Select Scene Detection.

Step 3: Configure Settings to Generate Sub-Clips

A dialog window will appear with options for cut detection and clip generation:

  • Cut scene into sub-clips: Check this box. This is the critical setting that instructs Kdenlive to automatically generate individual sub-clips in your Project Bin for every detected cut, rather than merely placing markers or cutting on the timeline.
  • Threshold / Sensitivity: Adjust the detection threshold depending on your footage:
    • A lower threshold (e.g., 10–15) increases sensitivity, which is ideal for subtle cuts or low-contrast scene changes, but it may cause false positives on fast camera pans.
    • A higher threshold (e.g., 25–35) is best for high-contrast, obvious scene transitions.
  • Minimum Scene Duration: Set the minimum number of frames required for a section to be considered a new scene. This prevents rapid flashing or lighting changes from creating tiny, unusable sub-clips.

Step 4: Run the Job

  1. Click the Start button in the Scene Detection window.
  2. You can monitor the progress of the analysis in the Job Queue panel.
  3. Once the process completes, look in your Project Bin. Kdenlive will have populated the bin with sequentially numbered sub-clips corresponding to each detected scene.

You can now drag these sub-clips directly to your timeline, rename them, or group them into bin folders for an organized editing workflow.
