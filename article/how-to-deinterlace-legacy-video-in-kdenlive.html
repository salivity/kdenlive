How to Deinterlace Legacy Video in Kdenlive

Legacy field-based video formats often produce visible horizontal "combing" artifacts when played on modern progressive displays. Kdenlive allows you to remove these artifacts by deinterlacing footage, converting interlaced fields into smooth, progressive frames. This guide covers how to configure clip properties for automatic deinterlacing and how to manually apply deinterlace filters directly to your timeline clips.

Method 1: Automatic Deinterlacing via Clip Properties

Kdenlive can handle deinterlacing at the source level before you even begin editing.

  1. Import the Video: Add your legacy interlaced video file to the Project Bin.
  2. Open Clip Properties: Right-click the clip in the Project Bin and select Clip Properties.
  3. Set Field Order: Navigate to the Properties tab (the filmstrip icon). Under the Scanning or Interlace settings:
    • Select Top Field First (tff) or Bottom Field First (bff), matching your source media (DV footage is typically Bottom Field First; standard HD interlaced is typically Top Field First).
  4. Apply Changes: Click OK. Kdenlive will now internally deinterlace the video frames to match your project’s progressive frame rate.

Method 2: Applying the Deinterlace Filter in the Timeline

If you prefer to apply deinterlacing as a discrete effect or need to tweak specific deinterlacing algorithms:

  1. Add Clip to Timeline: Drag your video from the Project Bin onto an active video track in the Timeline.
  2. Access Effects: Open the Effects panel (go to View > Effects if it is not visible).
  3. Search for Filter: In the search bar, type Deinterlace or Yadif.
  4. Apply to Clip: Drag the Deinterlace filter (often powered by Libavfilter/Yadif) directly onto your timeline clip.
  5. Adjust Parameters:
    • Select the clip to view its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
    • Mode: Choose between converting each field into a frame (double the frame rate) or generating one progressive frame per interlaced frame.
    • Field Order: Ensure the field order is set to match the original recording method (Top Field or Bottom Field).
    • Spatial/Temporal check: Enable auto-detection checks to minimize blur while suppressing combing lines.

Exporting as Progressive Video

  1. Click the Render button in the top toolbar (or press Ctrl + Return).
  2. Choose a modern progressive preset (such as MP4 - H.264/AAC).
  3. Ensure the render profile settings specify a progressive scanning format (denoted by p, e.g., 1080p or 576p, rather than i).
  4. Click Render to File to produce a clean, progressive video file free of interlacing artifacts.
