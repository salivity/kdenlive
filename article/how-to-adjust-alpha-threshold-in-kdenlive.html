How to Adjust Alpha Threshold in Kdenlive

Adjusting the alpha threshold of an imported logo graphic inside Kdenlive allows you to control transparency, eliminate semi-transparent fringe edges, and clean up matte borders. This guide explains how to import your logo, apply the appropriate alpha channel effects, and fine-tune threshold values directly on your timeline for a clean overlay.

Step 1: Add the Logo to the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your logo image file (such as a PNG or SVG with transparency) into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag and drop the logo onto a video track positioned above your background video footage.

Step 2: Apply an Alpha Control Effect

Kdenlive offers multiple tools within the Alpha, Mask and Keying effect category to manipulate transparency thresholds:

  • Method A: Using the Levels Effect (Recommended for Fine Control)

    1. Open the Effects tab.
    2. Search for Levels (located under Color and Image Correction).
    3. Drag and drop the Levels effect onto your logo clip in the timeline.
    4. In the Effect/Composition Stack panel, change the Channel dropdown from RGB or All to Alpha.

  • Method B: Using the Color Selection / Alpha Intensity Effect

    1. Search for Alpha Intensity or Color Selection in the Effects panel under Alpha, Mask and Keying.
    2. Apply the effect directly to the logo clip.

Step 3: Adjust the Alpha Threshold Values

Depending on the effect selected:

  • If using Levels (Alpha Channel):

    • Input Black Level: Increase this slider to make faint, semi-transparent pixels completely transparent (removes soft halos and artifact edges).
    • Input White Level: Decrease this slider to force semi-transparent pixels into full opacity.
    • Gamma: Shift the gamma slider left or right to adjust the transition sharpness between transparent and opaque areas.

  • If using Alpha Intensity / Color Selection:

    • Adjust the Threshold or Delta sliders until the desired level of transparency cutoff is achieved.
    • Adjust the Softness slider to avoid harsh pixelation along the edges of the logo.

Step 4: Preview the Output

Play the timeline in the Project Monitor against your background track to ensure the logo edges remain clean, sharp, and correctly blended without unintended transparency or jagged borders.
