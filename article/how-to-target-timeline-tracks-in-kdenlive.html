How to Target Timeline Tracks in Kdenlive

In Kdenlive, track target indicators determine exactly which video and audio tracks receive media when you paste clips or insert them from the project bin. By activating and directing these indicators within the timeline header, you can precisely route copied elements to specific tracks at the playhead's position without overwriting existing footage.

Understanding Track Target Indicators

Track target indicators are visual controls located in the timeline track headers on the far left side of the timeline interface. Each track header displays an indicator representing whether that specific track is currently targeted for incoming video or audio data. When an indicator is illuminated or highlighted, Kdenlive designates that track as an active destination for paste operations and keyboard insert commands.

How to Configure Track Targets

  1. Locate the Track Headers: Navigate to the left edge of your timeline where your video tracks (e.g., V1, V2) and audio tracks (e.g., A1, A2) are listed.
  2. Toggle the Target State: Click directly on the track name or the designated target box next to the track label. Clicking toggles the targeting state on or off.
  3. Set the Video Target: Click the target area for your preferred video track. The visual highlight confirms it is now the active video recipient.
  4. Set the Audio Target: Click the target area for your preferred audio track to ensure the audio stream of a copied clip routes to the correct audio lane.

Pasting Clips to the Targeted Tracks

  1. Select and copy your desired clip from the timeline or project bin using Ctrl + C (or Cmd + C on macOS).
  2. Move the timeline playhead cursor to the exact timestamp where you want the clip to begin.
  3. Confirm that the target indicators on your desired video and audio tracks are active.
  4. Press Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V) to paste the clip. The video portion will land on the targeted video track, and the audio portion will land on the targeted audio track, aligned with the playhead position.

Best Practices for Track Targeting

  • Separate Audio and Video: You can target different track pairings (such as Video 2 and Audio 1) to split elements without breaking timeline synchronization.
  • Avoid Unintended Overwrites: If a targeted track already contains media at the playhead position, ensure the timeline editing mode is set appropriately (such as Overwrite Mode or Insert Mode) to prevent accidental deletions or unwanted timeline shifts.
  • Locked Tracks: A track that is locked cannot be targeted or pasted into until it is unlocked.
