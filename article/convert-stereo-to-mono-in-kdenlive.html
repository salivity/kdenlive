Convert Stereo to Mono in Kdenlive

Converting a stereo audio track to mono in Kdenlive is a straightforward process that helps balance uneven audio channels or center a single-channel microphone input. This guide provides a clear, step-by-step walkthrough on how to find, apply, and configure the "Stereo to Mono" filter on your timeline clips in Kdenlive.

Step 1: Add Your Clip to the Timeline

Before applying any effects, ensure your video or audio clip is placed on the timeline.

  1. Drag your clip from the Project Bin down to an audio or video track on the timeline.
  2. If your audio is grouped with a video track, you can apply the effect directly to the combined clip, or right-click the clip and select Split Audio to work on the audio track independently.

Step 2: Locate the Stereo to Mono Effect

Kdenlive features a dedicated audio effects library where you can find the channel conversion tools.

  1. Navigate to the Effects tab (usually located in the top-left or bottom-left pane of the default interface). If you cannot see it, enable it by going to the top menu and selecting View > Effects.
  2. In the search bar of the Effects tab, type "Stereo to Mono".
  3. Alternatively, you can find it manually by navigating through the category folders: Audio > Channels > Stereo to Mono.

Step 3: Apply the Effect to Your Audio

Once you have located the effect, you need to apply it to your specific timeline clip.

  1. Click and hold the Stereo to Mono effect from the list.
  2. Drag and drop the effect directly onto the audio clip in your timeline.
  3. A small icon or green line will appear on the clip, indicating that an effect has been successfully applied.

Step 4: Configure the Effect Settings

After applying the filter, you can choose how the stereo channels are merged or split.

  1. Click on the audio clip in the timeline to select it.
  2. Look at the Properties or Effect/Composition Stack panel (typically on the right or next to the Effects tab).
  3. Under the Stereo to Mono effect settings, you will see options to customize the output. You can choose to:
    • Use only the Left Channel (duplicates the left channel to both sides).
    • Use only the Right Channel (duplicates the right channel to both sides).
    • Average both channels (mixes left and right together equally into a single mono signal).
  4. Select the option that best fits your audio source. Play back the timeline to preview the change and ensure your audio is balanced.
