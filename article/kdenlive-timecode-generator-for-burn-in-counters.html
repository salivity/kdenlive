Kdenlive Timecode Generator for Burn-In Counters

The Timecode generator in Kdenlive allows video editors to overlay a dynamic, frame-accurate numeric counter directly onto footage, creating what the post-production industry calls a "burn-in" timecode (BITC). This article explains the primary functions of this tool, how it enhances the client and team review process, and the practical advantages of applying reference counters to draft sequences.

Providing Frame-Accurate Feedback

During the review and approval stages of video production, clear communication is essential. Without a visible counter, clients and collaborators often provide vague timestamps based on web player interfaces, which can vary depending on player buffering, frame rates, or user error. The Timecode generator burns the exact timeline or source time (hours, minutes, seconds, and frames) into the image, enabling reviewers to cite specific frames (e.g., 01:04:12:18) when requesting edits, visual effects fixes, or audio adjustments.

Timeline vs. Source Clip Tracking

Kdenlive’s Timecode filter provides the flexibility to display different time references depending on the workflow requirements:

  • Sequence Timecode: Displays the master project timeline position, ensuring that all feedback corresponds to the global edit structure.
  • Source Timecode: References the original metadata of individual media clips, which is particularly useful for assistant editors, sound designers, and colorists who need to trace specific shots back to raw camera files.

Customization and Legibility

To ensure the reference counter is readable against varying backgrounds without completely obscuring the visual content, the generator includes several formatting options:

  • Positioning and Scaling: Editors can place the counter in unobtrusive areas, such as the bottom-right corner or lower third of the screen.
  • Background Shading: A customizable background box with variable opacity can be added behind the numbers to maintain high contrast over bright or busy scenes.
  • Display Formats: The generator supports various standard frame rates (e.g., 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 60 fps) to match the project's delivery standards.

Non-Destructive Master Track Workflow

The most efficient way to utilize the Timecode generator for review exports in Kdenlive is by applying it directly to the Master video track or an adjustment clip. This approach displays the counter across the entire sequence automatically, avoiding the need to add the effect to individual cuts. Once the review cycle is complete and the final delivery is ready, the effect can be disabled or deleted from the Master track with a single click, leaving the clean, final edit untouched.
