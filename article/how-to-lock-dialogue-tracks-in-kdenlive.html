How to Lock Dialogue Tracks in Kdenlive

During complex montage editing, rapid cutting and rearranging of video and sound effects can easily disrupt or desynchronize your core dialogue. Locking your dialogue audio tracks in Kdenlive protects them from unintended edits, splits, moves, and deletions, ensuring your spoken audio remains perfectly aligned while you freely manipulate other timeline elements.

Step 1: Organize Your Dialogue on Dedicated Tracks

Before locking, ensure all primary dialogue clips are placed on dedicated audio tracks (such as Audio 1 and Audio 2). Keeping dialogue separate from ambient sound, Foley, and background music makes track management straightforward and prevents unnecessary locking of tracks you still need to edit.

Step 2: Enable the Track Lock

  1. Locate the Track Header area on the far-left side of the timeline.
  2. Find the audio track containing your dialogue.
  3. Click the Padlock icon in the track header.

When the icon changes to a closed padlock and the track dims slightly with a diagonal line pattern across the clips, the track is fully locked.

Step 3: Verify Edit Restrictions

Once locked, Kdenlive applies the following safeguards to the entire track:

  • Razor Tool (Cut): Splitting clips using Shift + R or the Razor tool will ignore locked tracks, preventing accidental cuts to spoken audio.
  • Spacer Tool: Using the Spacer tool to insert gaps will shift clips on unlocked tracks while keeping dialogue locked in its absolute timeline position.
  • Ripple Delete: Deleting clips with ripple edit functions will not pull or displace locked dialogue.
  • Selection and Movement: Locked clips cannot be dragged, trimmed, or deleted directly.

Step 4: Work Safely in Complex Montages

With dialogue locked, you can freely perform multi-track ripple edits, slip edits, and rapid b-roll placement on your video and secondary audio tracks. If you need to make volume keyframe adjustments, apply audio effects, or adjust dialogue timing, click the padlock icon again to temporarily unlock the track, make your modifications, and re-lock the track immediately.
