How to Use the Spacer Tool in Kdenlive

The Spacer Tool in Kdenlive is an essential timeline utility designed to quickly move large sections of media elements simultaneously without disrupting the relative positioning of your project. This guide explains the core functions of the Spacer Tool, how to activate it, and how it simplifies managing gaps, adding new footage, and maintaining multi-track synchronization across your editing timeline.

Primary Function of the Spacer Tool

In video editing, inserting new clips or extending existing media often requires shifting all subsequent footage further down the timeline. Doing this manually by selecting individual clips can lead to accidental misalignments, broken cuts, or out-of-sync audio.

The Spacer Tool solves this problem by acting as a dynamic barrier. When activated, it selects every element—including video clips, audio tracks, titles, transitions, and keyframed effects—that sits to the right of your cursor, allowing you to drag them collectively as a single block.

Key Capabilities

  • Creating Timeline Space: By clicking on an empty area or clip and dragging to the right, you can instantly open up a gap across your tracks to insert new B-roll, dialogue, or title sequences.
  • Closing Gaps: Dragging to the left pulls all downstream media forward to close empty spaces left by deleted footage, maintaining the pacing without ripple-delete errors.
  • Preserving Multi-Track Sync: The tool shifts elements across all unlocked tracks concurrently, ensuring that background music, sound effects, cutaways, and main footage stay strictly aligned.

How to Use the Spacer Tool

  1. Activate the Tool: Select the Spacer Tool from the timeline toolbar or press the default keyboard shortcut M.
  2. Choose a Starting Point: Hover the cursor over the specific point on the timeline where you want the shift to begin.
  3. Click and Drag: Click the left mouse button and drag right to insert space or left to close space.
  4. Target Specific Tracks: By default, the Spacer Tool affects all unlocked tracks. You can lock specific tracks to exclude them from the move, or use modifier keys like Shift (depending on your configuration) to restrict movement to a single track.
  5. Switch Back: Press S to return to the standard Selection Tool once your clips are repositioned.
