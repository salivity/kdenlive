Kdenlive: How to Set Multiply Track Compositing

This guide explains how to configure video track compositing in Kdenlive and apply the "Multiply" blend mode to darken composite overlays. By combining multi-track layering with blend modes, you can seamlessly integrate dark textures, shadows, and grading elements over your base footage.

Step 1: Arrange Your Video Tracks

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and locate the Timeline.
  2. Place your base background video onto a lower video track (e.g., V1).
  3. Place the overlay video or image (such as a grunge texture, vignette, or darkening matte) onto the video track directly above it (e.g., V2).

Step 2: Add a Composition Between the Tracks

To blend the upper track with the lower track, you must apply a composition effect between them:

  1. Hover your cursor over the bottom edge or bottom-left corner of the clip on the upper track (V2) until the pointer changes to a composition icon.
  2. Click to add a composition, or right-click the clip on V2 and select Insert Composition > Composite and Transform (or Cairo Blend).
  3. Ensure the composition spans the full duration of the clip by dragging its edge to match the clip length.

Step 3: Change the Blend Mode to Multiply

  1. Click on the composition block on your timeline to open its settings in the Properties panel.
  2. In the Compositing (or Blend Mode) dropdown menu, change the selection from Normal to Multiply.
  3. The preview monitor will update immediately: the white and bright areas of the overlay clip will become transparent, while the dark areas will multiply with the underlying footage, creating a darkening overlay.

Step 4: Fine-Tune the Overlay Intensity

  1. In the same Properties panel, locate the Opacity slider.
  2. Lower the opacity from 100% to reduce the intensity of the darkening effect if the result is too harsh.
  3. If using Composite and Transform, you can also adjust the position, scale, and keyframes to animate the overlay across the base clip.
