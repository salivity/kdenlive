Set Monitor Overlay Shortcuts in Kdenlive

Customizing keyboard shortcuts in Kdenlive significantly speeds up your editing workflow by giving you instant access to essential framing tools. This guide provides a direct, step-by-step walkthrough on how to map custom hotkeys to cycle through and toggle monitor overlays—such as grids, guides, and safe zones—directly within Kdenlive.

Step 1: Open the Keyboard Shortcuts Menu

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open any project.
  2. Click on Settings in the top menu bar.
  3. Select Configure Keyboard Shortcuts... from the dropdown menu (or press Ctrl + Alt + , on Linux/Windows).

Step 2: Search for Monitor Overlay Actions

  1. In the shortcut configuration window, locate the Search bar at the top.
  2. Type overlay or grid to filter the available commands.
  3. Look for the specific monitor control actions. Depending on your version of Kdenlive, you will see actions such as:
    • Monitor Overlay: Toggles or cycles through the visual guide options.
    • Toggle Grid: Switches the alignment grid on and off.
    • Toggle Safe Zones: Enables or disables title and action safe areas.

Step 3: Assign Your Custom Shortcut

  1. Click on the action you wish to map (e.g., Monitor Overlay or Next Overlay).
  2. Select the Custom radio button in the bottom section of the window.
  3. Click the shortcut input button (which defaults to "None" if unassigned).
  4. Press the key combination on your keyboard that you want to use (for example, Alt + G or Shift + O).
  5. If the shortcut is already assigned to another command, Kdenlive will prompt a conflict warning. Choose a different combination or reassign the existing one.

Step 4: Apply and Test

  1. Click OK to save your new key mapping.
  2. Focus on either the Clip Monitor or Project Monitor.
  3. Press your newly assigned shortcut repeatedly to cycle through the available overlays or toggle them on and off.
