How to Deinterlace Video in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to remove combing artifacts from legacy interlaced video using the deinterlacing tools in Kdenlive. When working with analog captures, DV footage, or broadcast recordings, interlacing manifests as horizontal jagged lines during motion. By correctly configuring your project profile, setting the clip properties, and selecting the appropriate deinterlace algorithm, you can convert interlaced fields into smooth, progressive video frames.

Understanding Interlacing and Combing

Interlaced video stores a single frame as two alternating sets of lines: odd lines (Field 1) and even lines (Field 2), recorded at slightly different moments in time. When viewed on modern progressive displays, moving objects display a horizontal jagged pattern known as combing. Deinterlacing combines or interpolates these two fields to create a single progressive frame without visual tearing.

Step 1: Set a Progressive Project Profile

To prevent Kdenlive from outputting interlaced video, ensure your project timeline is set to a progressive profile:

  1. Go to Project > Project Settings.
  2. Under the Profiles tab, choose a resolution that matches your source video, ensuring the scan mode is set to Progressive (for example, DV/DVD PAL Progressive or Custom Progressive).
  3. Click OK to apply the settings.

Step 2: Configure Clip-Level Deinterlacing

Kdenlive handles deinterlacing most effectively at the source clip level before effects are applied:

  1. Import your interlaced footage into the Project Bin.
  2. Right-click the video clip and select Clip Properties.
  3. Navigate to the Properties (or Video) tab.
  4. Locate the Scanning or Field Order setting:
    • Bottom Field First (BFF): Typically used for standard DV/DVCAM footage.
    • Top Field First (TFF): Typically used for analog VHS captures, DVD video, and high-definition broadcast footage.
  5. Enable the Deinterlace checkbox if it is not automatically detected.

Setting the correct field order is critical; selecting the wrong field order causes motion to appear jittery or stutter backward.

Step 3: Select Deinterlacing Algorithms

Kdenlive uses the MLT multimedia framework and FFmpeg libraries to process video. Depending on your installation, several deinterlacing modes are available:

  • Yadif (Yet Another Deinterlacing Filter): The recommended general-purpose algorithm. It analyzes spatial and temporal data to reconstruct edges without significant loss of detail.
  • BWDIF (Bob Weaver Deinterlacing Filter): A modern, high-quality motion-adaptive deinterlacer that minimizes motion blur and edge flickering better than standard filters.
  • Linear / Blend: Combines both fields together into one frame. This completely eliminates jagged edges but introduces double-image ghosting during fast motion.
  • Bob (Spatial/Temporal): Scales each individual field into a full frame. It maintains high temporal resolution but can cause vertical line crawling or shaking.

To force a specific filter, search for the Deinterlace or Yadif effect in the Effects tab and drag it onto your clip in the timeline, then select your preferred processing mode from the effect properties.

Step 4: Rendering the Progressive Output

Once your video looks clean in the preview monitor, export the video:

  1. Click the Render button (or press Ctrl + Return).
  2. Choose a modern progressive format such as MP4 (H.264/AAC) or WebM.
  3. Ensure the scanning option in the render settings is explicitly set to Progressive.
  4. Click Render to File.
