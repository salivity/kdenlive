Adjust High Shelf in Kdenlive Parametric Equalizer

This guide explains how to configure the high-frequency shelf using the Parametric Equalizer audio effect in Kdenlive. Adjusting the high-shelf filter allows you to boost high-end clarity and brightness or reduce harshness, hiss, and sibilance from your audio tracks, giving your final video a polished, professional sound.

Step 1: Apply the Parametric Equalizer Effect

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and select the audio clip on the timeline (or select an entire track).
  2. Go to the Effects tab.
  3. Search for Parametric Equalizer in the search bar.
  4. Drag and drop the effect onto your target audio clip or track.

Step 2: Open the Effect Properties

  1. Make sure the clip or track is selected so that the Properties panel displays the effect settings.
  2. Inside the Parametric Equalizer window, locate the equalization bands. The high-shelf filter is typically assigned to the highest band (Band 4 or the High band, depending on the equalizer version).

Step 3: Configure the High-Shelf Parameters

To adjust how the high-frequency shelf affects your audio, modify the following parameters for the high band:

  • Filter Type: Ensure the filter type for the high band is set to High Shelf (or Shelving).
  • Frequency (Hz): Set the corner frequency where the shelf begins to take effect. For brightening vocals or adding air, values between 8,000 Hz (8 kHz) and 12,000 Hz (12 kHz) are commonly used. To tame harsh high frequencies, set the threshold closer to 5,000 Hz (5 kHz) to 7,000 Hz (7 kHz).
  • Gain (dB): Adjust the slider or input box to increase or decrease the volume of all frequencies above the selected cutoff point:
    • Positive Gain (+dB): Adds presence, sparkle, and top-end clarity.
    • Negative Gain (-dB): Reduces harshness, background hiss, and sharp "s" sounds.
  • Q / Slope (if available): Adjust the Q factor to control the steepness of the shelf transition. A lower value creates a gentle, natural transition, while a higher value creates a steeper slope.

Step 4: Preview and Fine-Tune

Play the timeline to listen to your audio adjustments in real time. Toggle the effect on and off using the enable/disable checkbox in the properties pane to compare the processed audio against the original track, adjusting the Gain and Frequency until you achieve a balanced mix.
