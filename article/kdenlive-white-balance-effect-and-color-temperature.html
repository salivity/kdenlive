Kdenlive White Balance Effect and Color Temperature

This article explains the purpose of the "White Balance" effect tool in the Kdenlive video editor and how its color temperature slider works. You will learn how this tool corrects color casts caused by different lighting sources, how the color temperature slider adjusts the warm and cool tones of your footage, and how to apply these adjustments to achieve natural-looking colors or creative visual styles in your video projects.

Purpose of the White Balance Effect

The primary purpose of the White Balance effect in Kdenlive is to ensure that colors in your video look natural and accurate to the human eye. Cameras often capture colors incorrectly because they do not adjust to different light sources (such as sunlight, fluorescent bulbs, or tungsten lights) the way human eyes do. This results in an unnatural color cast, making the footage look too orange (warm) or too blue (cool).

By applying the White Balance effect, you tell Kdenlive what "true white" or neutral gray should look like in your video. Once the software establishes this reference point, it automatically shifts all other colors in the image to correct the overall color balance.

The Color Temperature Slider

The color temperature slider is the main control within Kdenlive's White Balance effect. It allows you to manually adjust the warmth or coolness of your footage to compensate for the lighting conditions under which it was shot.

  • Correcting Cool/Blue Casts (Warm Up): If your video was shot on an overcast day or in the shade, it may have a cold, blue tint. Dragging the color temperature slider toward the warmer end (yellow/orange) adds warmth to the image, neutralizing the blue tint to make skin tones and environments look natural.
  • Correcting Warm/Orange Casts (Cool Down): If your video was shot indoors under incandescent or warm LED lighting, it may have a strong yellow or orange tint. Dragging the slider toward the cooler end (blue) introduces cool tones, neutralizing the excess orange and restoring natural color balance.

How to Use the Tool in Kdenlive

To use the White Balance tool effectively in Kdenlive:

  1. Search for White Balance in the Effects tab and drag it onto your video clip in the timeline.
  2. Use the Neutral Color color picker (eyedropper tool) in the effect options. Click on an area in your video that should be a neutral white or gray (such as a white shirt, a piece of paper, or concrete). This will automatically adjust the settings to establish a baseline.
  3. Fine-tune the results using the Color Temperature slider. If the automatic correction is slightly too warm or too cold, nudge the slider left or right until the colors look correct.
  4. Optionally, use the green/tint slider if you need to correct green or magenta color casts often caused by fluorescent lighting.
