How to Use Fade to Black Buttons in Kdenlive

In Kdenlive, you can quickly fade your video clips to and from black without manually searching for effects in the effects library. This guide explains how to use the built-in timeline clip handles—also known as quick fade buttons—to apply, adjust, and remove "Fade from Black" (fade-in) and "Fade to Black" (fade-out) transitions directly on your timeline.

Locating the Fade Handles

Kdenlive includes interactive fade handles directly on the corners of every video and audio clip placed on the timeline.

  1. Add your video clip to the timeline.
  2. Move your mouse cursor to the top-left or top-right corner of the clip.
  3. A small green or red circular handle will appear at the corner when your cursor hovers over it.

Applying "Fade from Black" (Fade-In)

To make a clip start as black and gradually reveal the video:

  1. Hover your cursor over the top-left corner of the clip until the circular handle appears.
  2. Click and hold the handle.
  3. Drag the handle to the right along the clip.
  4. Release the mouse button when you reach the desired duration. A shaded triangular overlay will appear on the clip, indicating the active fade duration.

Applying "Fade to Black" (Fade-Out)

To make a clip gradually transition into black at its end:

  1. Hover your cursor over the top-right corner of the clip until the circular handle appears.
  2. Click and hold the handle.
  3. Drag the handle to the left into the clip.
  4. Release the mouse button at your chosen duration. A matching shaded triangle will appear on the tail end of the clip.

Adjusting or Removing the Fade

  • Adjust Duration: Click and drag the existing handle left or right to increase or decrease the fade length.
  • Remove the Fade: Drag the handle all the way back to the outer edge of the clip until the shaded triangle disappears.
  • Fine-Tuning via Effects Panel: Select the clip and open the Effects (or Properties) panel. You will see the "Fade from Black" or "Fade to Black" effect listed, where you can type exact frame counts or timecodes for precise timing.
