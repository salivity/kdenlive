Adjust Low-Shelf Gain in Kdenlive Master Equalizer

This guide explains how to locate, apply, and adjust the low-shelf gain setting using the Master Equalizer audio effect in Kdenlive. By modifying the low-shelf gain, you can boost or attenuate the lowest audio frequencies to either add warmth and bass punch or eliminate low-frequency rumble from your project.

Step 1: Apply the Master Equalizer Effect

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and select the audio clip or audio track you want to modify.
  2. Open the Effects tab (if not visible, enable it via View > Effects).
  3. Search for Master Equalizer in the search bar.
  4. Drag and drop the effect onto your selected audio clip or onto the master audio track header.

Step 2: Open the Effect Stack

  1. Select the clip containing the effect.
  2. Open the Effect Stack or Properties panel to view the controls for the Master Equalizer.

Step 3: Adjust the Low-Shelf Gain

  1. Locate the Low-Shelf (often represented as the first band or labelled specifically as Low Shelf/Band 1) parameter controls in the effect settings.
  2. Find the Gain slider or numerical input box for this band.
  3. To boost bass: Drag the Gain slider to the right or enter a positive decibel (dB) value (e.g., +3.0 dB).
  4. To cut/reduce bass: Drag the Gain slider to the left or enter a negative decibel (dB) value (e.g., -6.0 dB).
  5. Adjust the corresponding Frequency value (commonly set between 80 Hz and 200 Hz) to determine the threshold where the shelf filter begins to take effect.

Step 4: Preview the Audio

  1. Play your project timeline to monitor the changes in real-time.
  2. Make fine adjustments to the gain and frequency parameters until the low frequencies achieve the desired balance.
