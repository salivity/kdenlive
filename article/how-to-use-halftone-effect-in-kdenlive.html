How to Use Halftone Effect in Kdenlive

This guide demonstrates how to apply and customize the Halftone visual effect in Kdenlive to give your videos a stylized, retro comic-book print appearance. You will learn where to find the effect, how to adjust its parameters for the best look, and how to combine it with other filters to maximize the pop-art aesthetic.

Step 1: Add Your Video to the Timeline

  1. Launch Kdenlive and import your video footage into the Project Bin.
  2. Drag and drop the video clip onto the timeline where you want to apply the effect.

Step 2: Locate and Apply the Halftone Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (if you do not see it, go to the top menu and select View > Effects).
  2. In the search bar of the Effects panel, type Halftone.
  3. Drag the Halftone effect from the list and drop it directly onto your video clip in the timeline.

Step 3: Customize the Halftone Settings

Once applied, select your clip in the timeline to view the effect's parameters in the Properties (or Effect/Composition Stack) panel. Adjust the following settings to get the classic comic-book print look:

  • Cell Size / Dot Size: This slider controls how large the halftone dots are. A smaller size retains more detail, while a larger size creates a bolder, more stylized retro print look.
  • Angle: Adjusts the angle of the dot grid. Setting the grid to 45 degrees is the industry standard for traditional comic book printing.
  • Colors / Mode: You can choose between a full-color CMYK halftone (similar to vintage color comic books) or a monochrome/grayscale halftone (resembling classic manga or newspaper print).

Step 4: Enhance the Comic-Book Look (Optional)

To make the comic-book style more convincing, you can layer additional effects over the halftone:

  • Edge Detection / Cartoon: Search for the Cartoon or Edge effect in the Effects panel and place it above the Halftone effect in the stack. This adds thick, black ink outlines to your subjects.
  • Posterize: Apply the Posterize effect to reduce the color steps in your video, making the color gradients flatter and more cartoon-like.
  • Contrast: Increase the contrast of your clip using the Color Correction effects to make the halftone dots pop against the background.
