How to Apply Vignette Effect in Kdenlive

Adding a vignette effect in Kdenlive allows you to draw focus to the center of your footage by darkening or altering the outer edges of the frame. This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough on how to locate the Vignette filter in Kdenlive, apply it to a video track, and adjust the inner and outer radius parameters to customize the boundary and softness of the effect.

Step 1: Add Your Clip to the Timeline

  1. Launch Kdenlive and open your project.
  2. Drag your video or image clip from the Project Bin onto a video track in the Timeline.
  3. Click on the clip in the timeline to select it.

Step 2: Locate the Vignette Effect

  1. Open the Effects panel (if not visible, go to View > Effects in the top menu).
  2. Use the search bar at the top of the Effects panel and type Vignette.
  3. Locate the Vignette filter in the search results (typically found under the Stylize or Alpha / Mask categories).

Step 3: Apply the Filter

  1. Drag and drop the Vignette effect directly onto the selected clip on the timeline, or double-click the effect while the clip is selected.
  2. The effect will now appear in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.

Step 4: Customize Inner and Outer Radius Boundaries

  1. In the Effect/Composition Stack panel, locate the sliders for the Vignette settings.
  2. Inner Radius: Adjust this value to define the central area where the image remains untouched. A higher value keeps more of the center clear before the darkening begins.
  3. Outer Radius: Adjust this value to define the boundary where the vignette reaches maximum darkness or opacity. Setting this farther from the inner radius creates a smoother, more gradual blend.
  4. Softness / Smoothness: Adjust any available smooth falloff controls to prevent harsh visible lines between the inner and outer boundaries.

Step 5: Preview and Refine

  1. Play back the video in the Project Monitor to check the intensity and placement of the vignette.
  2. Make minor tweaks to the radius values as needed until the desired depth and focus are achieved.
