How to Change Workspace Themes in Kdenlive

Kdenlive offers a highly customizable interface, allowing video editors to switch between different workspace layouts and color themes to suit their workflow and editing environment. This guide provides a direct, step-by-step walkthrough on how to change both the visual color theme of the interface and the functional workspace layouts in Kdenlive.

Changing the Color Theme

To change the overall visual style and color scheme of the Kdenlive interface, follow these steps:

  1. Open Kdenlive.
  2. Click on Settings in the top menu bar.
  3. Hover your cursor over the Color Scheme option.
  4. Select your preferred theme from the drop-down list (such as Breeze Dark, Breeze Light, or High Contrast). The interface will instantly update to the selected color theme.

Switching Workspace Layouts

Kdenlive includes several pre-configured workspace layouts designed for different stages of the video editing process, such as logging footage, editing, mixing audio, or color grading. To switch between these workspaces:

  1. Click on View in the top menu bar.
  2. Hover over Workspaces.
  3. Select the workspace layout that fits your current task:
    • Logging: Optimized for importing and organizing your source footage.
    • Editing: The standard layout for cutting and arranging clips on the timeline.
    • Audio: Maximizes audio meters and mixing tools.
    • Effects: Focuses on the effects stack and effect library.
    • Color: Prioritizes video scopes and color correction tools.

Alternatively, you can switch between these workspaces quickly by using the dedicated workspace tabs located in the top-right corner of the Kdenlive window.

Saving a Custom Workspace

If you arrange the interface windows to your liking and want to save it as a custom theme:

  1. Arrange the docks and windows by dragging and dropping them into your preferred positions.
  2. Go to View > Workspaces.
  3. Click on Save Workspace As....
  4. Enter a name for your custom layout and click OK. Your custom layout will now appear in the workspace list for easy access.
