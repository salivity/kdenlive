Export Video with Transparent Background in Kdenlive

Exporting a video with a transparent background (alpha channel) in Kdenlive is a simple process that requires choosing the correct render profile. This guide provides a direct, step-by-step walkthrough on how to prepare your timeline and configure your export settings to successfully output transparent video files using formats like WebM or QuickTime.

Step 1: Prepare Your Timeline

Ensure that there are no background elements, color clips, or solid tracks underneath the footage, text, or animation you want to keep. In Kdenlive, any empty space on the timeline automatically defaults to transparent. If you see the black preview background behind your assets, it will render as transparent as long as you select the correct export settings.

Step 2: Open the Render Menu

Once your project is ready to export, open the Render window:

  • Click the Render button in the top toolbar, or
  • Use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Enter (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Enter (Mac).

Step 3: Select an Alpha Preset

To retain transparency, you must use a video codec that supports an alpha channel. In the Render dialog window:

  1. Look at the left-hand column containing the preset categories.
  2. Scroll down and expand the Alpha category.
  3. Choose one of the following formats based on your needs:
    • WebM-VP9/Vorbis (alpha): Best for web use, online uploads, and streaming software like OBS Studio. It offers small file sizes with high quality.
    • QuickTime (QT Animation/Alpha): Best if you plan to import the transparent video into another video editing software. This format is lossless but results in larger file sizes.

Step 4: Export the Video

After selecting your preferred transparent format:

  1. Choose your output destination and name your file in the Output file field at the top.
  2. Ensure Full Project is selected under the render options if you want to export the entire timeline.
  3. Click the Render to File button at the bottom of the window.

Your exported video will now feature a fully transparent background.
