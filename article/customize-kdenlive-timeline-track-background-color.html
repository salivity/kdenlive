Customize Kdenlive Timeline Track Background Color

Customizing the background color of your timeline tracks in Kdenlive can significantly improve your editing workflow, reduce eye strain, and help you distinguish between different track types more easily. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to change these colors using Kdenlive’s built-in theme and configuration settings.

Step 1: Open the Kdenlive Settings

To begin customizing your interface colors, you need to access the main configuration menu:

  1. Open Kdenlive.
  2. Click on Settings in the top menu bar (on macOS, click Kdenlive in the menu bar).
  3. Select Configure Kdenlive... from the dropdown menu.

Step 2: Navigate to the Colors Tab

Once the configuration window opens, look at the sidebar on the left side of the window:

  1. Scroll down and click on the Colors tab.
  2. Here, you will see a list of customizable interface elements, including timeline components, audio waveforms, and track backgrounds.

Step 3: Change the Track Background Colors

In the Colors menu, you can customize the specific visual elements of your timeline:

  1. Find the entry labeled Timeline background or Track background in the list.
  2. Click on the color box next to the label to open the system color picker.
  3. Choose your desired color from the palette, or enter a specific HTML hex code for precise branding.
  4. Click OK to select the color.

Step 4: Apply and Save Your Changes

After selecting your preferred colors:

  1. Click the Apply button at the bottom right of the configuration window to preview the changes.
  2. If you are satisfied with the new look, click OK to save your settings and close the window.

Alternative: Change the Overall Color Scheme

If you prefer to change the entire interface theme rather than individual colors, Kdenlive offers pre-configured color schemes:

  1. Go to Settings in the top menu bar.
  2. Hover over Color Scheme.
  3. Select one of the available themes (such as Breeze Dark, Breeze Light, or High Contrast) to instantly update the entire interface, including the timeline track backgrounds.
