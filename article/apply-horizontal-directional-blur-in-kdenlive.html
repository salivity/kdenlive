Apply Horizontal Directional Blur in Kdenlive

Adding a directional blur to your video clips in Kdenlive is an effective way to simulate rapid horizontal movement, create seamless whip pans, or enhance high-speed action scenes. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to locate the Directional Blur effect, configure the horizontal angle, and use keyframes to animate the blur strength for realistic motion.

Step 1: Add Your Clip to the Timeline

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your video project files.
  2. Drag and drop the video or image clip you want to blur onto a video track in the timeline.
  3. If the blur is only needed for a specific moment (such as a fast transition or a sudden movement), use the Razor Tool (X) to cut and isolate that specific section of the clip.

Step 2: Apply the Directional Blur Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (typically located in the workspace panel next to the Project Bin).
  2. Type Directional Blur into the search bar.
  3. Drag the Directional Blur effect directly onto your target clip on the timeline.

Step 3: Set the Blur to Horizontal

  1. Select the clip on the timeline to reveal the effect settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Locate the Angle or Direction parameter.
  3. Set the angle to 0° (or 180°), which forces the blur to stretch exclusively along the horizontal X-axis.

Step 4: Adjust the Blur Length and Animate with Keyframes

To make the motion blur feel natural, it should dynamically increase during the fastest part of the movement and decrease when the motion stops.

  1. In the effect controls, find the Blur Length (or Amount) slider.
  2. Enable keyframing by clicking the clock or keyframe icon next to the parameter.
  3. Move the playhead to the beginning of the movement, create a keyframe, and set the blur amount to 0.
  4. Move the playhead to the middle of the movement (the peak speed), create a second keyframe, and increase the blur amount to your desired intensity (e.g., 20 to 50 pixels depending on the resolution).
  5. Move the playhead to where the movement finishes, add a final keyframe, and reduce the blur amount back to 0.

Step 5: Preview and Fine-Tune

  1. Play back the timeline to preview the motion effect in real time.
  2. Adjust the spacing of your keyframes or the blur intensity if the movement appears too abrupt or too subtle.
  3. Once satisfied with the horizontal motion effect, proceed with editing or export your project.
