Split Multi-Channel Audio to Mono in Kdenlive

Working with multi-channel audio in Kdenlive can be challenging when all channels are bundled into a single stereo or surround track. This guide explains how to configure Kdenlive to import, separate, and process multi-channel audio recordings—such as multi-mic setups or multi-track screen captures—as individual, editable mono streams directly on your timeline.

Method 1: Separate Channels via Clip Properties

The most efficient way to handle multi-channel audio without re-encoding your media is to change the channel mapping inside Kdenlive's Project Bin.

  1. Import your multi-channel media file into the Project Bin.
  2. Right-click the clip and select Clip Properties (or select the clip and open the Clip Properties panel).
  3. Navigate to the Audio tab at the top of the panel.
  4. Locate the Channels configuration section.
  5. Check the box labeled Separate channels (or select Split audio channels depending on your Kdenlive version).
  6. Click Apply.

When you drag the clip onto the timeline, Kdenlive will automatically distribute each mono channel onto separate, stacked audio tracks, allowing you to edit volume, effects, and panning for each track independently.

Method 2: Split Audio Tracks Directly on the Timeline

If a clip is already placed on the timeline as a combined track, you can split the streams in place:

  1. Right-click the audio clip (or the audio portion of a video clip) on the timeline.
  2. Navigate to Audio in the context menu.
  3. Select Split Audio and choose Split to Mono Tracks.
  4. Kdenlive will create new audio tracks below the original clip and assign each individual audio channel to its own track.

Method 3: Use the Audio Channel Mapper Effect

If you need dynamic control or only want to isolate specific channels without adding new tracks:

  1. Add your clip to the timeline.
  2. Open the Effects panel and search for Audio Channel Mapper (or Channel Copy).
  3. Drag the effect onto the audio clip.
  4. In the Effect Properties panel, route the desired source channel (e.g., Channel 1 for Mic 1) to the left and right outputs to convert that specific stream to a centered mono output.

Default Project Settings for Multi-Track Audio

To streamline future multi-track workflows:

  1. Go to Settings > Configure Kdenlive.
  2. Select the Timeline tab and locate the Audio options.
  3. Enable multi-track view options to ensure that dropped multi-stream clips automatically generate the required number of timeline tracks.
