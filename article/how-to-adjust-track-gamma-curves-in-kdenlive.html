How to Adjust Track Gamma Curves in Kdenlive

Adjusting the gamma curve of an entire video track in Kdenlive allows you to modify the midtone luminance and color balance across all clips on that track simultaneously. Rather than applying color corrections clip by clip, you can apply curve-based effects directly to the track header. This guide walks you through selecting a video track, adding the appropriate curve effect, and fine-tuning the gamma curve to achieve consistent color grading across your project.

Step 1: Select the Target Track Header

To apply an effect to an entire track instead of a single clip:

  1. Locate the timeline on the bottom section of your Kdenlive workspace.
  2. Identify the specific video track you want to adjust (for example, V1 or V2).
  3. Click the track header on the left side of the timeline to highlight it. A small icon or border will indicate that the track itself is active.

Step 2: Open the Effects Panel and Locate the Curve Tool

  1. Navigate to the Effects tab in the main interface panels. If it is hidden, enable it via View > Effects.
  2. In the search bar at the top of the Effects panel, type Curves or Color Curves.
  3. Locate the Bezier Curves, RGB Curves, or Color Curves effect depending on your installed version of MLT/Kdenlive.

Step 3: Apply the Effect to the Track

  1. Drag the curve effect directly onto the header of your chosen track, or double-click the effect while the track is selected.
  2. A small effect icon will appear in the track header, confirming that the effect is active at the track level.

Step 4: Adjust the Gamma Curve

  1. Open the Effect/Composition Stack panel (enable it via View > Effect Stack if not visible).
  2. With the track header selected, the curve effect settings will appear in the stack.
  3. Choose the channel you want to adjust (Luma/RGB for overall brightness, or individual Red, Green, and Blue channels for color tinting).
  4. Click on the center point of the diagonal curve line to create a midtone control point (Gamma).
  5. Drag the point upward to brighten the midtones without overexposing pure white highlights, or drag it downward to darken the midtones without crushing the absolute blacks.

Step 5: Verify Across the Timeline

Move the playhead across various clips on that track to ensure the gamma adjustment provides a consistent look across different lighting conditions and scene changes. If certain clips require unique adjustments, you can still apply additional clip-level effects without overriding the master track curve.
