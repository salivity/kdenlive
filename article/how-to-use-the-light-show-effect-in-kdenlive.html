How to Use the Light Show Effect in Kdenlive

This guide explains how to use Kdenlive's "Light Show" effect to create dynamic, music-synchronized visual patterns for your video projects. By applying this built-in audio visualization filter to a visual clip and linking it to your soundtrack, you can generate automated, beat-reactive lighting effects tailored to specific audio frequencies and rhythms.

1. Set Up Your Project and Media

  1. Open Kdenlive and import your audio track along with a background element (such as a video clip, image, or color clip).
  2. Drag your audio file onto an audio track (e.g., A1) in the timeline.
  3. Drag your visual clip or create a transparent/color clip on a video track (e.g., V1) directly above the audio, matching the duration of the music.

2. Apply the Light Show Effect

  1. Open the Effects tab (typically located in the project asset panel dock).
  2. Use the search bar to find the Light Show effect (located under the Audio Spectrum or Audio Visualization categories).
  3. Drag and drop the Light Show effect directly onto the visual clip on your video track.

3. Connect the Audio Source

  1. Select the clip with the effect to display its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Locate the audio source parameter within the effect controls.
  3. Set the audio track or clip reference to the specific timeline track containing your music (e.g., A1) so the effect can read the waveform data.

4. Adjust the Visual and Frequency Settings

  • Frequency Range: Adjust the minimum and maximum frequency sliders to choose which sounds trigger the visual. Lower frequencies (bass and kick drums) produce strong pulses, while higher frequencies react to hi-hats and vocals.
  • Sensitivity/Amplitude: Increase the gain or amplitude to make the light patterns more prominent, or lower it if the effect is clipping and overexposed.
  • Pattern and Colors: Configure the shape parameters, line thickness, radius, and color spectrum to customize how the light beams, rings, or flashes appear.
  • Blending Modes: Adjust the opacity or compositing blend mode (e.g., Screen or Add) to blend the generated light seamlessly with any underlying video footage.

5. Preview and Render

  1. Play back the timeline to ensure the patterns sync accurately with the beat.
  2. If playback lags, enable Timeline Preview or reduce the monitor preview resolution.
  3. Once satisfied with the timing and appearance, open the Render dialog (Ctrl + Enter) to export your synchronized music video.
