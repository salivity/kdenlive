Sort Assets by Date and Resolution in Kdenlive

Managing your media efficiently in Kdenlive requires customizing the Project Bin to match your editing workflow. This guide explains how to switch the Project Bin to detailed view mode, enable specific metadata columns, and sort your video clips, audio tracks, and images by file creation date or frame dimensions.

Step 1: Switch to Detailed View Mode

By default, the Project Bin may display your files as icons. To sort by specific metadata, you must switch to a list-based view:

  1. Locate the Project Bin panel in your Kdenlive workspace.
  2. Click the View Options icon (or right-click an empty area inside the bin).
  3. Select Tree View or Detailed View to display the assets with column headers.

Step 2: Enable Date and Dimension Columns

To sort by creation date or frame size, ensure these metadata columns are visible:

  1. Right-click on any existing column header (such as Name or Duration) at the top of the Project Bin.
  2. From the context menu, check Date to display the file creation/modification timestamp.
  3. Check Dimensions (or Resolution, depending on your Kdenlive version) to display the pixel width and height of the assets.

Step 3: Sort Assets by Creation Date

  1. Locate the Date column header in the Project Bin.
  2. Click the Date header once to sort your clips in ascending order (oldest to newest).
  3. Click the Date header a second time to reverse the order (newest to oldest).

Step 4: Sort Assets by Frame Dimension

  1. Locate the Dimensions column header.
  2. Click the Dimensions header to organize your assets from smallest to largest resolution (e.g., 720p to 4K).
  3. Click the header again to invert the sorting order from highest to lowest resolution.
