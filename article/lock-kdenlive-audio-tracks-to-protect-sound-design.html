Lock Kdenlive Audio Tracks to Protect Sound Design

Locking audio tracks in Kdenlive prevents accidental cuts, shifts, and deletions of finalized sound design layers while you continue editing visual elements. This guide details how to toggle timeline track locks, how locked tracks behave with major editing tools, and how to safeguard your mixed dialogue, Foley, and background music against desynchronization.

How to Lock an Audio Track

  1. Locate the Track Header: Navigate to the left-hand side of the Kdenlive timeline where the track headers (e.g., A1, A2, Master) are displayed.
  2. Click the Lock Icon: Click the Padlock icon on the specific audio track containing your finalized sound design.
  3. Verify the Locked State: When locked, the padlock icon will change to a closed position, and the clips within that track will display a hatched or dimmed overlay, indicating that editing operations are disabled for that layer.

Alternatively, right-click any empty area of the track header and select Lock Track from the context menu.

How Locked Tracks Behave During Editing

Once a track is locked, Kdenlive alters how standard editing operations interact with it:

  • Cut and Razor Tools: The Razor Tool (Shift + R or X) will not slice clips residing on locked tracks.
  • Spacer Tool: Using the Spacer Tool (M) to push or pull timeline clips will only move clips on unlocked tracks, leaving the locked audio in its exact timecode position.
  • Ripple Edits and Deletions: Performing an extract or ripple delete will remove space across unlocked tracks without shifting or truncating locked audio layers.
  • Direct Selection: Clips on locked tracks cannot be dragged, trimmed, grouped, or deleted until the track is unlocked.

Unlocking Tracks for Final Export Adjustments

To make further adjustments to your sound design, click the closed Padlock icon on the track header again. The track will return to its standard editable state, allowing you to fine-tune volume keyframes, apply audio effects, or adjust clip boundaries before final rendering.
