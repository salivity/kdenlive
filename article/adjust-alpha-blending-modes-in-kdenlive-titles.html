Adjust Alpha Blending Modes in Kdenlive Titles

Adjusting the alpha blending modes of overlapping title cards in Kdenlive allows you to control how text, shapes, and transparency interact between multiple layers. By placing title clips on separate tracks and applying composition effects or timeline transitions, you can achieve complex visual styles such as screen, multiply, overlay, and custom alpha operations. This guide walks you through the precise steps to configure these blend modes on your timeline.

Step 1: Place Title Cards on Separate Tracks

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive and locate your title clips in the Project Bin.
  2. Drag the base title card onto an upper video track (for example, V2).
  3. Drag the overlapping title card onto the track directly above it (for example, V3).
  4. Align the clips on the timeline so they overlap for the desired duration.

Step 2: Add a Composition or Transform Effect

To blend the upper title card with the lower one, you must apply a compositing tool:

  • Method A (Track Transition): Hover near the bottom edge of the top clip on the timeline until the composition pointer appears, then click to add a Composite and Transform composition between the two tracks.
  • Method B (Effects Stack): Select the upper title clip, open the Effects panel, search for Composite and Transform (or Transform), and drag it onto the clip.

Step 3: Change the Blend Mode

  1. Click on the applied composition or select the clip to open its settings in the Effect/Composition Stack panel.
  2. Locate the Composition or Blend Mode drop-down menu.
  3. Choose the appropriate blending mode based on your visual needs:
    • Screen / Add: Brightens the overlap, ideal for light text effects over darker elements.
    • Multiply: Darkens the overlap, effectively removing light areas.
    • Overlay / Soft Light: Combines multiply and screen effects to create higher contrast integration.
    • Destination In / Destination Out: Masks or cuts out the shape of the top title from the underlying title using the alpha channel.
  4. Adjust the Opacity slider to fine-tune the strength of the blend.

Step 4: Utilize Track Compositing (Alternative Method)

If you prefer global blending without individual clip transitions:

  1. Click the Track Header for the top video track (e.g., V3).
  2. Right-click the track header and ensure Track Compositing is set to High Quality or Composite and Transform.
  3. Apply blending directly through track-level effects if working with full-track title templates.
