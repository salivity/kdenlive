How to Set Low-Cut on Master Track in Kdenlive

Adjusting the low-cut frequency on the master output in Kdenlive removes unwanted low-end rumble, wind noise, and bass clutter across your entire project simultaneously. Applying a high-pass filter directly to the master audio bus ensures a clean, consistent sound mix without requiring adjustments on individual audio clips or separate tracks.

Step 1: Select the Master Audio Track

  1. Open your project in Kdenlive.
  2. Locate the track headers on the left side of the timeline.
  3. Click on the Master tab at the top of the track headers (or click the Master channel in the Audio Mixer window).
  4. Verify that the Effect/Composition Stack window now displays "Master" to confirm you are editing global project effects.

Step 2: Add the Filter

  1. Open the Effects panel (go to Menu > View > Effects if it is not visible).
  2. Click the Audio tab or filter icon within the Effects panel.
  3. In the search bar, type High Pass (a high-pass filter acts as a low-cut filter). Alternatively, search for Equalizer to use a multi-band parametric EQ.
  4. Double-click the High Pass filter or drag it directly into the Effect/Composition Stack panel for the Master track.

Step 3: Adjust the Low-Cut Frequency

  1. In the Effect/Composition Stack, locate the Frequency parameter of the High Pass effect.
  2. Drag the slider or type a specific value (in Hz) into the input box:
    • 80 Hz: Standard setting for general voice cleanup without thinning the voice.
    • 100 Hz – 120 Hz: Ideal for high levels of rumble, handling noise, or lighter vocal ranges.
  3. Play back your timeline to monitor the audio in real time.
  4. Fine-tune the frequency until unwanted sub-bass frequencies are eliminated while maintaining natural tone in your mix.
